# 0. Scope

`ZyUX-002` governs experience for organizations of any shape, organizational relationships, cross-organization participation, scoped Authority, delegation/re-delegation, bootstrap administration, external parties, restructuring, and current-vs-historical organizational context.

> **Organizations change. Relationships change. Authority changes. History must remain reconstructible.**

It does not redefine Authority, Standing, Delegation, Ownership, legal entity status, or Identity.

# 1. Core Model

```text
SUBJECT / ORGANIZATION
        ↓
RELATIONSHIP CONTEXT
        ↓
ROLE / CAPACITY (for comprehension)
        ↓
APPLICABLE AUTHORITY
        ↓
CURRENT STANDING
        ↓
SCOPE
        ↓
POLICY / DISCLOSURE
        ↓
CAPABILITY STATE
```

Relationship and role are inputs/context. Neither grants permission by itself.

# 2. Binding Laws

## ZyUX-002-S01 — Organization Is Not a Person's Account

An organization SHALL NOT be represented as permanently belonging to one human login. One human may administer it; the organization remains distinct.

## ZyUX-002-S02 — Authority Graph, Not Mandatory Org Tree

Unfict SHALL support organizational Authority/relationships as a graph capable of expressing sole trader, team, enterprise, regulator, agency, consortium, logistics, and other structures without requiring one permanent `Org → Department → Manager → Employee` tree.

## ZyUX-002-S03 — Relationship Is Context, Not Permission

A relationship MAY make a Subject relevant to a Job. It SHALL NOT by itself grant visibility, Authority, Standing, or capability.

## ZyUX-002-S04 — Role Does Not Equal Authority

Role/capacity labels help comprehension. They SHALL NOT self-execute permission.

## ZyUX-002-S05 — Ownership Does Not Equal Authority

Economic/property ownership SHALL NOT automatically create managerial, operational, signing, policy, administrative, or delegation Authority.

## ZyUX-002-S06 — Self-Execution Is Preserved

A Subject that directly holds applicable Authority MAY act without a synthetic self-delegation. Delegation SHALL NOT be manufactured merely to fit an organizational UI model.

## ZyUX-002-S07 — Delegation Conservation

A delegator SHALL NOT grant more Authority than it legitimately holds and is permitted to delegate.

## ZyUX-002-S08 — Scope Narrowing

Delegation may remain equal or become narrower downstream. It SHALL NOT silently widen.

## ZyUX-002-S09 — Re-Delegation Must Be Explicit

The ability to act SHALL NOT imply the ability to delegate that action further.

## ZyUX-002-S10 — Organizational Scope Is Authority / Policy Bounded

An organization governs only the organizational data, actions, sources, disclosures, delegations, and administration surfaces over which applicable Authority and Policy grant control.

## ZyUX-002-S11 — Current Access Is Temporal

A relationship may remain historically true after current Standing/capability ends.

## ZyUX-002-S12 — Organizational Mutation Does Not Rewrite History

Department merger, rename, restructure, acquisition, split, leadership change, or dissolution SHALL NOT rewrite historical attribution.

## ZyUX-002-S13 — Cross-Organization Relationship Does Not Merge Tenants/Scopes

Delegation or partnership between organizations SHALL NOT merge their data, users, governance, or unrelated scopes.

## ZyUX-002-S14 — External Party Is Not Automatically an Internal User

Contractors, auditors, regulators, agencies, logistics providers, suppliers, customers, and partners SHOULD be represented according to their real governed relationship rather than forced into fake employee membership.

## ZyUX-002-S15 — No Hidden Master Role

Labels such as Owner, Founder, Super Admin, or Administrator SHALL NOT silently bypass governed Authority.

## ZyUX-002-S16 — Bootstrap Authority Must Be Explicit and Replaceable

Where organization creation requires initial broad control, the underlying grant SHOULD be attributable, reviewable, transferable, and compatible with additional administrators. `creator = permanent god mode` is prohibited.

## ZyUX-002-S17 — Government / Regulator Authority Is External

A regulator may possess lawful oversight or decision Authority without becoming an internal company administrator.

# 3. Supported Organization Shapes

The doctrine must support at least:

```text
Sole Operator
Small Business
Multi-Branch Organization
Enterprise / Legal-Entity Group
Government / Regulator
Consortium / Network
Agency
Contractor Network
Logistics Relationship
Temporary Program / Project
```

No shape creates a new experience constitution.

# 4. Organization Creation

Creation SHOULD remain minimal. Legal/jurisdictional data should be requested only as necessary for current Jobs/assurance.

Creating the organizational context does not itself prove legal existence, Identity relationship, or representation Authority.

# 5. Delegation UX

The preferred human-facing delegation model is:

```text
Who?
What can they do?
For what scope?
For how long?
Can they delegate further?
```

Example:

```text
Give Sarah access
Scope: Alexandria Warehouse
Can: View shipments / Report exception / Approve release ≤ EGP 100,000
May re-delegate: No
Expires: 31 Dec 2026
```

Internal formalism may be deeper. UX owns simplification, not Authority law.

# 6. Scope Summary

A Subject SHOULD be able to understand, where disclosable:

```text
What can I do?
For what?
Where?
Until when?
Who/what established this Authority?
Can I pass it on?
```

# 7. Cross-Organization Delegation

Example:

```text
Manufacturer → Logistics Co → Driver Omar
```

Each hop remains governed, scoped, and attributable. Re-delegation must be authorized. Manufacturer and Logistics Co do not merge organizational scope.

# 8. Administrator Experience

Administration should be contextual and scopeable.

A branch administrator may manage branch access without seeing other branches or global policy.

Avoid binary `Admin / Not Admin` where the governed model is richer.

# 9. The Word “Owner”

`Owner` is ambiguous. UX SHOULD prefer context-specific labels such as Legal Owner, Organization Administrator, Billing Administrator, Policy Administrator, or Source Administrator where precision matters.

# 10. Current vs Historical Organization Context

Former relationships may move from active work to History.

```text
Company A
Former relationship
Period: 2024–2026
Current access: None
Historical actions: available only as permitted
```

Historical attribution does not create perpetual current visibility.

# 11. Inheritance / Succession Boundary

Ownership or economic rights may transfer under governing law. Delegated employment/office/managerial Authority does not automatically follow.

Heirs act as themselves under new lawful successor relationships, not as the deceased Subject.

# 12. Explicit Non-Scope

- inheritance law;

- final delegation schema;
- IAM protocols;

# 13. Drift Prohibitions

Do not drift into:

- organization = one user's account;
- employee = permanent user;
- relationship = permission;
- role = Authority;
- owner = super-admin;
- department = mandatory hierarchy;
- external partner = employee;
- regulator = company admin;
- inheritance = Authority transfer;
- delegated action = re-delegation right;
- organization merge = historical rewrite;
- access revocation = history deletion;
- partnership = data co-mingling;
- giant permission matrix as default UX.
