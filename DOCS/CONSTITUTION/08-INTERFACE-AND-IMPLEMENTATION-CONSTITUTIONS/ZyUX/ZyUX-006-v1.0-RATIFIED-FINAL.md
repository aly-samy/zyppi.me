# 0. Scope

`ZyUX-006` governs experience across time: relationship end, employment termination, credential loss/revocation, delegation expiry, organization restructure/merger/dissolution, administrator departure, host removal/replacement, ownership succession, recovery, death/life-state changes, and historical reconstruction.

> **The future may change completely without rewriting what was true, authorized, observed, decided, or executed in the past.**

## Identity Authority Boundary

ZyUX does **not** own Identity semantics.

Identity cardinality, Identity relationship topology, referent resolution, Representation lifecycle, consolidation/supersession, and Identity mutation are imported from the **currently operative constitutional Identity authority**.

`ZRM-ID-AMD-001 v0.9.1` may inform future alignment but, while it remains draft, this series SHALL NOT treat it as active law. Ratification of this series does not ratify that amendment.

Accordingly, ZyUX speaks normatively about **Subject continuity, authentication/access continuity, current relationships, current Authority/Standing, disclosure, and historical attribution** without inventing Identity lifecycle law.

# 2. Binding Laws

## ZyUX-006-S01 — Access Loss Does Not Delete the Subject

Revoking credentials, ending employment, ending a contract, or removing one relationship SHALL NOT by itself be presented as deletion/replacement of the underlying Subject.

Any Identity effects remain governed by the operative Identity authority.

## ZyUX-006-S02 — Future Capability May End Without Historical Rewrite

Ending Standing/Authority SHALL stop future capability while preserving historical validity/attribution of legitimately performed past actions as governed.

## ZyUX-006-S03 — Historical Attribution Does Not Guarantee Perpetual Visibility

A Subject may remain historically attributable without indefinite access to underlying organization data.

## ZyUX-006-S04 — Current State Must Not Rewrite Past State

Current role, organization, policy, ownership, Standing, source, or host SHALL NOT substitute for historical context that applied at the relevant time.

## ZyUX-006-S05 — Recovery Reconnects Access; It Does Not Manufacture a Subject

Recovery restores legitimate access/continuity where established. It SHALL NOT silently create a replacement Subject or stronger Authority.

## ZyUX-006-S06 — No Hidden Universal Recovery Master

Recovery SHALL NOT rely on an invisible universal Unfict super-admin capable of assuming arbitrary Subjects/organizations.

## ZyUX-006-S07 — Recovery Assurance Matches Consequence

Higher-risk recovery MAY require stronger Evidence, approval, delay, quorum, or equivalent assurance as governed.

## ZyUX-006-S08 — Offboarding Is Scope-Specific

Ending Company A SHALL NOT automatically end Company B, personal, freelance, or other unrelated contexts.

## ZyUX-006-S09 — Organization Change Is Temporal

Department, leadership, merger, acquisition, split, dissolution, or successor change affects current structure without rewriting historical organizational attribution.

## ZyUX-006-S10 — Ownership Transfer Does Not Equal Authority Transfer

Ownership/economic rights may transfer while employment, office, signing, policy, managerial, or delegated Authority does not automatically transfer.

## ZyUX-006-S11 — Death / Life-State Is Not Subject Erasure

A governed life-state transition may restrict current participation and trigger succession processes without erasing the Subject or historical attribution.

Identity relationship consequences remain external-owner semantics.

## ZyUX-006-S12 — Life-State Requires Governed Evidence

Consequential life-state changes SHALL NOT be established by casual assertion alone.

## ZyUX-006-S13 — Recovery Preserves Scope Isolation

Recovered access SHALL restore only legitimate scope, not every context known to Unfict.

## ZyUX-006-S14 — Historical Views Are Clearly Historical

Historical reconstruction SHALL be visually/semantically distinguishable from current actionable state.

## ZyUX-006-S15 — Host Removal Does Not Erase History

Disconnecting/replacing a host ends future integration capability as governed but does not rewrite retained historical attribution/Receipts.

## ZyUX-006-S16 — Lifecycle Changes Should Be Explainable

When capability disappears or changes unexpectedly, Unfict SHOULD provide an appropriate, scope-safe lifecycle reason.

# 4. Employment Termination — Canonical Pattern

```text
Company A SSO → revoked
Company A relationship → ended
Company A Standing/Authority → ends as governed
Company A current visibility → ends
```

Unaffected unless separately governed:

```text
Ahmed as Subject
personal authentication methods
Mimi relationship
Company B
Company X freelance relationship
historical Company A attribution
```

# 5. Mid-Session Revocation

If current access changes while a sensitive surface is open, the experience should close/refresh content and controls as required and explain safely:

```text
Your Company A access has ended.
This page is no longer available.
```

# 6. Historical Context

A future audit may show:

```text
Invoice #882
Approved by: Ahmed
Capacity: Company A — Accountant
Standing: valid at action time
Date: 12 Feb 2025
```

Current employment is irrelevant to historical attribution.

# 7. Organization Restructure / Merger / Dissolution

Current structure may change. Historical actors remain historical actors.

A successor organization may gain new rights/obligations under governing law without becoming the historical author of predecessor actions.

# 8. Recovery

Recovery should be calm, bounded, non-enumerating, and proportional to consequence.

Potential patterns include another verified credential, enterprise IdP recovery, administrator quorum, legal organization Evidence, time delay, or support-assisted governed review.

Support SHALL facilitate process, not impersonate arbitrary Subjects.

# 9. Credential Reassignment

Old email/mobile possession SHALL NOT automatically prove continuity because identifiers may be recycled or compromised.

# 10. Death / Succession

Heirs or estate representatives log in as themselves.

Where law/policy establishes successor relationships, UX may express:

```text
Omar
acting as Estate Representative for Ahmed
```

not:

```text
Omar becomes Ahmed
```

Shares/property/custody may enter succession. Employment/CEO/delegated Authority does not automatically transfer.

# 11. Historical Proof Degradation

Evidence may later be unavailable, corrected, revoked, redacted, or legally removed while a Receipt remains.

The UX should explain degraded proof honestly rather than pretending the original Evidence is still fully available.

# 12. Current vs Historical Search

Authorized search MAY include historical actors, dissolved departments, former relationships, and old roles. Results SHALL clearly label historical/current status and obey disclosure rules.

# 13. Time Semantics

Where material, distinguish effective time from recorded time and scheduled changes from already-effective changes.

Backdated changes and disputed historical changes should be visibly exceptional.

# 14. Privacy / Retention Boundary

ZyUX does not decide erasure, legal hold, retention, pseudonymization, probate, or jurisdictional law.

The UX SHALL avoid false promises such as `delete account = erase every historical record` unless the governing authority actually makes that true.

# 15. Explicit Non-Scope

This document does not define legal inheritance/probate/employment law, retention schedules, cryptographic recovery, backup restoration, final Receipt retention, final life-state ontology, or court admissibility.

# 16. Drift Prohibitions

Do not drift into:

- delete account = delete person;
- fire employee = delete history;
- current role = historical role;
- current policy = historical policy;
- current org chart = historical org chart;
- heir = deceased Subject;
- inherited ownership = inherited office Authority;
- SSO revocation = global account deletion;
- host removal = history deletion;
- recovery = new identity;
- support agent = universal master;
- recycled email = same person;
- expired delegation = erased delegation;
- anonymous public user = account lifecycle.
