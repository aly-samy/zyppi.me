# 0. Amendment Purpose

This Amendment repairs a constitutional-generation conflict in the meaning of **Identity**.

The frozen Identity Mathematics currently contains:

```text
TH-003
Every constitutional constituent possesses exactly one immutable identity.

TH-030
Identity is indivisible and cannot be partitioned,
merged, or decomposed into multiple identities.
```

Those theorems were ratified under an older model in which Identity was treated as an assigned constitutional object.

Higher ratified ZRM authority establishes a different meaning:

```text
Identity is not a Primitive.

Identity is a relationship
between Reality / an entity
and a Representation.

Reality exists independently of Identity.

Identity points to Reality.
```

This Amendment SHALL reconcile that conflict without:

- rewriting historical theorem text;
- renaming old theorems after the fact;
- inventing replacement mathematics;
- defining zID;
- importing Trust, Authority, Evidence, or execution semantics into Identity.

# 8. AM-ID-001 — Identity Relationship Topology

An Identity relationship SHALL connect:


# 10. AM-ID-003 — Constituent Participation Cardinality

A Reality constituent MAY participate in:

```text
0..N
```

Identity relationship instances.

This cardinality applies **only** to a Reality constituent's participation in Representation-linked Identity relationships.

It SHALL NOT be interpreted as:

```text
0..N intrinsic identities
0..N Reality selves
0..N uniqueness objects
```

The `0` case preserves Reality that currently has no Representation.

The `N` case permits one constituent to be represented through multiple representations.

---

# 11. AM-ID-004 — No Universal Inverse Cardinality

This Amendment SHALL NOT establish the universal rule:

```text
one Representation
→ exactly one Reality constituent globally
```

ZRM-000 expressly permits Representations to be:

- uncertain;
- conflicting;
- false;
- incomplete.

A Representation or identical representation content may also occur under different namespaces, scopes, issuers, times, or contexts.

Therefore:

> **Representation uniqueness and referent resolution SHALL NOT be inferred merely from a raw Representation value.**

This Amendment establishes relationship-instance endpoint integrity, not universal identifier uniqueness.

Domain or namespace constitutions MAY impose stricter uniqueness where constitutionally authorized.

Nothing in this Amendment establishes a global count of Identity relationship instances in which a Representation may participate.

A Representation MAY appear in more than one claimed, contextual, temporal, jurisdictional, or namespace-scoped Identity relationship instance where the governing semantic model permits such claims.

Whether concurrent referential claims are valid, conflicting, historical, superseded, or mutually exclusive is a semantic-resolution question, not an Identity-structure question.

# 14. AM-ID-007 — Continuity Under Representational Change

- destroy a Reality constituent;
- merge distinct Reality constituents;
- split one Reality constituent into multiple Reality constituents;
- alter the primitive nature of a Reality constituent.
