# 1. Purpose of This Declaration

This declaration records and governs the succession of the **Zyppi** master brand by **Unfict**.

It exists to make the transition explicit, attributable, controlled, and historically coherent.

The change of master brand SHALL NOT create uncertainty about:

- whether Zyppi and Unfict are the same continuing project;
- whether prior constitutional documents remain valid;
- whether prior technical work remains authoritative;
- whether intellectual property created under Zyppi remains continuous;
- whether historical records should be rewritten;
- whether repositories, APIs, protocols, packages, identifiers, domains, or legal instruments change automatically;
- whether the brand change creates a new company or new platform;
- how public and institutional audiences should understand the transition;
- what migration work is required;
- what migration work is prohibited without separate authorization.

This declaration is the formal bridge between the **Zyppi era** and the **Unfict era**.

# 3. Constitutional Continuity

## 3.1 One Continuous System

There are not two constitutional platforms.

There is one continuous system:

> **The system developed under the Zyppi name continues under the Unfict name.**

All ratified, valid, or otherwise authoritative Zyppi-era constitutional constructs remain valid according to their existing status unless they are separately amended, superseded, deprecated, or revoked through their proper governing process.

The brand succession alone SHALL NOT:

- invalidate a ratified document;
- reopen a closed mandate;
- reset a constitutional version;
- alter an invariant;
- modify a semantic contract;
- change a governance decision;
- change the meaning of Reality, Evidence, Trust, Intent, Capability, Action, Proof, Receipt, or any other canonical construct;
- create a new execution stage;
- change a technical contract;
- change ownership or authorship;
- erase provenance.

## 3.2 Legacy Name as Historical Referent

In Zyppi-era documents, the term `Zyppi` remains historically correct.

From the effective date of this declaration, where context permits, `Zyppi` in an existing constitutional or technical document SHALL be understood as referring to the same continuous platform now operating under the master brand **Unfict**, unless the passage is specifically discussing the historical brand itself.

This continuity rule SHALL NOT be used to alter quoted historical language.

## 3.3 No Silent Constitutional Rewrite

Existing ratified or historical documents SHALL NOT be mass-edited merely to replace `Zyppi` with `Unfict`.

Doing so could:

- falsify historical provenance;
- break citations;
- disturb hashes;
- invalidate receipts;
- obscure chronology;
- create ambiguity about which version was actually ratified.

A future document version may replace legacy brand language where appropriate, but only through an explicit amendment, revision, or supersession process.

# 7. Documents and Corpus Handling

## 7.1 New Documents

Documents created after the effective date SHOULD use **Unfict** as the active master brand.

Where constitutional continuity matters, metadata MAY state:

> **Platform lineage: Zyppi → Unfict**

New documents SHALL NOT describe Unfict as a separate successor technology unless a real substantive fork or replacement has occurred.

## 7.2 Existing Ratified Documents

Existing ratified documents SHALL remain unchanged until their own authorized revision.

Their titles remain canonical historical identifiers.

Examples include:

- `NORTH STAR v7.0 — Zyppi — Reality Sync`;
- `What is Zyppi? v4.0 — Reality Sync`;
- Zyppi constitutional series;
- governance receipts;
- prior mandates;
- closure documents;
- architectural records.

A future revision MAY adopt Unfict naming while preserving version history and supersession lineage.

## 7.3 Existing Drafts

Active drafts MAY be migrated to Unfict before ratification where doing so does not obscure their provenance.

The migration SHOULD be documented in the draft's change history.

## 7.4 Citations

When citing a historical Zyppi document, use its actual historical title.

Do not silently modernize the citation.

Correct:

> `NORTH STAR v7.0 — Zyppi — Reality Sync`

Incorrect:

> `NORTH STAR v7.0 — Unfict — Reality Sync`

unless a separately ratified Unfict edition actually exists.

# 8. Technical Continuity and Rename Boundary

## 8.1 Brand Change Does Not Equal Technical Rename

The following SHALL NOT be automatically renamed merely because the master brand changed:

- Git repositories;
- package names;
- source directories;
- TypeScript namespaces;
- API paths;
- database schemas;
- environment variables;
- secrets;
- cloud resources;
- deployment identifiers;
- package scopes;
- container names;
- network identifiers;
- protocol identifiers;
- migration identifiers;
- test fixtures;
- CI/CD variables;
- historical commit messages;
- issue references;
- receipts;
- canonical IDs.

## 8.2 Separate Authorization Required

A technical rename MAY proceed only where there is a clear benefit and an authorized migration plan.

The migration plan SHALL consider:

- backwards compatibility;
- redirects;
- aliases;
- package deprecation;
- API stability;
- import paths;
- client breakage;
- external integrations;
- documentation;
- deployment dependencies;
- secrets and environment configuration;
- analytics continuity;
- release history;
- security implications.

## 8.3 Semantic Invariance

No technical migration may change business, epistemic, Trust, authorization, policy, error, or execution semantics merely to match the new brand.

Brand migration is presentation and identity work unless a separate technical mandate expressly authorizes more.

Historical accuracy is a requirement, not unfinished work.

# 25. Interpretation Rule

If a question arises during migration, apply this sequence:

1. **Is the item historical?**  
   Preserve it unless an authorized revision is needed.

2. **Is the item public and brand-facing?**  
   Migrate it to Unfict.

3. **Is the item a technical identifier?**  
   Do not rename automatically.

4. **Could changing it break compatibility, citations, provenance, legal meaning, or semantics?**  
   Require a specific migration decision.

5. **Could leaving Zyppi create current-brand confusion?**  
   Add an explicit Unfict succession notice or migrate the active surface.

6. **Is the issue legal, corporate, trademark, tax, or ownership-related?**  
   Do not infer consequences from the brand change; handle it through the appropriate legal process.
