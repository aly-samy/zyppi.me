**Program:** CAW-011 — Commerce Atlas Wedge **Milestone:** M08 — Runtime Verification Pipeline **Task:** IT-0804 — Policy evaluation integration **Mandate:** AMS-0804 **Document Role:** Implementation Authorization Mandate **Status:** AUTHORIZED FOR IMPLEMENTATION **Authority:** Zyppi Constitutional Council **Governing Plan:** M08-PLAN v1.4 — FINAL **Implementation Boundary:** Runtime policy evaluation only **Implementation Agent:** Authorized engineering agent designated by the Council **Implementation Authority:** THIS DOCUMENT

# 1. Mandate Purpose

AMS-0804 authorizes implementation of the **Active Execution / Policy Evaluation** portion of the existing M04 nine-stage Runtime pipeline for CAW-011.

The implementation SHALL integrate the Council-authorized GS1 Commerce Atlas Wedge policy semantics into the existing Runtime pipeline.

The implementation SHALL:

- preserve the existing M04 Runtime architecture;

- operate exclusively on explicit inputs;

- remain deterministic, synchronous, pure, and zero-I/O;

- evaluate only the policy universe authorized by the Active Constitutional View;

- implement the Council-authorized policy evaluation semantics;

- enforce deterministic resolution-budget semantics;

- produce the authorized policy evaluation result required by the M08 execution surface;

- preserve the separation between Policy authorization and Trust;

- produce bounded constitutional diagnostics where required;

- remain compatible with downstream ExecutionOutput and receipt materialization;

- provide complete verification evidence demonstrating conformity.

This mandate SHALL NOT create a new Runtime architecture or general-purpose policy platform.

# 5. Constitutional Implementation Closure Rule

AMS-0804 SHALL be executed under the **Constitutional Implementation Closure Rule (CICR)**.

CICR is operationalized through six layers.

## Layer 1 — Authority Closure

Every semantic behavior implemented by AMS-0804 SHALL be traceable to ratified governing authority.

The implementation agent SHALL NOT invent:

- policy types;

- policy operators;

- policy grammar;

- comparison semantics;

- precedence;

- temporal semantics;

- outcome mappings;

- diagnostic meanings;

- budget meanings;

- policy-version semantics.

The Council-authorized policy semantics are the source of truth.

## Layer 2 — Contract Closure

Every Runtime contract consumed or produced by AMS-0804 SHALL be an existing source-of-truth contract or a contract whose creation/materialization is explicitly authorized by this mandate from already-ratified semantics.

Relevant contracts include, as applicable:

- `ExecutionRequest`;

- `ExecutionContext`;

- `ActiveConstitutionalView`;

- `PolicyRecord`;

- `PolicyContext`;

- `EvaluatorResult`;

- `PolicyDecision`;

- `ExecutionOutput`;

- `Outcome`;

- `TrustResult`;

- `PipelineResult`;

- `PipelineError`.

M08 identifies these as part of the execution contract surface.

No duplicate constitutional representation SHALL be introduced.

## Layer 3 — Repository Evidence Closure

The repository SHALL contain the physical artifacts required to execute the authorized semantics.

Where an authorized artifact is absent, its absence SHALL NOT be interpreted as permission to invent it.

Instead, AMS-0804 provides the Layer 6 materialization mechanism defined below.

## Layer 4 — Consistency Closure

Consistency SHALL be verified across:

### 4A — Semantic Consistency

Ratified constitutional sources SHALL not contradict one another.

### 4B — Representational Consistency

Physical contracts SHALL accurately represent their authorized semantic meaning.

### 4C — Repository Consistency

Seed data, policy definitions, schemas, and implementation artifacts SHALL correspond to their authorized governing sources.

## Layer 5 — Implementation Closure

Implementation is authorized only for behavior whose semantic and representational meaning is closed.

The agent SHALL implement the authorized mechanism and SHALL NOT expand its scope.

## Layer 6 — Authorized Materialization

Where the Council-authorized semantics are closed but their physical repository representation is absent, AMS-0804 authorizes **strict materialization** of the missing representation.

Materialization SHALL be limited to:

- serialization;

- schema representation;

- type representation;

- fixture construction;

- seed construction;

- deterministic mapping from authoritative semantics into repository form.

Materialization SHALL NOT include:

- semantic invention;

- policy design;

- operator selection;

- meaning expansion;

- policy-language design;

- alternate interpretation;

- discretionary contract redesign.

If materialization requires a semantic choice not already resolved by governing authority, the agent SHALL STOP.

# 6. Provenance Lock

Every artifact created through Layer 6 materialization SHALL carry or be accompanied by deterministic provenance identifying its governing authority.

The provenance SHALL establish, at minimum:

- governing artifact identity;

- governing decision/gate identity where applicable;

- source semantic version or revision where available;

- materialization authority;

- materialization scope.

Repository presence SHALL NOT itself create authority.

An artifact lacking required provenance SHALL be treated as **unverified repository evidence** and SHALL NOT be treated as a new semantic source of truth.

A modification that changes semantic representation SHALL require renewed authorized materialization.

A purely mechanical formatting correction MAY be performed only where it demonstrably preserves semantic identity.

# 8. Policy Universe

The policy universe SHALL be determined exclusively by:
`ActiveConstitutionalView.applicablePolicies `
The Runtime SHALL NOT:

- discover policies;

- dynamically select policies;

- add policies;

- remove policies;

- retrieve policies;

- resolve missing policy dependencies through infrastructure.

`PolicyContext` SHALL provide evaluation context/evidence.

`PolicyContext` SHALL NOT determine which policies govern the execution.

# 9. Policy Evaluation Algebra

Each evaluated policy SHALL produce exactly one of:
`ALLOW DENY INDETERMINATE `
No fourth policy-evaluation state SHALL be introduced.

The aggregate policy result SHALL be conjunctive:
`DENY   > INDETERMINATE   > ALLOW `
The implementation SHALL perform complete evaluation where required by the governing policy semantics.

The implementation SHALL NOT short-circuit merely because a `DENY` has already been observed if complete evaluation is constitutionally required.

# 10. Deterministic Policy Traversal

The policy graph SHALL be evaluated deterministically.

Traversal SHALL follow the authorized ordering:

1. topological ordering of the policy dependency graph;

2. Policy ID deterministic tie-breaking where multiple eligible nodes are otherwise equivalent.

Equivalent constitutional inputs SHALL produce equivalent:

- traversal order;

- individual policy decisions;

- aggregate policy result;

- budget consumption;

- diagnostics.

The implementation SHALL NOT rely on:

- object insertion order as an implicit constitutional rule;

- database retrieval order;

- network arrival order;

- process scheduling;

- host timing;

- randomized iteration.

# 11. Structural Failure Boundary

Structural defects SHALL remain distinct from policy indeterminacy.

The implementation SHALL NOT convert structural defects into `INDETERMINATE` merely to keep execution moving.

Examples of structural/admission failures include:

- malformed policy representation;

- invalid policy graph;

- unresolved dependency;

- cyclic dependency;

- incomplete required graph;

- invalid contract representation.

These SHALL terminate or reject execution according to the governing Runtime failure semantics.

`INDETERMINATE` is reserved for authorized evaluation uncertainty such as unavailable required evaluation context or unsupported authorized policy semantics where the governing policy rules prescribe that result.

# 12. Missing Context and Unsupported Semantics

Where the governing policy semantics require information that is unavailable, the evaluator SHALL NOT guess.

The evaluator SHALL produce the Council-authorized uncertainty result.

In particular, the implementation SHALL NOT:

- substitute defaults;

- approximate missing facts;

- infer missing temporal facts;

- coerce unavailable values into usable values;

- use ambient host state;

- manufacture policy context.

Temporal evaluation SHALL use only the explicit constitutional temporal coordinate supplied through authorized execution input.

The Runtime SHALL NOT call:
`Date.now() new Date() `
for constitutional evaluation.

# 18. Execution Budget

AMS-0804 SHALL enforce the Council-approved resolution-step budget.

A budget SHALL NOT represent:

- CPU time;

- wall-clock time;

- host memory;

- machine performance;

- hardware effort;

- arbitrary computational cost.

The authorized policy-resolution model SHALL use:
`1 policy-node evaluation = 1 resolution step = 1 budget unit `
where this represents the constitutionally defined policy evaluation event.

Internal predicates within a single policy node SHALL NOT independently consume policy-node resolution steps unless separately authorized.

# 19. Budget Enforcement

Budget SHALL be:

- explicit;

- deterministic;

- checked before each budget-consuming step;

- consumed exactly once for each admitted policy-node evaluation;

- never silently replenished;

- never silently borrowed;

- never silently reset.

A deterministically evaluated/rejected policy-node evaluation that has been admitted as a resolution step SHALL consume its designated unit.

Equivalent constitutional executions SHALL consume equivalent budget.

# 20. Budget Exhaustion

Budget exhaustion is a constitutional execution outcome, not an infrastructure crash.

When the next required policy-node evaluation cannot be admitted because the budget is exhausted:

1. no unbudgeted evaluation SHALL begin;

2. previously completed policy evaluations SHALL remain valid;

3. traversal SHALL terminate deterministically;

4. the aggregate policy state SHALL become `INDETERMINATE` where required by the governing policy semantics;

5. the authorized diagnostic SHALL identify the exhaustion condition;

6. the result SHALL remain bounded and replayable.

No silent budget extension is permitted.

# 21. Policy and Trust Separation

AMS-0804 SHALL NOT equate:
`Policy ALLOW `
with:
`TRUSTED `
Policy evaluation determines policy authorization.

Trust is an independent constitutional result.

The evaluator SHALL NOT manufacture or upgrade `TrustResult` merely because policies evaluate to `ALLOW`.

The fixed TrustResult vocabulary remains:
`definite probable possible uncertain speculative `
The policy evaluator SHALL provide the authorized policy result required for downstream TrustResult derivation without collapsing the two domains.
