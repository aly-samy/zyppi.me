# 1. Question Presented

The governing question for G-0817 is:

**What bounded constitutional diagnostic information shall the Runtime preserve when an execution is constrained, interrupted, degraded, or otherwise reaches a constitutionally significant non-standard condition?**

G-0817 determines the semantic meaning and bounded representation of constitutional diagnostics.

It does not determine:

- policy evaluation semantics;

- policy-version semantics;

- execution-budget semantics;

- TrustResult semantics;

- operational logging or telemetry;

- cryptographic hash construction;

- receipt persistence;

- implementation-specific error handling;

- concrete TypeScript schema.

Those matters remain governed by their respective authorities.

# 2. Authority and Evidence Basis

This decision is governed by, and shall not weaken:

1. Ratified Zyppi constitutional authorities;

2. applicable CEngS requirements;

3. `RI-006`;

4. `POL-001`;

5. `SEC-001`;

6. `CAW-007 — Runtime Contracts`;

7. `CAW-011 — Commerce Atlas Wedge Build Order`;

8. M08 preparation and gate records;

9. ratified predecessor decisions, particularly G-0807, G-0813, G-0815, and G-0816.

G-0813 explicitly establishes that budget exhaustion must be diagnostically distinguishable from unrelated Runtime failure and that the final diagnostic representation belongs to G-0817.

G-0813 further establishes that budget exhaustion is a constitutional outcome requiring explicit representation and that the resulting execution must remain auditable.

G-0816 establishes the complementary boundary that `decisionSummary` answers **what policy result the Runtime produced**, while G-0817 answers **what bounded diagnostic information is necessary to explain the Runtime's execution condition**.

# 3. Constitutional Finding

The Council finds that neither a complete execution trace nor a cryptographic diagnostic digest is an appropriate constitutional representation for G-0817.

A full evaluation trace would allow evidence volume to grow with implementation and execution complexity.

A cryptographic digest would prematurely determine cryptographic construction and would improperly move G-0809 concerns into the diagnostic-semantic gate.

The Council therefore establishes:

**Constitutional diagnostics are bounded, deterministic semantic information describing constitutionally significant execution conditions.**

They are not:

- operational logs;

- debugging traces;

- complete policy traces;

- infrastructure telemetry;

- cryptographic digests;

- substitutes for `Outcome`;

- substitutes for `decisionSummary`;

- substitutes for `TrustResult`;

- a general Runtime error registry.

# 4. Selected Architectural Direction

## D-0817-01 — Bounded Constitutional Diagnostics

**DECISION: SELECTED AND RATIFIED**

M08 shall use:

**Direction B — Bounded Constitutional Diagnostics.**

Constitutional diagnostics shall preserve only the bounded information necessary to establish a constitutionally significant execution condition.

The representation shall be:

- deterministic;

- bounded;

- semantically meaningful;

- attributable where necessary;

- independent of operational infrastructure;

- replay-stable;

- compatible with subsequent canonical serialization and hashing.

The Council does **not** prescribe a concrete TypeScript interface in this gate.

# 5. Rejected Direction A — Full Evaluation Trace

## D-0817-02

**DECISION: REJECTED**

The Council rejects the retention of the full Runtime evaluation trace as constitutional diagnostic evidence.

A complete trace may contain:

- intermediate evaluation states;

- implementation-specific execution details;

- internal evaluator information;

- operational events;

- infrastructure observations;

- repeated or redundant information.

Such material is not inherently constitutional evidence.

Retaining it as part of the constitutional diagnostic surface would allow evidence size to grow with execution complexity and would unnecessarily couple constitutional semantics to implementation details.

Operational tracing remains outside G-0817.

# 6. Rejected Direction C — Summary + Digest

## D-0817-03

**DECISION: REJECTED FOR THIS GATE**

The Council does not establish a diagnostic digest as part of G-0817.

Cryptographic construction belongs to:

**G-0809 — Hash Domains.**

G-0817 therefore does not define:

- hash algorithms;

- digest algorithms;

- hash-domain labels;

- hash inclusion rules;

- canonical hash construction;

- trace-digest construction;

- external trace commitments.

G-0817 defines only the semantic diagnostic material that may subsequently be subject to cryptographic treatment if G-0809 determines that such treatment is required.

# 7. Constitutional Diagnostic Definition

## D-0817-04 — Diagnostic Meaning

A constitutional diagnostic is:

**bounded deterministic information describing a constitutionally significant condition that materially constrained, interrupted, degraded, or terminated Runtime execution, where that information is necessary to preserve constitutional interpretability or auditability.**

A condition shall not become a constitutional diagnostic merely because it is useful to an operator.

Constitutional relevance, not operational usefulness, is the governing criterion.

# 8. Diagnostic Scope

## D-0817-05 — Material Constitutional Conditions

G-0817 may represent conditions including, where constitutionally applicable:

- deterministic execution-budget exhaustion;

- a constitutionally significant execution-stage condition;

- a structural condition that materially prevented or constrained constitutional evaluation;

- a bounded condition necessary to explain why a normal constitutional execution result was not produced;

- other constitutionally governed conditions established by an applicable upstream or neighboring semantic authority.

The diagnostic representation shall not independently invent new policy, trust, budget, or outcome semantics.

The diagnostic records the condition established by the applicable authority.

# 9. Budget Exhaustion

## D-0817-06 — Budget Diagnostic

Budget exhaustion is a first-class constitutional execution condition.

G-0813 requires that budget exhaustion be diagnostically distinguishable from unrelated Runtime failure.

Accordingly, G-0817 shall provide a bounded diagnostic representation capable of establishing, where applicable:

1. that budget exhaustion occurred;

2. the authorized budget context necessary for interpretation;

3. the remaining budget at exhaustion;

4. the resolution boundary at which the next step could not be admitted;

5. sufficient causal provenance to establish why execution reached that boundary.

G-0817 does not redefine the semantics of budget consumption or resolution steps.

Those semantics remain governed by G-0813 and the applicable policy/runtime authorities.

# 10. Relationship to Policy Evaluation

## D-0817-07 — Policy Diagnostic Boundary

G-0817 shall not duplicate the policy evaluation model established by G-0807.

G-0807 owns:

- policy evaluation;

- individual policy decisions;

- policy aggregation;

- deterministic policy traversal.

G-0817 may provide bounded diagnostic information where necessary to explain a constitutionally significant condition affecting policy evaluation.

It shall not reproduce the complete `PolicyDecision[]`.

An incomplete policy evaluation shall not be transformed by G-0817 into a new policy result.

The semantic policy result remains governed by G-0807 and represented by G-0816 where applicable.

G-0807 already establishes that budget exhaustion must not silently continue and that incomplete evaluation must not produce aggregate `ALLOW`.

# 11. Relationship to TrustResult

## D-0817-08 — Trust Diagnostic Boundary

G-0817 shall not redefine `TrustResult`.

Where a constitutionally significant condition affects Trust evaluation, G-0817 may preserve bounded diagnostic information necessary to explain that condition.

The diagnostic shall not become a second TrustResult taxonomy.

G-0806 remains authoritative for Trust semantics.

# 12. Relationship to `decisionSummary`

## D-0817-09 — Decision Separation

G-0816 answers:

**"What policy result did the Runtime produce?"**

G-0817 answers:

**"What bounded diagnostic information is necessary to explain the Runtime's execution condition?"**

Therefore:

`Policy evaluation result` → `G-0816 decisionSummary`

and, independently where required:

`Constitutionally significant execution condition` → `G-0817 diagnostic`

G-0817 shall not absorb `decisionSummary`.

G-0816 already expressly excludes detailed diagnostics from the canonical decision summary.

# 13. Relationship to Runtime Outcome and Errors

## D-0817-10 — Diagnostic Is Not Outcome

A diagnostic does not independently determine the Runtime `Outcome`.

The applicable Runtime and execution contracts remain authoritative for determining whether an execution:

- succeeds;

- is denied;

- is indeterminate;

- is rejected;

- or otherwise terminates.

G-0817 supplies bounded explanatory information where constitutionally necessary.

It shall not become an alternative outcome vocabulary or a replacement for an applicable Runtime error contract.

A structural Runtime failure shall not be retroactively represented as an ordinary policy decision.

This preserves the distinction already established by G-0816 between policy results and Runtime structural failure.

# 14. Determinism

## D-0817-11 — Deterministic Diagnostics

For identical:

- explicit execution inputs;

- authorized constitutional context;

- governing policy state;

- governing Runtime semantics;

- relevant constitutional corpus;

the resulting constitutional diagnostic representation shall be identical.

Diagnostics shall not depend upon:

- wall-clock time;

- machine identity;

- process identity;

- thread scheduling;

- filesystem state;

- network state;

- locale;

- ambient randomness;

- memory addresses;

- implementation-specific execution timing.

This follows the M08 deterministic-execution principle already established across the preceding gates.

# 15. Replay

## D-0817-12 — Replay Stability

Constitutional diagnostics are part of the semantic execution evidence.

Where an identical execution is replayed under identical authorized conditions, constitutionally significant diagnostic conditions shall reproduce equivalently.

A replay shall not create a different diagnostic merely because of:

- infrastructure differences;

- execution timing;

- implementation optimization;

- operational logging differences;

- machine identity.

The diagnostic is therefore a semantic consequence of the constitutional execution, not of its operational environment.

# 16. Boundedness

## D-0817-13 — Bounded Constitutional Evidence

Constitutional diagnostics shall have bounded representation.

The diagnostic representation shall not become an unrestricted collection of:

- execution events;

- policy evaluations;

- stack traces;

- intermediate values;

- telemetry;

- debugging messages;

- implementation traces.

The Council deliberately does **not** establish an arbitrary numerical threshold in G-0817.

Concrete implementation limits, where required, shall be established by the appropriate implementation governance without changing the semantic requirement of boundedness.

This is consistent with G-0816, which likewise rejects arbitrary numerical thresholds at the semantic gate.

# 17. Deterministic Ordering

## D-0817-14 — Diagnostic Ordering

Where more than one constitutional diagnostic element is present, its semantic representation shall have deterministic ordering.

The ordering shall not depend upon:

- incidental execution scheduling;

- infrastructure behavior;

- logging order;

- nondeterministic collection traversal.

G-0817 establishes the semantic requirement for deterministic ordering.

The precise serialization and canonical ordering mechanism required for cryptographic participation remains delegated to G-0809.

# 18. Operational Telemetry Exclusion

## D-0817-15 — Operational Information Excluded

The following are not constitutional diagnostics merely because they may assist debugging:

- wall-clock timestamps;

- process identifiers;

- thread identifiers;

- machine identifiers;

- memory addresses;

- stack traces;

- raw exception text;

- debugging logs;

- evaluator internals;

- infrastructure events;

- performance measurements;

- network observations;

- implementation-specific trace output.

Such information belongs to operational telemetry unless another constitutional authority explicitly establishes otherwise.

G-0817 shall not convert operational telemetry into constitutional evidence merely by attaching it to an execution.

# 19. No Diagnostic Taxonomy Expansion

## D-0817-16 — Semantic Scope Discipline

G-0817 establishes the semantic category of constitutional diagnostics.

It does **not** attempt to create a universal catalogue of every possible Runtime failure or policy/trust condition.

The Council therefore does not establish an exhaustive diagnostic code registry through this gate.

Where a diagnostic code or classification is required, its semantic authority shall remain with the governing domain or contract responsible for the underlying condition.

G-0817 provides the diagnostic boundary and representation principles through which such constitutionally authorized information may be surfaced.

This prevents G-0817 from becoming a second general-purpose Runtime error taxonomy.

# 20. No Trace Digest

## D-0817-17 — Cryptographic Separation

The Runtime shall not be understood, by virtue of G-0817, as generating or committing to cryptographic digests of operational traces.

G-0817 does not require the Runtime to:

- hash operational logs;

- hash stack traces;

- hash debugging output;

- hash infrastructure telemetry;

- reference an external trace store;

- construct a trace commitment.

If a future cryptographic construction requires diagnostic material, the inclusion and construction rules belong to G-0809.

No such cryptographic requirement is established by G-0817.

# 21. Persistence Boundary

## D-0817-18 — Persistence Deferred

G-0817 defines the semantic Runtime representation of constitutional diagnostics.

It does not determine:

- database schema;

- persistence format;

- indexing;

- storage engine;

- retention policy;

- external telemetry architecture.

Persistence remains governed by the applicable receipt and persistence authorities.

The semantic diagnostic meaning established here must nevertheless be preserved by any authorized downstream representation.

# 22. Receipt Boundary

## D-0817-19 — Diagnostic Receipt Relationship

G-0817 does not independently determine final receipt-field membership.

G-0804 remains authoritative for receipt field-set membership.

Where diagnostics are represented as a separate `ExecutionOutput` sibling, they shall remain semantically distinct from:

- `ExecutionReceipt`;

- `Outcome`;

- `TrustResult`;

- `decisionSummary`;

- `PolicyDecision[]`;

- `evidenceReferences`.

The existing receipt architecture explicitly preserves diagnostics as separate from the `ExecutionReceipt` surface.

# 23. Relationship to G-0809

## D-0817-20 — Hash-Domain Separation

G-0817 defines semantic diagnostic material only.

G-0809 determines whether, and how:

- diagnostics participate in `outputHash`;

- diagnostics participate in `deterministicHash`;

- diagnostics participate in an evidence hash;

- diagnostic material receives a dedicated hash domain;

- diagnostic serialization enters a cryptographic preimage.

No cryptographic treatment may be inferred from G-0817 alone.

This preserves the same semantic/cryptographic separation already ratified for `decisionSummary`.

# 26. Constitutional Invariants

The Council ratifies the following invariants.

### D-0817-21 — Constitutional Relevance

Only constitutionally significant execution conditions may enter the diagnostic surface.

### D-0817-22 — Bounded Representation

Diagnostic representation shall remain constitutionally bounded and shall not become an unrestricted execution trace.

### D-0817-23 — Determinism

Identical authorized execution conditions shall produce identical constitutional diagnostics.

### D-0817-24 — Replay Stability

Constitutional diagnostics shall reproduce under deterministic replay of equivalent execution conditions.

### D-0817-25 — Operational Separation

Operational telemetry shall remain outside the constitutional diagnostic surface unless separately authorized.

### D-0817-26 — Semantic Ownership

G-0817 shall not redefine policy, trust, budget, outcome, error, persistence, or cryptographic semantics owned elsewhere.

### D-0817-27 — Cryptographic Separation

G-0817 shall not independently establish hash-domain or digest semantics.

### D-0817-28 — No Arbitrary Bounds

G-0817 shall establish boundedness semantically without inventing arbitrary numerical limits.

### D-0817-29 — Causal Attribution

Where a constitutional diagnostic is required, it shall contain sufficient bounded information to establish the material execution condition it represents.

# 29. Final Constitutional Principle

The Council records:

**Constitutional diagnostics preserve only the bounded information necessary to explain constitutionally significant execution conditions.**

And:

**Constitutional evidence shall preserve what is necessary to establish why execution was constrained, interrupted, degraded, or otherwise materially affected, while operational detail remains outside the constitutional diagnostic surface unless separately authorized.**

And:

**A diagnostic is explanatory constitutional evidence; it is not a policy result, Runtime outcome, operational trace, cryptographic digest, or replacement for another governing contract.**
