# G-0807 — Council Decision Record

## Policy Evaluation Semantics

**Gate:** G-0807 **Decision:** Council Decision Record **Status:** RATIFIED — CLOSED **Decision Date:** 8 August 2026 **Authority:** Zyppi Constitutional Council **Implementation Authority:** NONE GRANTED BY THIS RECORD

## 1. Constitutional Purpose

G-0807 establishes the constitutional semantics governing policy evaluation within the Zyppi Runtime.

G-0807 defines how the Runtime evaluates the policy universe already constitutionally resolved for an execution.

It does not authorize the Runtime to create, discover, select, modify, or reinterpret the policy universe.

The governing principle is:

**The Runtime evaluates the policy universe constitutionally resolved for an execution. It does not author, discover, select, or alter the policy universe that governs itself.**

# 3. D1 — Policy Universe Architecture

### Ratified Decision

**Direction D — ACV-Bound Explicit Policy Graph.**

The policy universe for an execution is the exact policy graph constitutionally resolved into:

`ActiveConstitutionalView.applicablePolicies`

The upstream constitutional resolution mechanism is authoritative for policy applicability and dependency resolution.

Before Runtime admission:

- the applicable policy graph MUST be complete;

- dependencies MUST be resolved;

- the graph MUST be acyclic;

- unresolved dependencies MUST constitute structural/admission failure.

The Runtime SHALL NOT:

- discover policies;

- select policies;

- add policies;

- remove policies;

- suppress policies;

- infer policies; or

- resolve missing policy dependencies.

### Rejected Alternatives

**Direction A — Explicit Wedge Policy:** rejected as the fundamental policy-universe architecture. Wedge scope remains an application/M08 scope constraint.

**Direction B — Runtime Discovery:** rejected because policy discovery during execution violates Runtime purity and deterministic execution.

**Direction C — Caller-Supplied Policy References:** rejected because the caller is not the constitutional authority for determining the governing policy universe.

### Constitutional Effect

A policy not constitutionally present in the ACV policy graph is not applicable to that execution.

# 4. D2 — `applicablePolicies` / `policyContext`

### Ratified Decision

**Model A — Evaluation Context.**

The semantic separation is absolute:

- `applicablePolicies` determines **what governs**.

- `policyContext` supplies **what is evaluated**.

`policyContext` is strictly non-selecting.

It SHALL NOT:

- select a policy;

- add a policy;

- remove a policy;

- suppress a policy;

- alter policy version;

- alter policy dependencies; or

- otherwise modify the policy universe.

The caller supplies evaluation evidence, not governing authority.

### Missing Context

When a policy requires context that is absent:

**Policy result = `INDETERMINATE`.**

The Runtime SHALL NOT infer, fabricate, or obtain missing context from ambient sources.

A policy reference supplied through `policyContext` that is absent from the ACV SHALL NOT expand the policy universe and constitutes a structural inconsistency.

# 5. D3 — Policy Evaluation Result Contract

### Ratified Decision

The policy result universe is exactly:

**`ALLOW` | `DENY` | `INDETERMINATE`**

### `ALLOW`

The policy was successfully evaluated against the supplied context and its conditions were satisfied.

`ALLOW` means only that the particular policy evaluated successfully and permitted the requested operation.

It does not mean:

- trusted;

- verified;

- authenticated;

- globally authorized; or

- execution success.

### `DENY`

The policy was successfully evaluated and its conditions prohibit the requested operation.

### `INDETERMINATE`

The policy could not produce a determinate result because evaluation could not be completed from the constitutionally supplied inputs.

This includes conditions such as:

- missing mandatory evaluation context;

- required temporal context unavailable;

- unsupported policy semantics within the authorized evaluation scope; or

- another explicitly recognized evaluation-level inability.

### Structural Failure Boundary

Malformed or structurally invalid policy material SHALL NOT become `INDETERMINATE`.

The following remain structural/admission failures:

- malformed policy definitions;

- unresolved dependencies;

- cyclic policy graphs;

- incomplete policy graphs; and

- invalid structural contracts.

The existing `validatePolicyRecord` validation boundary remains responsible for structural validation. G-0807 does not redefine that validation mechanism.

### Policy Decision

Every evaluated policy SHALL produce an auditable `PolicyDecision` containing, at minimum:

- `policyId`;

- `policyVersion`; and

- `result`.

Any additional diagnostic information SHALL remain bounded and deterministic and SHALL NOT alter policy semantics.

### Aggregate Result

The aggregate policy result is a distinct semantic concept from an individual `PolicyDecision`.

# 6. D4 — Aggregation, Precedence, and Termination

### Ratified Aggregation

Policy aggregation is **conjunctive**.

The precedence relationship is:

**`DENY > INDETERMINATE > ALLOW`**

Therefore:

- any `DENY` → aggregate `DENY`;

- no `DENY` but any `INDETERMINATE` → aggregate `INDETERMINATE`;

- all `ALLOW` → aggregate `ALLOW`.

### Complete Evaluation

The Runtime SHALL evaluate the complete applicable policy graph.

A `DENY` may establish the aggregate result, but SHALL NOT by itself terminate policy traversal.

An `INDETERMINATE` SHALL NOT terminate traversal merely because the aggregate result can no longer become `ALLOW`.

This distinction is constitutional:

**Determining the aggregate result is not equivalent to terminating policy evaluation.**

Complete evaluation preserves deterministic auditability and produces the full policy-decision record.

Evaluation may terminate only where a separate constitutional boundary requires termination, including structural failure or authorized budget exhaustion.

# 7. D5 — Deterministic Traversal and Budget Boundary

### Policy Graph

Policy dependencies SHALL form a directed acyclic graph.

### Canonical Traversal

The Runtime SHALL evaluate the graph in deterministic topological order.

Where multiple independent policy nodes are simultaneously eligible, the canonical tie-break is:

**lexicographical ordering of `Policy ID`.**

The resulting `PolicyDecision` sequence SHALL preserve this canonical evaluation order.

### Atomic Evaluation Event

One atomic policy-evaluation event is:

**One policy node evaluated against its explicit evaluation context after its required dependencies are available.**

Internal predicates within a policy are not separate policy-evaluation events for M08.

### Budget Boundary

G-0807 defines the semantic meaning of an atomic policy-evaluation event.

G-0813 remains authoritative for:

- budget accounting;

- budget consumption mechanics;

- budget limits; and

- budget exhaustion mechanics.

Dependency traversal itself is not a G-0807 policy-evaluation event.

### Budget Exhaustion

When authorized policy-evaluation budget is exhausted:

- evaluation SHALL NOT silently continue;

- the condition SHALL be deterministic and auditable;

- evaluation SHALL fail closed;

- an incomplete evaluation SHALL NOT produce aggregate `ALLOW`.

The aggregate policy state resulting from incomplete evaluation is `INDETERMINATE`, subject to the precise budget/error representation owned by G-0813.

### Runtime Execution Model

The current synchronous Runtime model is an implementation characteristic of M08, not a permanent constitutional prohibition on future parallel execution.

Future implementations may evaluate independent branches differently provided that constitutional semantics, canonical result ordering, and replay determinism remain identical.

# 8. D6 — Policy Result to Execution Outcome

### Ratified Boundary

Policy authorization and Trust are separate constitutional domains.

**Policy `ALLOW` does not equal `TRUSTED`.**

### Aggregate `ALLOW`

Aggregate policy `ALLOW` is a necessary prerequisite for progression to downstream Trust evaluation.

It is not sufficient by itself for:

- `TrustResult = TRUSTED`;

- execution success; or

- final execution authorization.

G-0806 remains authoritative for TrustResult semantics.

### Aggregate `DENY`

Aggregate `DENY` is sufficient to reject execution.

No downstream Trust determination may convert a policy denial into permission.

### Aggregate `INDETERMINATE`

Aggregate `INDETERMINATE` SHALL fail closed.

It SHALL NOT be converted into `ALLOW`.

The precise representation of the resulting execution Outcome remains subject to the Outcome vocabulary and ownership established by the downstream execution/trust gates.

### Constitutional Boundary

G-0807 owns:

- policy evaluation;

- individual policy decisions;

- policy aggregation;

- deterministic policy traversal.

G-0807 does not own:

- TrustResult semantics;

- authentication semantics;

- final trust computation; or

- downstream receipt representation.

# 9. Cross-Gate Constitutional Boundaries

### G-0801 — Constitutional Time

G-0807 consumes the temporal semantics established by G-0801.

Runtime SHALL NOT obtain temporal coordinates from ambient time sources.

Where required temporal context is unavailable, policy evaluation SHALL NOT produce `ALLOW`.

### G-0806 — TrustResult

G-0806 consumes policy results as an input to Trust evaluation.

G-0806 SHALL NOT redefine G-0807 policy semantics.

### G-0808 — Execution Context

G-0807 consumes the explicit execution context established by the applicable Execution Context contract.

It SHALL NOT silently redefine that contract.

### G-0809 — Hash Domains

G-0807 establishes deterministic ordering of policy decisions.

G-0809 remains authoritative for hash-domain construction.

### G-0813 — Budget

G-0807 defines the semantic atomic policy-evaluation event.

G-0813 defines budget mechanics and accounting.

### G-0815 / G-0816 / G-0817

These downstream gates remain authoritative for their respective representations of:

- policy version;

- decision summary; and

- diagnostics.

G-0807 does not assume ownership of those representations.

# 10. CAW-011 / M08 Scope

G-0807 confirms that M08 SHALL evaluate only the minimum policy catalog authorized for the **CAW-011 GS1 Commerce Atlas Wedge**.

The specific catalog contents are a **scope-definition matter**, not a semantic rule of G-0807.

Accordingly:

- G-0807 SHALL NOT invent additional policy types;

- G-0807 SHALL NOT expand M08 into federation or future cross-jurisdictional policy mechanisms;

- policies outside the constitutionally authorized M08 catalog are outside this gate's implementation scope.

The authoritative CAW-011 policy catalog shall be established by the appropriate wedge/application scope authority.

# 11. Ratified G-0807 Invariants

The following invariants are hereby ratified:

**P-0807-01 — Explicit Policy Universe** Runtime evaluates only policies constitutionally established as applicable for the execution.

**P-0807-02 — No Ambient Discovery** Runtime SHALL NOT discover policies through ambient state or external I/O.

**P-0807-03 — Structural Applicability** Policy applicability is established before Runtime evaluation.

**P-0807-04 — Deterministic Ordering** Equivalent executions produce equivalent policy evaluation ordering.

**P-0807-05 — Fail Closed** Missing, unresolved, malformed, or unevaluable policy conditions SHALL NOT produce `ALLOW`.

**P-0807-06 — Policy/Trust Separation** Policy `ALLOW` SHALL NOT imply `TrustResult = TRUSTED`.

**P-0807-07 — Implementation Independence** Policy semantics SHALL remain independent of Runtime implementation mechanics.

**P-0807-08 — Replay-Stable Ordering** Policy evaluation ordering SHALL be deterministic and replay-stable.

**P-0807-09 — No Constitutional Mutation** Policy evaluation SHALL NOT mutate the constitutional inputs establishing the policy universe.

**P-0807-10 — Budget Boundary** Policy evaluation exposes deterministic atomic events to the budget mechanism governed by G-0813.

**P-0807-11 — Caller Non-Selection** `policyContext` supplies evaluation evidence and SHALL NOT select the governing policy universe.

**P-0807-12 — Complete Evaluation** Applicable policies SHALL be evaluated completely unless a separate constitutional termination condition prevents completion.

# 13. Required Constitutional Amendments

The following amendments are required where not already represented in the governing corpus:

1. Policy-universe resolution MUST be complete before Runtime admission.

2. `policyContext` MUST be explicitly non-selecting.

3. `ALLOW`, `DENY`, and `INDETERMINATE` SHALL be formally defined as the policy result universe.

4. Structural/admission failures SHALL remain distinct from `INDETERMINATE`.

5. Aggregation SHALL be conjunctive with precedence `DENY > INDETERMINATE > ALLOW`.

6. Canonical topological traversal and Policy ID tie-breaking SHALL be preserved.

7. Policy `ALLOW` SHALL be explicitly distinguished from Trust.

8. Budget-event semantics SHALL remain separated between G-0807 and G-0813.

These amendments are semantic requirements, not implementation instructions.

# 14. Rejected Architectural Alternatives

The Council formally rejects:

- Runtime policy discovery;

- caller-controlled policy selection;

- policy selection through `policyContext`;

- binary-only policy result semantics;

- treating missing evaluation context as `ALLOW`;

- conflating structural failure with policy `INDETERMINATE`;

- disjunctive policy aggregation for the M08 model;

- policy `ALLOW` as equivalent to Trust;

- Runtime mutation of the resolved policy universe.

# 15. Final Council Determination

The Zyppi Constitutional Council hereby **RATIFIES AND CLOSES G-0807 — Policy Evaluation Semantics**.

The six semantic decisions are final as recorded herein.

G-0807 establishes a deterministic policy-evaluation boundary in which:

**Authority is constitutionally resolved before execution. Context supplies evidence for evaluation. Runtime evaluates without discovering or altering the governing policy universe. Policy results are explicit, auditable, deterministic, and fail closed. Policy permission remains distinct from Trust.**
