# G-0806 — Council Decision Record

## TrustResult Semantics

**Gate ID:** G-0806 **Gate Name:** `TrustResult` Semantics **Milestone:** M08 — Runtime Execution Semantics **Decision Authority:** Zyppi Constitutional Council **Status:** **RATIFIED** **Decision Class:** Constitutional Semantic Decision **Implementation Authority:** None granted by this record

## 1. Constitutional Question

What does the Runtime's `TrustResult` mean, and how is it derived from evidence, authority, policy, and other execution results?

The existing M08-GDR identifies three candidate directions:

- **Direction A — Evidence-Derived Trust**

- **Direction B — Composite Trust**

- **Direction C — Trust as an Independent Constitutional Result**

CAW-007 requires a `trustResult: TrustResult`, while the existing CAW vocabulary exposes trust states including:

`definite | probable | possible | uncertain | speculative`

The M08-GDR explicitly requires M08 to define the constitutional mapping rather than invent an unrelated trust vocabulary.

# 2. Council Decision

## **RATIFIED — Direction C: Trust Is an Independent Constitutional Result**

The Council ratifies **Direction C**.

`TrustResult` is a distinct constitutional result computed from the constitutionally authorized evidence available to the execution.

Trust is neither:

- a PolicyDecision;

- an authorization result;

- an execution Outcome;

- a capability grant;

- an identity itself;

- nor a general-purpose execution health score.

Trust SHALL remain semantically distinct from Policy and Outcome.

The Runtime SHALL compute Trust according to the ratified TrustResult contract and SHALL NOT infer Trust from the fact that a Policy evaluates to `ALLOW`.

The principle established by this decision is:

**Policy ALLOW does not mean TRUSTED.**

This preserves the semantic separation required by the G-0807 policy decision and the M08 layer-separation invariant. The M08-GDR expressly identifies Direction C as the direction that prevents policy permission from being conflated with trust.

# 3. D1 — Meaning of Trust

### Decision

`TrustResult` expresses the Runtime's deterministic constitutional assessment of the **trustworthiness of the evidence underlying the execution context**.

Trust is an emergent constitutional conclusion derived from evidence.

It does not itself answer:

- whether an action is permitted;

- whether a Subject possesses a particular permission;

- whether an execution succeeded;

- whether the requested operation should be performed.

Those questions remain owned by their respective constitutional domains.

### Constitutional Rule

**Trust describes the evidentiary trust state. It does not authorize the action and does not constitute the execution Outcome.**

# 4. D2 — Trust Inputs

### Decision

**Evidence is the foundational input to TrustResult.**

Identity, Authority, Standing and Capability SHALL NOT become independent "trust scores" within G-0806.

Where such constructs are relevant to Trust, their evidentiary basis is evaluated through the underlying constitutionally authorized Evidence.

The semantic separation is therefore:
`Evidence    │    ├──► TrustResult    │    ├──► Identity / Authority / Capability projections    │    └──► other constitutional evaluations  TrustResult + Policy Context    │    ▼ Policy Evaluation    │    ▼ Execution Outcome `
G-0806 does not redefine the constitutional semantics of Identity, Authority, Capability, Policy, or Outcome.

# 5. D3 — Policy / Trust Relationship

### Decision

**Policy SHALL NOT produce, modify, upgrade, downgrade, or otherwise participate in the computation of TrustResult.**

Policy and Trust are related through execution semantics but remain distinct.

The Policy Evaluator may **consume TrustResult** when evaluating a policy requiring a particular trust condition.

The Trust Evaluator SHALL NOT consume its own downstream PolicyDecision to manufacture or alter TrustResult.

Therefore:
`Evidence → TrustResult                  │                  ▼           Policy Evaluation                  │                  ▼               Outcome `
and not:
`Evidence → Trust → Policy → modified Trust `

### Constitutional Principle

**Trust is not created by Policy, and Policy ALLOW does not create Trust.**

This prevents circular evaluation and preserves the G-0807 boundary.

# 6. D4 — Trust Vocabulary

### Decision

The authoritative M08 `trustStatus` vocabulary is:

- `definite`

- `probable`

- `possible`

- `uncertain`

- `speculative`

The Council explicitly rejects creation of a second, parallel constitutional Trust vocabulary for M08.

In particular, the following proposed vocabulary is **not adopted as a constitutional Runtime vocabulary**:

- `VERIFIED`

- `PARTIAL`

- `CONFLICTING`

- `UNVERIFIED`

- `COMPROMISED`

The M08-GDR already identifies the five CAW trust states and states that M08 must establish their constitutional mapping rather than invent a new one.

### Important Boundary

This decision establishes the **authoritative result vocabulary**.

It does **not** authorize M08 implementation to invent arbitrary numerical probability values or unsupported thresholds for these states.

The detailed semantic mapping SHALL be implemented only according to the authoritative Trust semantics available to the implementation at the time of planning.

# 7. D5 — Evidence, Completeness and Degradation

### Decision

Trust SHALL degrade deterministically when the constitutionally required evidentiary basis is incomplete, invalid, stale, conflicting, or otherwise insufficient to establish a stronger Trust state.

The Runtime SHALL NOT:

- fabricate missing evidence;

- obtain evidence from ambient infrastructure;

- silently substitute unavailable evidence;

- convert unresolved evidence requirements into `definite`;

- silently treat missing evidence as successful verification.

The resulting Trust state remains a **TrustResult**.

It does not automatically become an execution Outcome merely because evidence is degraded.

Whether a particular Trust state satisfies the requirements of a particular action is determined by the governing Policy and downstream execution semantics.

This is consistent with the M08 fail-closed invariant: unresolved constitutional requirements must not be converted into `allow`, `success`, `trusted`, or `valid`.

# 10. D8 — Minimum `TrustResult` Contract

### Decision

The constitutional minimum is a bounded Trust result containing:
`TrustResult ├── trustStatus └── degradationFactors `

### `trustStatus`

The computed Trust state using the authoritative vocabulary:
`definite probable possible uncertain speculative `

### `degradationFactors`

A bounded, deterministic collection of reason codes or equivalent constitutional explanations identifying material factors preventing a stronger Trust state.

The exact diagnostic vocabulary and canonicalization remain subject to the appropriate downstream diagnostic authority.

### Explicit Exclusions

`TrustResult` SHALL NOT contain:

- raw Evidence payloads;

- complete EvidenceBundle contents;

- PolicyDecision records;

- execution Outcome;

- execution trace;

- stack traces;

- arbitrary human prose;

- experience-layer presentation strings;

- unbounded diagnostic material.

This preserves the separation between semantic result, evidence, receipt representation, and diagnostics.

# 11. Rejected Directions

## Direction A — Evidence-Derived Trust

**Rejected as the complete architectural direction.**

Evidence remains the foundational input to Trust, but Direction A alone does not adequately express the Council's requirement that Trust exist as an independently governed constitutional result.

The Council therefore adopts the **evidence-derived semantics within Direction C**, rather than treating Direction A as the complete gate architecture.

## Direction B — Composite Trust

**Rejected.**

The Council rejects a composite Trust model that aggregates Policy, Authority, Capability, Outcome, and other heterogeneous execution dimensions into a single "trust score."

Such a model would:

- blur constitutional ownership;

- introduce precedence rules that belong to other domains;

- risk circular dependencies;

- make Trust difficult to explain;

- increase long-term semantic coupling.

# 12. Constitutional Invariants Established by G-0806

The following invariants are ratified:

### G806-T01 — Independent Trust

`TrustResult` is an independent constitutional result.

### G806-T02 — Evidence Foundation

Trust is derived from constitutionally authorized Evidence.

### G806-T03 — Policy Separation

Policy SHALL NOT generate or modify TrustResult.

### G806-T04 — Policy ALLOW ≠ TRUSTED

A Policy `ALLOW` SHALL NOT itself establish Trust.

### G806-T05 — Outcome Separation

TrustResult SHALL NOT itself constitute the execution Outcome.

### G806-T06 — No Ambient Trust

Trust evaluation SHALL NOT obtain hidden external state.

### G806-T07 — Deterministic Trust

Identical authorized inputs SHALL produce identical TrustResult.

### G806-T08 — Bounded Result

TrustResult SHALL remain bounded and SHALL NOT contain raw evidence or unbounded execution diagnostics.

### G806-T09 — Canonical Vocabulary

M08 SHALL use the established TrustResult vocabulary:

`definite | probable | possible | uncertain | speculative`

### G806-T10 — No Vocabulary Fragmentation

M08 SHALL NOT introduce an alternative constitutional Trust ontology merely for Runtime implementation convenience.

### G806-T11 — No Fabricated Evidence

Missing or insufficient evidence SHALL NOT be silently converted into a stronger Trust state.

### G806-T12 — Constitutional Ownership

G-0806 SHALL NOT redefine Policy semantics, Outcome semantics, Evidence semantics, Identity semantics, Capability semantics, or receipt/hash semantics owned by other constitutional gates.

# 16. Implementation Consequence

M08 implementation may proceed only within the boundaries established here.

The implementation SHALL:

- compute Trust from explicit constitutional evidence;

- produce the ratified `trustStatus`;

- produce bounded deterministic degradation information;

- remain deterministic and replayable;

- keep Trust separate from Policy and Outcome;

- avoid ambient information sources;

- avoid introducing a second constitutional Trust vocabulary.

The implementation SHALL NOT:

- infer Trust from Policy `ALLOW`;

- create a composite trust score from unrelated constitutional dimensions;

- invent probabilistic thresholds;

- introduce ambient verification;

- embed raw evidence inside `TrustResult`;

- use G-0806 as authority to modify unrelated constitutional contracts.

The M08-GDR establishes that implementation eligibility follows Council ratification and that implementation agents must stop rather than infer unresolved constitutional semantics.

# 19. Constitutional Rationale

The Council selects Direction C because it provides the cleanest permanent boundary:
`Evidence    │    ▼ TrustResult    │    ▼ Policy Evaluation    │    ▼ Execution Outcome `
This structure prevents three fundamental category errors:

1.

**Evidence ≠ Trust** Evidence is the basis from which Trust is derived.

2.

**Trust ≠ Permission** Trust does not authorize an action.

3.

**Permission ≠ Outcome** Policy evaluation does not itself constitute execution success.

The decision therefore preserves semantic independence while maintaining a deterministic execution model.

The M08-GDR identifies semantic separation, prevention of "policy allowed = trusted," and long-term extensibility as the principal advantages of Direction C.
