# PRJ-003 — Digital Product Passport Projection Specification

###### Version: 1.0 (Ratified)

**Status:** LOCKED

**Parent Constitution:** `PRJ-001` Projection Architecture Constitution

**Dependent Constitutions:**

- ZRM v1.x
- `ARM-001` Asset Reality Model
- `ARM-P-001` Product Profile
- `PRJ-001` Projection Architecture Constitution

---

# 1. Mission and Constitutional Scope

## 1.1 Purpose

`PRJ-003` governs the transformation of a Reality View into a Digital Product Passport (DPP) representation.

The Digital Product Passport is a projection.

It is not reality.

It is not a storage model.

It is not a data repository.

It is not an asset type.

`PRJ-003` defines how Asset Realities, Observations, and Relationships are rendered into passport outputs required by regulatory, commercial, and sustainability ecosystems.

---

## 1.2 Constitutional Boundary

`PRJ-003` SHALL NOT:

- Define products
- Define materials
- Define suppliers
- Define sustainability entities
- Define carbon entities
- Define repairability entities
- Define compliance entities

Those realities already exist within:

- `ARM-001`
- `ARM-P-001`
- ZRM

`PRJ-003` only projects them.

---

## 1.3 Directionality Rule

Reality SHALL flow into Passport Projections.
````
Reality → DPP
````
Allowed.
````
DPP → Reality
````
Forbidden.

No passport requirement may modify:

- Asset Realities
- Observation structures
- Identity bindings
- Relationships
- Canonical zIDs

---

# 2. Generic Passport Principle

## 2.1 Jurisdiction Neutrality

`PRJ-003` SHALL remain jurisdiction agnostic.

The projection engine SHALL NOT be coupled to:

- EU Battery Passport
- EU Textile Passport
- EU Electronics Passport
- China Passport Systems
- Future National Passport Systems

Jurisdictional requirements SHALL be implemented through registered schemas.

---

## 2.2 Passport Schema Separation

Passport standards SHALL exist as schema configurations.

Schemas SHALL NOT alter:

- Reality computation
- Identity resolution
- Observation models
- Relationship models

Schemas affect formatting only.

---

# 4. Passport Schema Registry

## 4.1 Purpose

The Passport Schema Registry defines projection formats.

Schemas are external contracts.

Schemas are not constitutional entities.

---

## 4.2 Example Registry Entries

````
eu:battery:v1
eu:textile:v1
eu:electronics:v1

cn:battery:v1
cn:textile:v1

global:circularity:v1
````
Additional schemas may be registered without constitutional amendment.

---

## 4.3 Schema Independence Rule

The same Reality View may be projected simultaneously into multiple schemas.

**Example:**
````
Reality View
      │
      ├── eu:battery:v1
      │
      ├── global:circularity:v1
      │
      └── future:jurisdiction:v1
````
All projections derive from the same reality.

---

# 5. Identity Resolution

## 5.1 Canonical Identity Requirement

All passport generation SHALL begin with canonical `zID` resolution.

Passports SHALL NEVER be generated directly from:

- GTIN
- Serial Number
- Lot Number
- External URI
- Regulatory Identifier

External identifiers MUST first resolve through Identity Bindings.

---

## 5.2 Resolution Path
````
External Identifier
        │
        ▼
Identity Resolution
        │
        ▼
Canonical zID
        │
        ▼
Reality View
        │
        ▼
Policy Evaluation
        │
        ▼
Passport Projection
````

---

## 6.4 Regulatory View

# 7. Passport Mapping Rules

## 7.1 Constitutional Rule

`PRJ-003` SHALL NOT create new data structures.

Passport content SHALL be derived exclusively from:

- Asset Realities
- Observations
- Relationships

---

## 7.2 Material Composition

Material composition SHALL be projected from relationships.

Examples:
````
Contains
Component_Of
Composed_Of
````

---

## 7.3 Sustainability Information

Sustainability information SHALL be projected from observations.

Examples:
````
Carbon_Audit_Completed
Recycled_Content_Verified
Energy_Efficiency_Verified
````

---

## 7.4 Compliance Information

Compliance information SHALL be projected from observations.

Examples:
````
REACH_Compliance_Verified
SCIP_Submission_Completed
Hazardous_Substance_Detected
````

---

## 7.5 Repairability Information

Repairability SHALL be derived from:

- Product Profile parameters
- Observations
- Supporting relationships

No native repairability entity shall exist.

# 12. Passport Disappearance Test

If `PRJ-003` is permanently deleted:

- Products continue to exist
- Batches continue to exist
- Instances continue to exist
- Observations continue to exist
- Relationships continue to exist
- Identity bindings continue to exist

Only passport outputs disappear.

# Final Constitutional Statement

`PRJ-003` establishes Digital Product Passports as jurisdiction-neutral projections of canonical reality.

It introduces:

- No new entities
- No new storage models
- No new identity systems
- No new reality primitives

It is a projection layer only.

All future passport standards SHALL inherit from `PRJ-003` through schema registration rather than constitutional modification.

**`PRJ-003` Ratified and Locked.**
