# §1 — Purpose & Constitutional Position

ARM-001 defines the minimum constitutional structure required for any entity to participate actively in Zyppi Reality. It does not replace, duplicate, or extend the ZRM ontology. It is an operational specification that equips an already-constituted CL-05 Referent with the capabilities necessary to be addressed, observed, and projected within the Zyppi ecosystem.

The constitutional dependency chain is:

```
WS-01 through WS-05E (Constitutional Foundation)
│
▼
ZRM v1.1 (Reality Model Doctrine)
│
▼
CL-05 Referent (Ontological Classification — already ratified)
│
▼
ARM-001 Asset Reality Model ← THIS DOCUMENT
│
▼
PRJ-001 Projection Architecture
│
▼
ZT-001 / EXP-001 (Touch & Experience Layers)
```

ARM-001 answers one question and one question only:

> "What must be true of an entity for it to be addressable, observable, and **projectable within Zyppi**?"

Everything else — GS1 identifiers, lifecycle state machines, experience rules, touch protocols, API design, SDK behavior — is explicitly out of scope and governed by successor documents.

# §2 — Constitutional Decision: Asset Reality and CL-05

## 2.1 Ruling

The council unanimously adopts Option A:

> An Asset Reality is a CL-05 Referent equipped with a permanent Zyppi identity (zID), an observation index, and a profile declaration.

ARM-001 does not create a new ontological concept. It adds operational participation capability to a concept already constituted by CL-05 and WS-03A.3. No constitutional amendment is required.

## 2.2 Consequences

· Every existing CL-05 subtype (Physical, Document, Digital, Composite Referent) is a valid Asset Reality through profile declaration.
· The ZRM ontology is fully preserved. ARM-001 sits above it, not beside it.
· Future asset classes are introduced as CL-05 subtypes, not as new ARM-001 concepts.
· The zID is a Zyppi-layer identifier assigned to a CL-05 Referent. It does not replace or compete with CL-04 Identity references.

```
ZRM Ontology
└── CL-05 Referent (what exists)
    └── Asset Reality (ARM-001 operational model)
        └── Profiles (ARM-P-001 through ARM-P-NNN)
```

# §3 — Constitutional Principles (CP-AR-001 through CP-AR-008)

The following eight principles are hereby ratified and locked. They govern all downstream work in the ARM, PRJ, ZT, and EXP series.

---

## CP-AR-001 — zID Permanence

Every Asset Reality possesses a permanent Zyppi identity (zID) that is independent of any external standard, identifier system, or regulatory framework. External identifiers (GTIN, EPC, VIN, ISBN, etc.) are projections of or references to the zID. They are never equivalent to it. A zID, once assigned, is never changed, reassigned, or retired.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

---

## CP-AR-002 — Projection Immutability

The generation of any projection (GS1 Digital Link, EPCIS event, DPP artifact, API response, consumer experience) from an Asset Reality SHALL NOT modify the canonical Asset Reality. Projections are derived, read-only representations. Reality remains canonical. All projections expire; canonical reality does not.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

---

## CP-AR-003 — Observation Completeness

Every interaction with an Asset Reality SHALL produce a CL-11 Event referencing the Asset's zID, regardless of whether resolution succeeds. A failed scan, an unknown product signal, and a counterfeit detection are constitutionally equivalent observations to a successful resolution. Zyppi records reality, not only success.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

---

## CP-AR-004 — Asset Universality

Any CL-05 Referent capable of participating in reality may be represented as an Asset Reality through profile declaration. Products, documents, locations, devices, vehicles, persons (in regulatory contexts), and all future entity classes are valid Asset Realities. No asset class is privileged in the core model.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

---

## CP-AR-005 — Projection Multiplicity

An Asset Reality MAY generate unlimited projections without altering its canonical state. The same Asset Reality may simultaneously support GS1 Digital Link, EPCIS, DPP, SAP, consumer experience, and AI-readable projections. Projection quantity does not affect canonical reality.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

---

## CP-AR-006 — Reality Links

Relationships are first-class participants in reality. Reified Relationships (per WS-04A and WS-04B) SHALL possess independent identity, lifecycle governance, temporal validity, and provenance. Observations MAY reference Relationships as subjects. Relationships SHALL NOT own or accumulate observations directly. Observation ownership belongs exclusively to CL-11 Event.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

---

## CP-AR-007 — Computed Reality

Observations are immutably stored. Reality state, projections, experiences, and representations SHALL be derived from stored observations, relationships, policies, and context at the time of computation. No derived state SHALL be treated as a canonical source of truth. Materialized views and cached projections are constitutionally permitted as performance artifacts, provided they declare their derivation source and are never treated as authoritative.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

---

## CP-AR-008 — Profile Isolation

A profile SHALL only declare what is specific to its asset class. Profiles SHALL NOT introduce new constitutional primitives, modify ARM-001 core concepts, embed commercial or standard-specific assumptions into shared constitutional structure, or create dependencies between profiles. The first profile produced SHALL NOT be treated as the default model for all Asset Realities.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

# §4 — The Asset Reality Core

## 4.1 Definition

The Asset Reality core is the minimum conceptual structure that any CL-05 Referent must possess to actively participate in Zyppi Reality. It is defined as five constitutional concepts. These are concepts, not field definitions. Schema and storage representations belong to RI-002 CSM translation.

```
Asset Reality Core
│
├── zID                Permanent, immutable Zyppi identity
├── Asset Type         Profile reference (declares specialization)
├── Identities         References to CL-04 Identity records
├── Observations       Index of CL-11 Events referencing this Asset
└── Provenance         Constitutional origin chain
```

## 4.2 zID

The zID is the permanent, canonical Zyppi identifier for an Asset Reality. It is assigned at asset constitution time and never changes. It is independent of any external identifier. It is the primary resolution target for all zTouch interactions and the anchor for all projections.

- Format: UUIDv7, deterministically generated at constitution time per RI-001 §27.
- Permanence: a zID is never retired, reassigned, or modified (CP-AR-001).
- Relationship to external IDs: external identifiers (GTIN, EPC, VIN, etc.) are registered as CL-04 Identity references that resolve to the zID, not as equivalents of it.

## 4.3 Asset Type

The Asset Type declares which profile governs this Asset Reality. It is a reference to a registered ARM-P series profile. It determines which attributes, projection declarations, and lifecycle extensions apply beyond the core.

- Every Asset Reality SHALL declare exactly one primary Asset Type.
- Asset Type is immutable after constitution. If an entity changes class, supersession creates a new Asset Reality.
- The profile registry is governed by §6 of this document.

## 4.4 Identities

The Identities concept references the set of CL-04 Identity records (per WS-03A.2) that resolve to this Asset Reality. A QR code, an NFC tag, a GTIN, a serial number, a GS1 Digital Link, and an internal SKU are all CL-04 Identities referencing the same Asset Reality.

- Cardinality: 0..N — an Asset Reality MAY have zero identities while in Draft state.
- Identity Convergence (from WS-03A.3 CR-REF-04): multiple identities may reference the same Asset Reality without conflict.
- The Identity lifecycle is governed by WS-03A.2, not by ARM-001.

## 4.5 Observations

The Observations concept is an index of CL-11 Events that reference this Asset Reality's zID. ARM-001 does not redefine, extend, or duplicate CL-11. The full event model — immutability, bitemporality, confidence, source taxonomy, no lifecycle states — is inherited directly from WS-03A.8.

- CP-AR-003 requires that every interaction produces an observation, regardless of outcome.
- The observation index is a derived view of CL-11, not a separate store.
- Intelligence derived from observations belongs to CL-16 and is governed by WS-03A.9.

## 4.6 Provenance

The Provenance concept records the constitutional origin of the Asset Reality: which compiler run constituted it, which Population Plan batch it originated from, which Certificate authorized it, and which Artifact Manifest references it. This preserves the full traceability chain established by RI-001 through RI-005.

# §5 — State Definition

## 5.1 What State Is

State is a deterministic projection derived from the set of all observations referencing an Asset Reality, evaluated against active relationships, policies, and context at a specific point in time.

> **State** is computed. **State** is never stored as canonical. **State** has no independent existence.

This definition prevents the proliferation of competing state concepts across profiles. Words such as status, condition, lifecycle, and phase are all expressions of State in specific profile contexts. They derive from observations; they do not own canonical truth.

## 5.2 Stored vs. Derived


Category  | Examples  | Storage Rule
---|---|---
Stored (immutable) | CL-11 Events, CL-09 Transactions, CL-10 Outcomes, CL-04 Identities, zID | Stored permanently and immutably
Derived (computed) | Current location, ownership status, trust score, product pedigree, lifecycle phase | Computed from stored facts at query time
Materialized (permitted cache) | GS1 projection cache, experience routing cache, AI context snapshot | Permitted for performance; non-canonical; must declare derivation source

Materialized caches are constitutionally permitted under CP-AR-007 provided they satisfy two conditions: they declare which observations and policies they were derived from, and they are invalidated when any referenced observation or policy changes. The cache governance model is specified in PRJ-001.

## 5.3 State and Profiles

Profiles MAY define named state labels specific to their asset class (e.g., a Product Profile may define states such as Pre-Production, Active, Recalled, Decommissioned). These labels are human-readable tags on computed projections. They are not stored lifecycle state machines. They do not represent canonical truth. They are derived from observations and policies at query time.

# §6 — Relationship Model

## 6.1 Constitutional Basis

The relationship model for Asset Reality entities inherits the full WS-04A and WS-04B constitutional framework. Structural Edges represent topology. Reified Relationships represent governed, auditable connections. ARM-001 adds one constitutional rule: the Reification Boundary.

## 6.2 Reification Boundary

A relationship between two Asset Realities SHALL be constituted as a Reified Relationship (per WS-04B) if and only if at least one of the following conditions is true:


Condition  | Rationale  | Example
---|---|---
It has its own lifecycle | Reification required when the relationship transitions through states independently of either Asset | Warranty Agreement: active → claimed → expired
It requires independent provenance |  Reification required when the relationship must be traceable to evidence, authority, or transaction | Ownership Transfer: requires title, authority, transaction
It can be referenced independently |  Reification required when other entities (Events, Intelligence, Contracts) point to the relationship directly | Custody Record: referenced by compliance audits
It can be acted upon independently | Reification required when an Intent or Contract targets the relationship itself rather than either endpoint | Service Agreement: subject of a renewal Contract

Absent all four conditions, a Structural Edge is constitutionally sufficient. The test prevents graph inflation. Simple topological facts **(a product is contained in a case, a building contains a floor)** remain Structural Edges.

## 6.3 Relationship Assets

When a Reified Relationship satisfies the reification conditions above, it participates in reality as a first-class object per CP-AR-006. It receives a UUIDv7 identity per WS-04B §4. Observations may reference it per CP-AR-003. It is therefore a Reality Link — a relationship that is itself addressable within the Zyppi graph.

Recursion is bounded: a Reality Link does not become an Asset Reality. It does not receive a zID. It does not have its own profiles. It participates in reality through the existing Reified Relationship model.

# §7 — Profile Architecture

## 7.1 Profile Definition

A profile is a constitutional extension of the ARM-001 core that declares the attributes, projection support declarations, and state label vocabulary specific to one asset class. Profiles are implementation specifications, not constitutional foundations. They extend downward from the ARM-001 core. They never reach upward to modify it.

## 7.2 Profile Governance Rules

### PR-RULE-001 — Profile Minimalism

A profile SHALL only declare what cannot be derived from the ARM-001 core. If an attribute, projection, or state label applies to all Asset Realities equally, it belongs in the core, not in every profile separately.

### PR-RULE-002 — Profile Isolation

A profile SHALL NOT modify ARM-001 core concepts. Profiles SHALL NOT create dependencies on other profiles. A Product Profile SHALL NOT assume or reference a Location Profile. Cross-profile relationships are expressed through Reified Relationships between Asset Reality instances, not through profile coupling.

### PR-RULE-003 — Profile Projection Declaration

Every profile SHALL declare the projections it supports. A Product Profile declares GS1 Digital Link, EPCIS, and DPP support. A Vehicle Profile declares VIN and fleet management projections. Projection support is a profile attribute. It is not a core attribute. The projection content is governed by PRJ-001 and its successors.

### PR-RULE-004 — No First-Profile Privilege

The first profile produced (ARM-P-001 Product) SHALL NOT be treated as the default or reference model for all other profiles. All profiles are equal specializations of the ARM-001 core. Commercial priority does not confer constitutional authority.

## 7.3 Profile Registry

The following profile slots are reserved. Profiles are drafted only after ARM-001 is ratified. Each profile is an independent document; no profile blocks any other.


Profile ID  | Asset Class  | Primary Projections (indicative)
---|---|---
ARM-P-001 | Product | GS1 Digital Link, EPCIS, DPP, Consumer Experience
ARM-P-002 | Document | Regulatory Filing, Certificate, Contract
ARM-P-003 | Location | GS1 GLN, Mapping Services, Spatial Indexes
ARM-P-004 | Device / Machine / Vehicle | IoT Protocols, Fleet Management, Maintenance Records
ARM-P-005 | Person (regulatory contexts only) | Healthcare Identity, Credential Verification

Future profiles (ARM-P-006 and beyond) require council approval and must satisfy PR-RULE-001 through PR-RULE-004 before admission.

# §8 — Observation Contract

## 8.1 Reference, Not Redefinition

ARM-001 does not define an observation model. It references CL-11 Event exclusively. The full event model — source taxonomy, event categories, bitemporality, confidence, immutability, no lifecycle states, no supersession, evidence optional — is inherited entirely from WS-03A.8.

## 8.2 The Single ARM-001 Observation Rule

### OBS-001 — Universal Observation Rule

Every interaction with an Asset Reality SHALL produce a CL-11 Event that references the Asset's zID in its related_entities field. This rule applies regardless of interaction outcome. A successful QR resolution, a failed NFC read, a counterfeit signal detection, and an AI agent query against an unknown product are all constitutionally equivalent observations. The zTouch layer (ZT-001) is responsible for guaranteeing this event emission before experience resolution begins.

###### Status: RATIFIED & LOCKED

## 8.3 Intelligence Separation

Observations (CL-11) are raw, immutable facts. Interpretations of observations (trust scores, risk classifications, pattern detection, supply chain intelligence) belong to CL-16 Intelligence per WS-03A.9. ARM-001 does not govern intelligence. The intelligence model is inherited from the existing constitutional framework.

# §9 — Projection Contract

## 9.1 ARM-001's Role in Projections

ARM-001 establishes the constitutional ground rules for projections. The content, format, and generation mechanics of individual projections are governed by PRJ-001 (Projection Architecture) and its successors. ARM-001 states only what is universal across all projections.

## 9.2 Universal Projection Rules

### PROJ-RULE-001 — Derivation Only

Every projection is derived from the Asset Reality core, profile declarations, active policies, and context. No projection may contain data not derivable from these inputs. No projection may modify the Asset Reality it derives from (CP-AR-002).

### PROJ-RULE-002 — Profile Declaration Required

A projection type may only be generated from an Asset Reality whose profile declares support for that projection type (PR-RULE-003). An Asset Reality with a Device Profile does not generate GS1 Digital Links unless the Device Profile declares GS1 projection support.

### PROJ-RULE-003 — Cache Non-Canonicality

Materialized projection caches are permitted for performance under CP-AR-007. They are never canonical. They must declare their derivation source, derivation timestamp, and invalidation conditions. Cache governance is specified in PRJ-001.

### PROJ-RULE-004 — GS1 Is a Projection

GS1 Digital Link, EPCIS events, GDSN records, and all GS1-family outputs are projections of Asset Reality. They are CL-14 Compliance entities per WS-03A.0 §4. They are never the canonical identity of an Asset Reality. This rule is constitutionally permanent and may not be reversed by any profile or projection specification.

## 11.3 Sequencing Logic

The sequence above is dependency-ordered, not priority-ordered. The following rules govern it:

1. `ARM-001` must be ratified before any profile or successor layer begins drafting. The core must precede all specializations.
2. Profiles (`ARM-P-001` through `ARM-P-004`) may be drafted in parallel after `ARM-001` ratification. They do not block each other.
3. `PRJ-001` must precede all individual projection specifications (`PRJ-002`, `PRJ-003`). The universal projection rules must exist before GS1-specific rules are written.
4. `ZT-001` must precede all touch carrier specifications (`ZT-002` through `ZT-004`) for the same reason.
5. `EXP-001` requires both `PRJ-001` and `ZT-001`, because experience resolution sits above both projection and touch.
6. `SDK-001` requires `EXP-001`. Developers cannot be given runtime interfaces before the experience model is constituted.
7. `WS-003E` is final because it governs how constitutional changes propagate through the entire stack, which only makes sense once the stack is complete.
