# 1. Purpose

SEC-001 establishes the constitutional laws governing trust, identity, isolation, attestation, and security throughout the Zyppi Runtime Era.

It defines the immutable security properties that every compliant Runtime MUST preserve regardless of implementation language, infrastructure, cryptographic algorithm, deployment topology, operating system, hardware platform, cloud provider, or future technology.

SEC-001 does **not** define security implementations.

It defines constitutional security invariants.

Implementations evolve.

The Constitution does not.

## 12. Technology-Independent Security Invariants
 
### Purpose
 
The constitutional security guarantees defined in this section SHALL remain valid regardless of implementation language, cryptographic algorithm, operating system, cloud provider, hardware platform, execution environment, or future technological paradigm.
 
These invariants define **what SHALL always be true**, never **how** they are implemented.
  
### SEC-INV-001 — Truth Integrity
 
A Runtime SHALL NEVER modify constitutional truth.
 
Published constitutional artifacts SHALL remain immutable throughout their entire lifecycle.
 
This applies to, but is not limited to:
 
 
- Active Context Volumes (ACVs)
 
- Registry Bundles
 
- Merkle Trees
 
- Verification Certificates
 
- Constitutional Documents
 
- Published Ontology
 

 
A Runtime may consume truth.
 
It SHALL NEVER rewrite truth.
  
### SEC-INV-002 — Provenance Preservation
 
Every constitutional artifact SHALL preserve complete provenance.
 
No Runtime SHALL:
 
 
- remove lineage
 
- replace lineage
 
- truncate lineage
 
- fabricate lineage
 
- obscure lineage
 

 
Every output SHALL remain traceable to its constitutional source.
  
### SEC-INV-003 — Capability Enforcement
 
Every governed operation SHALL require Proof of Capability.
 
No execution path SHALL bypass capability evaluation.
 
If capability cannot be proven,
 
execution SHALL terminate.
  
### SEC-INV-004 — Identity Integrity
 
Every acting entity SHALL possess a constitutional identity.
 
Anonymous execution SHALL NEVER exist inside the Runtime.
 
Every action SHALL be attributable to:
 
 
- a Human Identity,
 
- an Organizational Identity,
 
- or a Technical Identity.
 

  
### SEC-INV-005 — Attestation Integrity
 
Every trust decision SHALL originate from one or more valid constitutional attestations.
 
Self-declared trust SHALL possess no constitutional value.
  
### SEC-INV-006 — Non-Repudiation
 
Every attested constitutional action SHALL be non-repudiable.
 
Once an attestation has been issued,
 
its existence SHALL remain historically verifiable,
 
even after expiration or revocation.
  
### SEC-INV-007 — Runtime Isolation
 
Execution Contexts SHALL remain mutually isolated.
 
Failure inside one Execution Context SHALL NOT compromise:
 
 
- another Execution Context
 
- another tenant
 
- another Runtime
 
- another Organization
 

 
Isolation SHALL be preserved throughout execution.
  
### SEC-INV-008 — Least Capability
 
Every entity SHALL operate using the minimum capability required to complete its assigned responsibility.
 
Capabilities SHALL NOT be expanded for convenience.
 
Temporary capability elevation SHALL:
 
 
- be explicitly attested,
 
- be time bounded,
 
- be revocable,
 
- be fully auditable.
 

  
### SEC-INV-009 — Deterministic Failure
 
When constitutional trust cannot be established,
 
the Runtime SHALL fail deterministically.
 
The Runtime SHALL NEVER:
 
 
- guess,
 
- infer,
 
- retry using weaker guarantees,
 
- silently continue,
 
- substitute alternative trust.
 

 
Failure SHALL always preserve constitutional correctness.
  
### SEC-INV-010 — Revocation Priority
 
Revocation SHALL always supersede previous trust.
 
When conflicting attestations exist,
 
the most recent valid revocation SHALL prevail.
 
Revoked trust SHALL immediately become invalid for future operations.
  
### SEC-INV-011 — Cryptographic Agility
 
No constitutional invariant SHALL depend upon any specific cryptographic algorithm.
 
Implementations MAY evolve.
 
Constitutional guarantees SHALL remain unchanged.
  
### SEC-INV-012 — Auditability
 
Every governed operation SHALL be reconstructable.
 
Independent auditors SHALL be capable of determining:
 
 
- who acted,
 
- what was accessed,
 
- why capability existed,

# 14. Constitutional Trust Lifecycle
 
## Purpose
 
Trust is not permanent.
 
Trust is a constitutional state that is established, exercised, suspended, revoked, and renewed through attested events.
 
Every constitutional trust relationship SHALL possess a well-defined lifecycle.
 
No trust SHALL exist outside this lifecycle.
  
## 14.1 Lifecycle Principles
 
The Constitutional Trust Lifecycle SHALL satisfy the following principles:
 
 
- Trust SHALL be explicitly established.
 
- Trust SHALL never be assumed.
 
- Trust SHALL remain bounded.
 
- Trust SHALL remain continuously verifiable.
 
- Trust SHALL be revocable.
 
- Trust SHALL be historically auditable.
 
- Trust SHALL never become permanent.
 

  
## 14.2 Trust States
 
Every constitutional trust relationship SHALL exist in exactly one of the following states.
 
### State 1 — Established
 
Trust begins when a valid constitutional attestation is issued.
 
Establishment SHALL define:
 
 
- subject identity
 
- capability scope
 
- sponsoring authority
 
- validity period
 
- constitutional issuer
 
- attestation identifier
 

 
No constitutional capability exists before Establishment.
  
### State 2 — Active
 
A trust relationship becomes Active once all establishment requirements have been satisfied.
 
During this state:
 
 
- capabilities MAY be exercised,
 
- policies SHALL be evaluated,
 
- execution MAY proceed,
 
- attestations SHALL remain valid.
 

 
Active trust SHALL always remain continuously verifiable.
  
### State 3 — Suspended
 
Trust MAY be temporarily suspended without permanent revocation.
 
Examples include:
 
 
- security investigation
 
- abnormal runtime behavior
 
- policy review
 
- temporary organizational restrictions
 
- regulatory intervention
 

 
During Suspension:
 
 
- capabilities SHALL NOT be exercised,
 
- identity SHALL remain intact,
 
- historical provenance SHALL remain preserved.
 

 
Suspension SHALL itself constitute an attested constitutional event.
  
### State 4 — Revoked
 
Revocation permanently invalidates an active trust relationship.
 
Revocation SHALL occur whenever constitutional authority determines that trust is no longer valid.
 
Examples include:
 
 
- credential compromise
 
- runtime non-compliance
 
- organizational removal
 
- delegated authority withdrawal
 
- constitutional violation
 

 
Once revoked:
 
 
- capabilities immediately terminate,
 
- execution SHALL cease,
 
- future authorization SHALL fail.
 

 
Revocation SHALL NOT erase historical evidence.
  
### State 5 — Expired
 
Trust naturally expires when its defined validity period ends.
 
Expiration SHALL occur automatically.
 
Expired trust:
 
 
- possesses historical value,
 
- possesses no operational authority.
 

 
Expiration SHALL require no manual intervention.
  
### State 6 — Restored
 
Previously suspended or expired trust MAY be restored only through issuance of a new constitutional attestation.
 
Restoration SHALL NEVER reactivate an old attestation.
 
Every restoration SHALL create an entirely new constitutional trust relationship.
  
## 14.3 Trust State Machine
 
The constitutional lifecycle SHALL follow the following progression:
 `Established       │       ▼    Active    ├─────────────┐    ▼             ▼ Suspended     Expired    │             │    ▼             ▼ Restored      Restored    │    ▼  Active  At any point:  Active ─────────► Revoked Suspended ──────► Revoked Established ────► Revoked  Revoked is terminal. `  
## 14.4 Continuous Verification
 
Trust SHALL NOT rely upon a single verification event.
 
Every constitutional Runtime SHALL continuously verify:
 
 
- identity validity,
 
- attestation validity,
 
- capability validity,
 
- policy applicability,
 
- runtime compliance.
 

 
Trust SHALL decay unless continuously proven.
  
## 14.5 Revocation Priority
 
Whenever multiple trust decisions conflict,
 
the following precedence SHALL apply:
 
 
1. Revocation
 
2. Suspension
 
3. Expiration
 
4. Active
 
5. Established
 

 
Revocation SHALL always override previous trust.
  
## 14.6 Historical Preservation
 
Revoked, expired, and suspended trust relationships SHALL remain permanently auditable.
 
Historical trust SHALL NEVER be deleted.
 
Historical trust SHALL NEVER regain operational authority.
  
## 14.7 Trust Renewal
 
Renewal SHALL NOT extend an existing attestation.
 
Renewal SHALL always produce:
 
 
- a new attestation,
 
- a new validity period,
 
- a new constitutional record,
 
- a new audit chain.
 

 
Historical continuity SHALL remain preserved.
