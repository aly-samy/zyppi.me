# Purpose

CMM-001 establishes the canonical mathematical language of the Zyppi ecosystem.

Every future equation, simulation, optimization model, statistical analysis, forecasting engine, AI model, valuation model, trust algorithm, and computational implementation shall derive from this document.

Unlike CES-001, which defines economic simulation, CMM-001 defines the mathematical universe within which every Zyppi model operates.

It is therefore the constitutional mathematics of Zyppi.

---

# Constitutional Philosophy

Reality precedes mathematics.

Mathematics describes reality.

It never creates it.

Accordingly:

> Every mathematical model within Zyppi shall model constitutional reality rather than replace constitutional reality.

Mathematics exists to improve understanding—not to redefine truth.

---

# Constitutional Mathematical Laws

## Law 1 — Mathematical Consistency

Every equation shall be internally consistent.

No equation may violate its own assumptions.

---

## Law 2 — Constitutional Consistency

Mathematics may never contradict higher constitutional documents.

## Law 3 — Explicit Assumptions

- constraints
- variables
- constants
- uncertainty

## Law 4 — Reproducibility

Two independent implementations of CMM-001 shall produce equivalent results within defined numerical tolerances.

---

## Law 5 — Traceability

Every calculated value shall be traceable back to:

- inputs
- equations
- assumptions
- data sources

No calculation shall exist as a black box.

---

## Law 6 — Dimensional Integrity

Every equation shall preserve dimensional consistency.

Invalid units invalidate the equation.

---

## Law 7 — Separation of Mathematics and Implementation

Equations belong to CMM.

Software belongs to CES.

Programming languages shall never redefine mathematics.

---

## Law 8 — Stability

Small changes in input shall produce proportionally reasonable changes in output unless explicitly modeled otherwise.

---

## Law 9 — Constitutional Transparency

Every coefficient, multiplier, probability distribution, weighting factor, and threshold shall be documented.

---

## Law 10 — Continuous Calibration

Mathematical models may evolve only through constitutional governance using measured evidence.
