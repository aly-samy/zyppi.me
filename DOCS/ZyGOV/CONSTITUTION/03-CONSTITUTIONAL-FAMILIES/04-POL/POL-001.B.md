# 1. Purpose
 
This specification establishes the constitutional laws governing Authority.
 
It defines:
- where Authority originates,
 
- how Authority may be granted,
 
- how Authority may be delegated,
 
- how Authority terminates,
 
- how Authority propagates,
 
- and the immutable constraints governing every Authority relationship.
 

 
This document does not define runtime authorization algorithms or permission evaluation.
  
# 2. Constitutional Definition of Authority
 
Authority is the constitutional right to authorize actions affecting Reality.
 
Authority is never inherent.
 
Authority always originates from an explicit constitutional source.
 
Authority exists independently from execution.
  
# 3. Constitutional Sources of Authority
 
Authority SHALL originate only from recognized constitutional sources.
 
Three constitutional origins exist.
  
## AO-001 — Sovereign Authority
 
Authority originating directly from a sovereign constitutional actor.
 
Examples include:
- governments
 
- constitutional owners
 
- organizational founders
 
- governing boards
 
- legally recognized institutions
 
No lower authority may create Sovereign Authority.
  
## AO-002 — Institutional Authority
 
Authority granted by a recognized sovereign entity.
 
Institutional Authority derives legitimacy entirely from its grantor.
 
It possesses no independent sovereignty.
  
## AO-003 — Delegated Authority
 
Authority temporarily transferred by another authorized actor.
 
Delegated Authority is always subordinate to its originating Authority.
  
# 4. Authority Prerequisites
 
Authority SHALL exist only when all constitutional prerequisites are satisfied.
 
An Identity must possess:
- valid Identity
 
- valid Standing
 
- constitutional Capacity
 
- legitimate Authority Origin

Absence of any prerequisite invalidates Authority.
  
# 5. Authority Principles
 
## AP-001
 
Authority is granted.
 
Never assumed.
  
## AP-002
 
Authority is bounded.
 
Unlimited Authority is constitutionally invalid.
  
## AP-003
 
Authority is attributable.
 
Every Authority SHALL possess identifiable provenance.
  
## AP-004
 
Authority is revocable.
 
No Authority is constitutionally permanent.
  
## AP-005
 
Authority is contextual.
 
Authority never implies unrestricted action.
  
# 6. Delegation
 
Delegation is the constitutional transfer of Authority from one authorized actor to another.
 
Delegation transfers authority.
 
Delegation never transfers sovereignty.
  
# 7. Delegation Laws
 
## DR-001 — Delegation Requires Authority
 
Only an actor possessing valid Authority may delegate Authority.
  
## DR-002 — Delegation Cannot Exceed Origin
 
No delegated Authority may exceed the Authority possessed by the delegator.
  
## DR-003 — Delegation Preserves Provenance
 
Every delegation SHALL preserve constitutional lineage back to its originating Authority.
 
Authority provenance shall never be lost.
  
## DR-004 — Delegation Never Creates Sovereignty
 
Delegation creates subordinate Authority only.
 
It never creates new constitutional origins.
  
## DR-005 — Delegation Is Explicit
 
Delegation shall always occur through explicit constitutional grant.
 
Implicit delegation is prohibited.
  
## DR-006 — Delegation Is Independently Revocable
 
Every delegation may be revoked independently of sibling delegations.
  
## DR-007 — Delegation Is Temporally Bound
 
Delegated Authority shall exist only within constitutionally valid temporal boundaries.
  
# 8. Authority Revocation
 
Authority may cease through:
- expiration
 
- revocation
 
- Standing suspension
 
- Standing termination
 
- constitutional succession
 
- constitutional dissolution
 
Authority ceases immediately upon constitutional revocation.
 
Operational transition behavior is governed elsewhere.
  
# 9. Cascade Principle
 
Delegated Authority derives validity from its parent Authority.
 
Loss of constitutional validity of the parent Authority invalidates dependent delegations.
 
The constitutional relationship is immutable.
 
Operational handling of dependent systems is outside the scope of this specification.
  
# 10. Constitutional Succession
 
Authority may transfer through constitutional succession.
 
Succession does not preserve Authority identity.
 
Instead:
- previous Authority terminates
 
- successor Authority is constitutionally established

Historical provenance SHALL remain permanently preserved.
  
# 11. AI Authority
 
Artificial actors possess no inherent Authority.
 
AI Authority SHALL always originate through explicit constitutional delegation.
 
AI systems may exercise Authority only within constitutionally delegated boundaries.
 
AI systems shall never originate Sovereign Authority.
  
# 12. Organizational Authority
 
Organizations exercise Authority through recognized constitutional structures.
 
Organizational Authority belongs to the organization rather than any individual member.
 
Individuals exercise organizational Authority only through constitutionally recognized roles.
  
# 13. Federation
 
Authority shall never automatically cross constitutional domains.
 
Cross-domain Authority requires explicit constitutional recognition.
 
Recognition is distinct from delegation.
 
Recognition preserves sovereignty.
  
# 14. Emergency Authority
 
Emergency Authority is constitutional Authority established exclusively for extraordinary constitutional circumstances.
 
Emergency Authority:
- shall possess explicit constitutional origin,
 
- shall remain temporally bounded,
 
- shall remain fully attributable,
 
- shall remain permanently auditable.
 
Emergency Authority shall never permanently replace ordinary constitutional Authority.
  
# 15. Permanent Invariants
 
The following invariants SHALL remain true.

- Authority always has provenance.
 
- Authority always has origin.
 
- Authority never implies sovereignty.
 
- Delegation never exceeds origin.
 
- Delegation never creates sovereignty.
 
- Revocation terminates constitutional validity.
 
- Standing governs Authority eligibility.
 
- AI Authority is always delegated.
 
- Federation never implies shared sovereignty.
 
- Constitutional history is immutable.
 

  
# 16. Constitutional Boundaries
 
POL-001.B defines constitutional Authority only.
 
It intentionally excludes:
- permission evaluation,
 
- authorization algorithms,
 
- policy resolution,
 
- runtime contracts,
 
- execution behavior,
 
- implementation mechanisms.

Those specifications belong to subsequent POL documents.
