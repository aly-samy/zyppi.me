# 1. Purpose
 
The Policy Constitution establishes the immutable principles governing authority, permission, constitutional standing, and policy throughout the Zyppi ecosystem.
 
It defines **who may act**, **under what constitutional conditions**, and **according to which immutable laws**, while remaining completely independent of implementation, execution engines, storage technologies, programming languages, and runtime architecture.
 
This document defines constitutional truths rather than operational behavior.
  
# 2. Scope
 
This specification governs:
 
- Constitutional Standing
 
- Constitutional Capacity
 
- Authority Philosophy
 
- Permission Philosophy
 
- Policy Design Laws
 
- Permanent Policy Invariants
 
- Constitutional Vocabulary
 
- Constitutional Boundaries
 
 
This specification does not define:
 
 
- Runtime evaluation
 
- Authorization algorithms
 
- Delegation mechanics
 
- Federation protocols
 
- Security implementation
 
- Execution pipelines
 
- Interface contracts
 

 
Those are specified by subsequent POL documents.
  
# 3. Constitutional Principles
 
## CP-001 — Authority Exists to Protect Reality
 
Authority exists solely to regulate legitimate modification of Reality.
 
Authority is never an intrinsic property of an Identity.
 
Authority is always granted.
  
## CP-002 — Default Denial
 
No action is constitutionally permitted unless explicitly authorized.
 
Absence of permission SHALL be interpreted as denial.
  
## CP-003 — Explicit Governance
 
Every constitutional authorization shall originate from an explicit constitutional source.
 
Implicit authority does not exist.
  
## CP-004 — Separation of Identity and Governance
 
Identity establishes existence.
 
Policy establishes capability.
 
Neither modifies the other.
  
## CP-005 — Separation of Constitutional State and Operational Transition
 
Changes to constitutional state become immediately authoritative.
 
Operational transition behavior is governed separately by Runtime constitutional contracts.
  
# 4. Constitutional Standing
 
## Definition
 
Constitutional Standing is the constitutional capacity of an Identity to participate in governance.
 
Standing determines whether an Identity may hold or exercise Authority.
 
Standing is distinct from Identity.
 
Standing may change while Identity remains immutable.
  
## Standing States
 
An Identity SHALL possess exactly one Standing state.
 
Possible constitutional states include:
 
- Active
 
- Suspended
 
- Terminated
 
- Time-Gated

 
Additional jurisdiction-specific states may extend these classifications without modifying this Constitution.
  
## Standing Principles
 
### STD-001
 
Standing precedes Authority.
 
Authority cannot exist without valid Standing.
  
### STD-002
 
Standing is not Authority.
 
Standing establishes eligibility.
 
Authority establishes power.
  
### STD-003
 
Standing changes are constitutional events.
 
Every Standing transition SHALL be permanently recorded.
  
### STD-004
 
Standing is jurisdiction-aware.
 
Jurisdictions may recognize different Standing conditions while preserving Identity.
  
### STD-005
 
Standing never alters Identity.
 
Identity remains immutable regardless of Standing.
  
# 5. Constitutional Capacity
 
## Definition
 
> Capacity defines the constitutional functions an Identity in valid Standing may presently perform.
>
> Capacity constrains Authority without modifying Authority itself.
 
Examples include:
 
- Read-only
 
- Approval
 
- Delegation
 
- Administration
 
- Emergency
 
- Observation

 
Capacity is constitutional.
 
Permissions are contextual.
  
# 6. Authority Philosophy
 
Authority is never created spontaneously.
 
Authority SHALL originate from a recognized constitutional source.
 
Authority may be:
 
 
- Granted
 
- Delegated
 
- Restricted
 
- Revoked
 
Authority may never exist without constitutional provenance.
  
# 7. Permission Philosophy
 
Every constitutional permission is evaluated as the relationship between:

- Subject
 
- Action
 
- Target
 
- Context
 
No single element is sufficient by itself.
 
Permission exists only through the complete constitutional relationship.
  
# 8. Policy Design Laws
 
## PDL-001 — Law of Explicit Authority
 
All authority shall originate from an explicit constitutional source.
  
## PDL-002 — Law of Least Authority
 
Only the minimum authority necessary should exist.
  
## PDL-003 — Law of Constitutional Separation
 
Identity, Standing, Capacity, Authority, Permission, and Execution are independent constitutional concepts.
  
## PDL-004 — Law of Orthogonal Compliance
 
Authorization and Compliance are independent constitutional dimensions.
 
One does not imply the other.
  
## PDL-005 — Law of Immutable Provenance
 
Every constitutional decision shall possess permanent provenance.
  
## PDL-006 — Law of Jurisdictional Sovereignty
 
Jurisdictions govern their own policy without altering the Constitution.
  
## PDL-007 — Law of Explicit Delegation
 
Delegated authority shall never exceed its constitutional origin.
  
## PDL-008 — Law of Temporal Validity
 
Every constitutional authorization exists within time.
 
Timeless authority does not exist.
  
## PDL-009 — Law of Federation Sovereignty
 
Federation recognizes foreign authority without surrendering local sovereignty.
  
## PDL-010 — Law of AI Constitutional Equality
 
Artificial actors are governed by the same constitutional laws as every other Identity.
 
No special constitutional privilege exists.
  
## PDL-011 — Law of Explainability
 
Every constitutional decision shall be capable of constitutional explanation.
  
## PDL-012 — Law of Determinism
 
Identical constitutional inputs shall always produce identical constitutional outcomes.
  
# 9. Permanent Invariants
 
The following invariants SHALL remain true across all future versions of Zyppi.
 
 
- Identity precedes Standing.
 
- Standing precedes Authority.
 
- Authority precedes Permission.
 
- Permission precedes Execution.
 
- Authority always has provenance.
 
- Permission is contextual.
 
- Compliance is orthogonal.
 
- Identity is immutable.
 
- Constitutional truth is independent of implementation.
 
- Constitutional state changes are immediate.
