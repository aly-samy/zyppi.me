# POL-001.D — Policy Lifecycle & Temporal Governance

# 1. Purpose
 
This specification establishes the constitutional lifecycle of Policy.
 
It defines:
- how Policies come into existence,
 
- how Policies evolve,
 
- how Policies terminate,
 
- how historical Policies remain constitutionally valid,
 
- how time governs constitutional authorization.
 
This specification intentionally excludes implementation-specific deployment, storage, synchronization, and version-control mechanisms.
  
# 2. Constitutional Definition
 
A Policy is a constitutional governance artifact.
 
Policies are immutable once constitutionally activated.
 
Constitutional evolution occurs through succession rather than modification.
  
# 3. Policy Lifecycle
 
Every Policy SHALL exist in exactly one constitutional lifecycle state.
 
The constitutional lifecycle consists of:

- Draft
 
- Active
 
- Deprecated
 
- Retired
 
- Archived

Additional implementation states may exist provided they do not alter these constitutional meanings.
  
# 4. Lifecycle States
 
## PL-001 — Draft
 
A Draft Policy possesses no constitutional authority.
 
It may be reviewed, discussed, or revised.
 
It shall not participate in Authorization.
  
## PL-002 — Active
 
An Active Policy possesses full constitutional authority.
 
Only Active Policies may govern Authorization.
  
## PL-003 — Deprecated
 
A Deprecated Policy remains constitutionally valid for historical interpretation.
 
It shall not govern new constitutional authorizations unless explicitly permitted by constitutional governance.
  
## PL-004 — Retired
 
A Retired Policy no longer governs future Authorization.
 
Historical constitutional validity remains preserved.
  
## PL-005 — Archived
 
Archived Policies remain permanent constitutional records.
 
Archival never removes constitutional history.
  
# 5. Policy Immutability
 
Once a Policy becomes Active:
 
- its constitutional content is immutable,
 
- its constitutional identity is permanent,
 
- its historical meaning shall never change.
 
Corrections require a successor Policy.
 
Existing Policies are never edited.
  
# 6. Policy Succession
 
Policy evolution occurs exclusively through constitutional succession.
 
Successor Policies establish new constitutional governance without modifying prior Policies.
 
Both predecessor and successor remain permanent constitutional records.
  
## Succession Principles
 
### PS-001
 
Every successor SHALL explicitly identify its predecessor.
  
### PS-002
 
Succession preserves constitutional history.
  
### PS-003
 
Succession never rewrites historical Authorization.
  
### PS-004
 
Multiple successor branches may exist where constitutional governance permits.
  
# 7. Temporal Governance
 
All constitutional authorization exists within time.
 
Every Authorization SHALL be interpreted using the constitutionally valid Policies applicable at the relevant constitutional moment.
  
## Temporal Principles
 
### TG-001
 
Time is a constitutional dimension.
  
### TG-002
 
Historical decisions remain governed by historical Policies.
  
### TG-003
 
Future Policies shall never retroactively alter historical constitutional decisions.
  
### TG-004
 
Constitutional validity begins only upon constitutional activation.
  
### TG-005
 
Constitutional validity ends only through constitutional retirement or succession.
  
# 8. Historical Interpretation
 
Historical constitutional events SHALL always remain interpretable using the constitutional framework that existed when they occurred.
 
Historical interpretation shall never depend upon later constitutional revisions.
  
# 9. Constitutional Activation
 
Policies become constitutionally authoritative only through explicit constitutional activation.
 
Creation alone does not grant constitutional authority.
 
Activation SHALL occur through recognized constitutional governance.
  
# 10. Constitutional Expiration
 
Policies may possess finite constitutional validity.
 
Expiration shall never erase constitutional history.
 
Expiration affects future governance only.
 
Historical constitutional interpretation remains unchanged.
  
# 11. Offline Constitutional Validity
 
Constitutional validity is independent of network connectivity.
 
Temporary operational disconnection shall not alter constitutional status.
 
Mechanisms supporting offline governance belong to Runtime specifications.
  
# 12. Policy Continuity
 
At every constitutional moment:

- governance SHALL remain continuous,
 
- constitutional gaps SHALL not exist,
 
- applicable Policies SHALL be determinable.
 
Constitutional continuity is mandatory.
  
# 13. Permanent Invariants
 
The following invariants SHALL remain true.

- Policies are immutable once Active.
 
- Policy evolution occurs through succession.
 
- History is never rewritten.
 
- Historical authorization remains permanently valid.
 
- Time is a constitutional dimension.
 
- Policy activation is explicit.
 
- Policy expiration affects only future governance.
 
- Archived Policies remain permanent constitutional records.
 
- Constitutional continuity shall always exist.
 
- Historical interpretation is immutable.

  
# 14. Constitutional Boundaries
 
POL-001.D establishes only constitutional lifecycle principles.
 
It intentionally excludes:
 
- deployment procedures,
 
- storage mechanisms,
 
- synchronization,
 
- version-control systems,
 
- replication,
 
- caching,
 
- runtime distribution,
 
- implementation technologies.
 
Those concerns belong to Runtime specifications.
