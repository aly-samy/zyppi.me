# 1. Purpose

CES-001 establishes the canonical simulation model used to forecast Zyppi's long-term economic evolution.

Unlike conventional financial models, the Constitutional Economic Simulator models the evolution of the entire ecosystem rather than merely projecting financial statements.

It exists to answer one constitutional question:

> **"If our assumptions change today, how does the constitutional ecosystem evolve over the next 3, 5, 10, and 20 years?"**

---

# 2. Constitutional Philosophy

The future cannot be predicted.

It can only be explored.

CES therefore does not produce a single forecast.

It produces multiple constitutional futures based upon explicit assumptions.

Every output is therefore only as trustworthy as the assumptions that produced it.

---

## Constitutional Principle

Every simulation shall expose its assumptions.

Nothing shall be hidden.

---

CES shall enable Zyppi to:

- evaluate strategic decisions
