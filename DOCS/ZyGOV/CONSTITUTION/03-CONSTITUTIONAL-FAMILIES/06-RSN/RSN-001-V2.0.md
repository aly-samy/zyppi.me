# RSN-001 — Universal Reasoning Constitution

###### Version: 2.0

**Status**: PROPOSED RATIFICATION DRAFT

**Authority**: Zyppi Constitutional Architecture

**Classification**: Constitutional Domain

---

# 1. Purpose

RSN-001 establishes the universal constitutional laws governing the production of intelligence within Zyppi.

RSN governs how evidence becomes intelligence.

RSN does not govern Reality.

RSN does not govern Projections.

RSN does not govern Experiences.

RSN governs the transformation of evidence into governed intelligence artifacts.

The purpose of RSN is to guarantee that all reasoning performed within Zyppi remains:

- Evidence grounded
- Auditable
- Immutable
- Model independent
- Paradigm independent
- Epistemically safe

---

# 2. Constitutional Position

RSN occupies the Intelligence Production Layer of the constitutional stack.

Reality remains authoritative.

Projection remains deterministic.

Reasoning remains governed.

Experience remains composable.

Execution remains external.

---

# 3. Constitutional Definition of Reasoning

Reasoning is the constitutional capability to transform evidence into intelligence through a governed process that produces an auditable intelligence artifact.

Reasoning is defined by its outputs and guarantees, not by the computational methods used to generate them.

Reasoning SHALL remain independent of:

- Models
- Algorithms
- Toolchains
- Agent frameworks
- Future reasoning paradigms

---

# 4. RSN-LD-001 — Reasoning as Constitutional Infrastructure

Reasoning SHALL exist as a constitutional domain.

Reasoning SHALL NOT be implemented as unmanaged application logic.

All intelligence production SHALL occur through governed reasoning processes.

---

# 5. RSN-LD-002 — Model Independence

Computational models are execution engines.

Models are not constitutional assets.

Models MAY be replaced without constitutional amendment.

Constitutional assets include:

- Evidence
- Execution Proofs
- Reasoning Manifests
- Intelligence Artifacts
- Registered Methodologies
- Registered Reasoning Paradigms

---

# 6. RSN-LD-003 — Paradigm Abstraction

The Constitution SHALL govern reasoning principles rather than reasoning implementations.

The Blueprint Paradigm is the primary reasoning paradigm.

Future paradigms MAY be recognized by constitutional ratification provided they satisfy:

- Evidence grounding
- Auditability
- Immutable outputs
- Execution proof generation
- Manifest generation

The Constitution SHALL survive the replacement of any individual reasoning paradigm.

---

# 7. RSN-LD-004 — Epistemic Integrity

Every reasoning execution SHALL support:

Premise Validation

The ability to verify the foundational premise of an investigation.

Unknown Cause Conclusions

The ability to conclude that a phenomenon exists without forcing attribution.

Evidence Status Classification

Evidence SHALL be classified as:

- Supported
- Refuted
- Unsupported
- Unknown
- Not Evaluated

Novelty Detection

The ability to identify conditions that fall outside known methodologies.

---

# 8. RSN-LD-005 — Evidence Primacy

All reasoning begins with evidence.

Evidence SHALL originate from:

- ZRM Reality Graph
- Authorized Reality Views
- Authorized Projections
- Authorized Intelligence Artifacts

Evidence SHALL NOT be fabricated.

Primary evidence always outweighs derived evidence.

---

# 9. RSN-LD-006 — Execution Proof Requirement

Every reasoning execution SHALL generate an Execution Proof.

The Execution Proof demonstrates:

- What evidence was used
- What reasoning requirements were satisfied
- What validation steps occurred
- What conclusions were generated

An intelligence artifact without an Execution Proof is constitutionally invalid.

---

# 10. RSN-LD-007 — Reasoning Manifest Requirement

Every reasoning execution SHALL generate a Reasoning Manifest.

The Manifest serves as the public explanation layer.

The Execution Proof serves as the audit layer.

---

# 11. RSN-LD-008 — Intelligence Artifact Production

Reasoning exists to produce CL-16 Intelligence Artifacts.

The CL-16 specification governs artifact structure.

RSN governs artifact production.

---

# 12. RSN-LD-009 — Read-Only Reasoning

Reasoning SHALL NOT:

- Modify Reality
- Create Transactions
- Alter Identity
- Change Ownership
- Execute Actions

Reasoning MAY generate:

- Conclusions
- Predictions
- Recommendations
- Action Proposals

Execution remains external.

---

# 13. RSN-LD-010 — Conditional Invocation

Reasoning SHALL activate only when analytical processing is required.

Examples:

- Investigation
- Diagnosis
- Prediction
- Optimization
- Recommendation
- Comparison

Reasoning SHALL NOT activate for simple retrieval operations.

---

# 14. RSN-LD-011 — Conflict Preservation

Conflicting conclusions SHALL be preserved.

Conflict is information.

Conflict SHALL NOT be hidden.

Confidence SHALL be disclosed.

Evidence SHALL be disclosed.

---

# 15. RSN-LD-012 — Human Escalation

The Constitution permits human escalation when:

- Reasoning paradigms conflict
- Divergence exceeds approved thresholds
- Governance policy requires review

Human escalation remains a governance mechanism rather than a reasoning mechanism.

---

# 16. RSN-LD-013 — Reasoning Immutability

Execution Proofs are immutable.

Reasoning Manifests are immutable.

Intelligence Artifacts are immutable.

Corrections require new artifacts.

History SHALL NOT be rewritten.

---

# 17. RSN-LD-014 — Universal Availability of Reasoning

Zyppi SHALL retain the ability to reason about novel situations.

No system state SHALL exist where intelligence production becomes impossible because a domain-specific methodology is absent.

Fallback reasoning mechanisms SHALL exist.
