# RSN-002 — Methodology Registry Constitution

###### Version: 1.0

**Status**: PROPOSED RATIFICATION DRAFT

**Authority**: Zyppi Constitutional Architecture

**Classification**: Constitutional Domain

**Depends On:**

- RSN-001 Universal Reasoning Constitution

---

# 1. Purpose

RSN-002 governs reusable reasoning methodologies.

RSN-002 does not define reasoning itself.

RSN-002 governs the registration, execution, governance, lifecycle, and auditability of methodologies used by reasoning paradigms.

The purpose of RSN-002 is to ensure that reusable methodologies remain:

- Governed
- Auditable
- Immutable
- Comparable
- Evolvable
- Safe

---

# 2. Constitutional Position

RSN-001 governs universal reasoning laws.

RSN-002 governs methodology artifacts.

Relationship:
````
Universal Reasoning
↓
Reasoning Paradigm
↓
Methodology Registry (RSN-002)
↓
Execution
↓
Execution Proof
↓
Reasoning Manifest
↓
CL-16 Intelligence Artifact
````

---

# 3. Constitutional Definition of Methodology

A Methodology is a reusable reasoning contract that defines how a class of investigations should be conducted.

Methodologies do not perform reasoning.

Methodologies constrain reasoning.

Methodologies do not execute.

Methodologies govern execution.

---

# 4. RSN-LD-011 — Methodology Registry

The Methodology Registry SHALL exist as a constitutional registry.

Only registered methodologies may execute within governed paradigms.

Unregistered methodologies are constitutionally prohibited.

---

# 5. RSN-LD-012 — Blueprint Definition

A Blueprint is the primary methodology artifact.

**A Blueprint defines:**

- Mandatory reasoning gates
- Required evidence categories
- Validation rules
- Output schema
- Failure policy
- Escalation policy
- Performance metrics

**A Blueprint SHALL NOT define:**

- Tool sequences
- Execution paths
- Models
- Agent architectures
- Internal planner logic

---

# 6. RSN-LD-013 — Blueprint Immutability

Blueprints are immutable.

Modification requires:

- New version
- New identifier
- Registry registration

Historical versions remain permanently accessible.

---

# 7. RSN-LD-014 — Blueprint Identity

Every Blueprint SHALL possess:

- blueprint_id
- version
- status
- title
- description
- intent_scope
- domain_scope
- owner
- created_at
- supersedes

---

# 8. RSN-LD-015 — Mandatory Reasoning Gates

Blueprints SHALL define mandatory gates.

A gate is a constitutional requirement that must be satisfied before execution is considered complete.

**Examples**:

- Premise Verification
- Candidate Cause Generation
- Evidence Validation
- Counterfactual Testing
- Confidence Assessment
- Divergence Analysis

Execution paths may vary.

Gate satisfaction may not vary.

---

# 9. RSN-LD-016 — Premise Verification Gate

Blueprints performing investigation SHALL include Premise Verification.

If the foundational premise is disproven:

Execution SHALL terminate.

The resulting artifact SHALL report:

Premise Refuted

rather than continuing investigation.

---

# 10. RSN-LD-017 — Evidence Requirements

Blueprints SHALL declare:

- Required evidence classes
- Optional evidence classes
- Evidence freshness requirements
- Evidence sufficiency thresholds

Blueprints SHALL NOT fabricate evidence.

---

# 11. RSN-LD-018 — Evidence Status Classification

Blueprint outputs SHALL support:

- Supported
- Refuted
- Unsupported
- Unknown
- Not Evaluated

Absence of evidence and evidence of absence are constitutionally distinct.

---

# 12. RSN-LD-019 — Unknown Cause Conclusions

Blueprints SHALL permit Unknown Cause outcomes.

Investigations SHALL NOT force attribution.

If all known explanations fail:

Unknown Cause

is a valid constitutional conclusion.

---

# 13. RSN-LD-020 — Polycausal Conclusions

Blueprints SHALL support multiple simultaneous causes.

Methodologies SHALL NOT require a single dominant explanation.

Distributed attribution is permitted.

---

# 14. RSN-LD-021 — Execution Proof Requirement

Every Blueprint execution SHALL produce an Execution Proof.

The proof must demonstrate:

- Gates satisfied
- Evidence used
- Validation performed
- Conclusions generated

Execution without proof is invalid.

---

# 15. RSN-LD-022 — Planner Independence

Execution engines are not constitutional assets.

Blueprints govern requirements.

Planners satisfy requirements.

The Constitution governs Blueprints and Execution Proofs.

The Constitution does not govern Planner internals.

---

# 16. RSN-LD-023 — Playbook Governance

Playbooks are organizational methodology extensions.

Playbooks are not constitutional artifacts.

**Playbooks may:**

- Configure
- Specialize
- Localize

**Playbooks may not:**

- Remove mandatory gates
- Reduce evidence requirements
- Weaken validation rules
- Alter constitutional guarantees

---

# 17. RSN-LD-024 — Methodology Performance

Blueprints SHALL define performance metrics.

**Examples**:

- Accuracy
- Calibration
- False Positive Rate
- False Negative Rate
- Outcome Alignment

Performance metrics are governance inputs.

Performance metrics SHALL NOT automatically alter routing decisions.

---

# 18. RSN-LD-025 — Divergence Handling

Competing methodologies may execute simultaneously.

When conclusions diverge:

- Divergence must be preserved
- Evidence must be disclosed
- Confidence must be disclosed

Conflict is information.

Conflict is not failure.

---

# 19. RSN-LD-026 — Human Escalation

Blueprints may require escalation.

Escalation triggers include:

- High-risk decisions
- Unresolved divergence
- Regulatory requirements
- Governance policies

Human review becomes part of the Execution Proof.

---

# 20. RSN-LD-027 — Anomaly Escalation

Blueprints may define anomaly escalation gates.

When residual unexplained variance exceeds threshold:

The methodology may:

- Request additional evidence
- Trigger broader investigation
- Generate Novelty Signals
- Request human review

---

# 21. RSN-LD-028 — General Inquiry Blueprint

The Registry SHALL maintain a General Inquiry Blueprint.

**Purpose**:

Provide governed reasoning when no domain-specific methodology exists.

The General Inquiry Blueprint SHALL:

- Perform premise verification
- Gather evidence
- Generate hypotheses
- Test hypotheses
- Produce confidence scores

Outputs are explicitly marked lower confidence.

---

# 22. RSN-LD-029 — Methodology Competition

Multiple methodologies may exist for the same intent.

The KRM determines methodology selection.

The Constitution permits competition.

The Constitution does not mandate competition.

---

# 23. RSN-LD-030 — Methodology Lifecycle

Methodology states:

- Draft
- Active
- Deprecated
- Retired

Retired methodologies remain queryable.

Historical artifacts remain valid.

---

# 24. RSN-LD-031 — Registry Governance

Methodologies require governance approval before activation.

Approval requirements:

- Structural review
- Constitutional compliance review
- Output validation review
- Registry registration

---

# 25. Constitutional Outputs

RSN-002 produces:

- Blueprint Definitions
- Playbook Definitions
- Methodology Metadata
- Performance Artifacts
- Divergence Artifacts
- Governance Artifacts

RSN-002 does not produce intelligence.

RSN-002 governs how intelligence is produced.

---

# Ratification Statement

Reasoning remains governed by universal laws.

Methodologies remain governed by registries.

Blueprints remain reusable.

Execution remains replaceable.

Proof remains mandatory.

Truth remains superior to methodology.
