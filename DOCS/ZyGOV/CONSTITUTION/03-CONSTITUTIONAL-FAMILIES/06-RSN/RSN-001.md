# RSN-001 — Reasoning Constitution

###### Version: 1.0

**Status:** RATIFIED

**Authority:** Zyppi Constitutional Architecture

**Classification:** Constitutional Domain

---

# 1. Purpose

RSN-001 establishes the constitutional framework governing how Zyppi transforms evidence into intelligence.

RSN does not govern Reality.

RSN does not govern Projections.

RSN does not govern Experiences.

RSN governs the production of Intelligence.

The purpose of RSN is to ensure that all analytical, investigative, predictive, diagnostic, and recommendation-oriented capabilities execute through a governed, auditable, model-agnostic process.

---

# 2. Relationship to Other Constitutional Domains

## 2.1 Reality

Reality is governed by ZRM.

Reality remains the authoritative source of truth.

RSN SHALL NOT modify Reality.

---

## 2.2 Projection

Projections are governed by PRJ.

**PRJ answers:**

> "What is true?"

RSN consumes Projections and Reality Views as evidence.

---

## 2.3 Intelligence

`CL-16` defines Intelligence Artifacts.

RSN defines how Intelligence Artifacts are produced.

`CL-16` governs artifact structure.

RSN governs artifact generation.

---

## 2.4 Experience

EXP governs presentation.

RSN outputs Intelligence Artifacts.

EXP may consume Intelligence Artifacts when constructing Experience Manifests.

---

## 2.5 Touch

ZT governs Intent Signals.

A Touch may trigger Reasoning when the classified intent requires analytical processing.

---

## 2.6 SDK

SDK implementations consume manifests.

SDK implementations SHALL render Reasoning Artifacts without knowledge of the internal reasoning process.

---

# 3. Constitutional Definition of Reasoning

Reasoning is a governed, auditable process that assembles Evidence from the Reality Graph under a registered Blueprint, evaluates one or more Hypotheses against that Evidence, and produces an immutable Intelligence Artifact independent of the computational model used for inference.

**Reasoning consists of:**

1. Evidence Assembly
2. Blueprint Execution
3. Hypothesis Evaluation
4. Confidence Assessment
5. Intelligence Artifact Production

---

# 4. RSN-LD-001 — Reasoning as a Constitutional Domain

Reasoning SHALL be a constitutional domain within Zyppi.

Reasoning SHALL NOT be implemented as unmanaged application logic.

Reasoning SHALL operate as the constitutional production layer for `CL-16` Intelligence Artifacts.

---

# 5. RSN-LD-002 — Model Independence

No computational model SHALL be a constitutional asset.

Models are execution engines.

**Constitutional assets include:**

- Evidence
- Blueprints
- Knowledge Routing Matrix
- Reasoning Manifests
- Intelligence Artifacts

Models MAY be replaced without constitutional amendment.

---

# 6. RSN-LD-003 — Mandatory Reasoning Manifest

Every Reasoning operation SHALL produce a Reasoning Manifest.

Direct analytical responses generated without a Reasoning Manifest are constitutionally prohibited.

The Reasoning Manifest becomes the authoritative audit artifact for the operation.

---

# 7. RSN-LD-004 — Evidence Primacy

All Reasoning SHALL begin with Evidence.

Evidence SHALL originate exclusively from the Reality Graph.

**Permitted Evidence Sources:**

- CL-11 Events
- CL-09 Transactions
- CL-10 Outcomes
- PRJ Projections
- Authorized Reality Views
- Prior CL-16 Intelligence Artifacts as secondary evidence

Evidence SHALL NOT be fabricated.

Evidence derivation depth SHALL NOT exceed two levels.

Primary evidence SHALL always outweigh derived evidence.

---

# 8. RSN-LD-005 — Blueprint Registry

Blueprints are constitutional artifacts.

**A Blueprint defines:**

- Investigation methodology
- Evidence requirements
- Validation rules
- Hypothesis framework
- Confidence requirements
- Output schema

**Every Blueprint SHALL possess:**

- blueprint_id
- version
- status
- intent_scope
- domain_scope

Unregistered Blueprints SHALL NOT execute.

---

# 9. RSN-LD-006 — Playbook Governance

Playbooks are not constitutional artifacts.

Playbooks are organizational implementations of Blueprints.

**Playbooks MAY:**

- Specialize
- Extend
- Configure

Playbooks SHALL NOT weaken constitutional Blueprint requirements.

---

# 10. RSN-LD-007 — Knowledge Routing Matrix

The Knowledge Routing Matrix (KRM) SHALL govern Blueprint selection.

**Inputs:**

- Intent
- Domain
- Context

**Outputs:**

- Blueprint
- Optional Playbook
- Required Evidence Classes
- Authorized Tools

Models SHALL NOT self-select investigation pathways.

All routing SHALL occur through KRM.

---

# 11. RSN-LD-008 — Reasoning Does Not Modify Reality

Reasoning SHALL be **read-only**.

**Reasoning SHALL NOT:**

- Create Reality
- Modify Reality
- Execute Transactions
- Alter Identity
- Alter Assets

**Reasoning MAY generate:**

- Conclusions
- Predictions
- Recommendations
- Action Intents

Execution remains outside RSN.

---

# 12. RSN-LD-009 — Conditional Invocation

Reasoning is not universal middleware.

Reasoning SHALL activate only for analytical intents.

**Examples requiring RSN:**

- Analysis
- Investigation
- Diagnosis
- Prediction
- Recommendation
- Comparison
- Optimization

**Examples not requiring RSN:**

- Lookup
- Access
- Verification
- Direct Projection Retrieval

---

# 13. RSN-LD-010 — Copilot Independence

Copilot is not constitutional.

Copilot is an Experience consumer.

**The following consumers are constitutionally equivalent:**

- Copilot
- Dashboards
- SDK Applications
- Agents
- Executive Workspaces
- Automated Systems

RSN SHALL remain independent of any interface paradigm.

---

# 15. Reasoning Manifest Standard

Every Reasoning Manifest SHALL contain:

- manifest_id
- blueprint_id
- blueprint_version
- intent
- domains
- evidence_refs
- hypotheses
- findings
- conclusion
- confidence_score
- evidence_assembled_at
- generated_at
- model_reference
- reasoning_status

The manifest SHALL be immutable.

---

# 16. Blueprint Conflict Resolution

When multiple Blueprints produce conflicting conclusions:

- Both conclusions SHALL be preserved.
- Confidence SHALL be disclosed.
- No Blueprint may suppress another Blueprint.

Conflict is information.

Conflict is not an error.

---

# 17. Evidence Freshness

Every Reasoning Manifest SHALL declare:

- evidence_assembled_at

Blueprints MAY define:

- max_evidence_age

Manifests exceeding freshness thresholds SHALL be marked stale.

Stale manifests remain valid historical records.

---

# 18. Reasoning Atomicity

A single Reasoning operation SHALL execute under a single model version.

If execution is interrupted:

- Manifest status becomes abandoned.
- A new Reasoning operation SHALL be initiated.

Partial reasoning artifacts SHALL NOT be finalized.

---

# 19. Constitutional Outputs

RSN supports three output classes:

**Class A**

`Reasoning Manifest`

**Class B**

`Reasoning Manifest + Recommendations`

**Class C**

`Reasoning Manifest + Action Intent Proposal`

Action Intent execution requires authorization through constitutional execution domains.

RSN itself never executes actions.
