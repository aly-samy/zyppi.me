# Resolution

The Council hereby ratifies the constitutional scope, architecture, and governing principles of `RSN-003`.

`RSN-003` SHALL be known as:

> **«RSN-003 — Constitutional Attestation Framework»**

`RSN-003` establishes the universal constitutional framework governing all Attestations throughout Zyppi.

It does not govern Reasoning itself, Governance itself, or Identity itself.

Instead, it governs the constitutional proof that those governed processes complied with their respective constitutional requirements.

# Constitutional Primitive

An Attestation is a constitutional primitive.

It is never the source of truth.

It is cryptographic proof that a governed process complied with constitutional requirements.

Attestations certify compliance.

**They never replace:**

- Reality
- Reasoning
- Governance
- Identity
- Evidence

---

# Constitutional Principles

## RSN-003-CP-001 — Attestation Definition

An Attestation SHALL represent cryptographic proof that a governed constitutional process complied with applicable constitutional requirements.

Attestations SHALL certify process compliance only.

Attestations SHALL NEVER replace or become the source of truth.

---

## RSN-003-CP-002 — Attestation Necessity Principle

Constitutional Attestations SHALL ONLY exist where constitutional compliance cannot be independently established through:

- deterministic reproduction,
- intrinsic cryptographic integrity,
- or an existing constitutional governance mechanism.

Applying an Attestation to an operation already protected by one of the above constitutes Attestation Inflation and is constitutionally prohibited.

---

## RSN-003-CP-003 — Domain Isolation

RSN-003 SHALL govern Attestation mechanics independently of constitutional domains.

Each Attestation Domain SHALL evolve independently.

Adding, modifying, or deprecating one domain SHALL NOT alter another domain.

---

## RSN-003-CP-004 — Framework vs Artifact Separation

RSN-003 governs the Attestation Framework.

Individual Attestations are Attestation Artifacts implementing that framework.

The Framework defines constitutional mechanics.

Artifacts define domain-specific compliance content.

# Universal Attestation Contract

The Universal Contract SHALL remain completely domain-agnostic.

Every Attestation Artifact SHALL minimally contain:
````
attestation_id

attestation_type

domain

artifact_id

producer_id

produced_at

status

content_hash

schema_version
````
No domain-specific compliance fields SHALL exist within the Universal Contract.

Domain extensions SHALL define additional requirements.

# Ratification

The Council unanimously ratifies:

- RSN-003 SHALL be the Constitutional Attestation Framework.
- Attestation SHALL be a universal constitutional primitive.
- The Universal Contract SHALL remain domain-agnostic.
- Domain-specific compliance SHALL exist only within their respective domains.
- Version 1.0 SHALL fully define only the Reasoning Domain (ATT-R).
- All future Attestation Domains SHALL extend, never modify, the Universal Framework.
