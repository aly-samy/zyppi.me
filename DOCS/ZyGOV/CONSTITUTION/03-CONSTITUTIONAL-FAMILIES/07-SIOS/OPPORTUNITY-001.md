# Purpose


OPPORTUNITY-001 establishes the universal principles for evaluating where structural intervention can create enduring value.


It does not diagnose resistance.


It does not invent solutions.


It does not define products.


It evaluates whether a diagnosed Friction represents a meaningful opportunity for intervention.


This document forms the constitutional bridge between **Diagnosis** and **Execution**.

# Constitutional Definition


> An **Opportunity** is an evidence-supported evaluative conclusion that reducing diagnosed Friction within an identified System is likely to create enduring structural value.


Opportunity is descriptive of potential.


It is not a solution.


It is not a commitment.


It is not an implementation plan.


An Opportunity is not an idea.


An Opportunity is not a product.


An Opportunity is not a market.


An Opportunity is an evaluative conclusion supported by evidence.


---


# Constitutional Responsibility


OPPORTUNITY-001 answers one question:


> **"Is this Friction worth intervening in?"**


It does not answer:


- How should we solve it?
- What should we build?
- Who should buy it?
- How should it be marketed?


Those responsibilities belong to later Framework documents.


---


# First Principles


## Principle 1 — Opportunity Requires Friction


Without diagnosed Friction there is nothing to evaluate.


No Opportunity exists independently of Friction.


---


## Principle 2 — Opportunity Requires Systems


Interventions always occur within Systems.


Opportunities never exist independently of structural context.


---


## Principle 3 — Opportunity Requires Evidence


Every Opportunity must be supported by observable Evidence.


Assumptions are not Opportunities.


Opinions are not Opportunities.


---


## Principle 4 — Opportunity Is Evaluative


Opportunity evaluates.


It never prescribes.


It never designs.


It never executes.


---


## Principle 5 — Opportunity Is Resource Neutral


The existence of an Opportunity is independent of the organization evaluating it.


Whether Zyppi, another company, or no one can pursue it is irrelevant.


An Opportunity exists because Reality supports it—not because an actor can exploit it.


---


# Opportunity as a Delta


Every Opportunity represents an observable change between two System states.


```
Current State
(System experiencing diagnosed Friction)


        ↓


Evaluated State Transition


        ↓


Desired State
(System experiencing reduced Friction)


```


Every Opportunity must explicitly describe:

- Current State
- Desired State
- Expected reduction in Friction


Without a defined state transition, no Opportunity exists.


---




# Reference State


Every Opportunity evaluation begins from the System's Reference State as established by SYSTEMS-001 and the diagnosed deviation described by FRICTION-001.


Opportunity evaluates whether movement toward a more desirable structural state represents enduring value.


It does not prescribe how that movement should occur.


---


# Evaluation Dimensions


Every Opportunity is evaluated across the following constitutional dimensions.


## Leverage


The degree to which a small intervention produces disproportionate structural change.


---


## Impact


The expected reduction in diagnosed Friction.


Impact measures structural change.


It does not measure commercial success.


---


## Constraint Sensitivity


The degree to which realization depends upon external constraints.


Constraint Sensitivity evaluates dependency on technology, regulation, infrastructure, economics, or other changing conditions.


It does not determine whether an Opportunity exists.


---


## Reach


The breadth of Systems potentially benefiting from intervention.


---


## Repeatability


The likelihood that value can be reproduced consistently across similar Systems.


---


## Durability


The expected persistence of value after intervention.


Durability evaluates structural longevity.


---


## Constitutional Alignment


The degree to which intervention respects constitutional principles, Reality, and enduring Strategic Forces.


It evaluates alignment with Reality—not with company strategy.


---


## Optionality


The degree to which intervention creates additional future possibilities.


---


## Risk


The observable uncertainty surrounding the evaluation.


Risk describes uncertainty.


It does not prioritize decisions.


---


## Cost


The observable resources required for intervention.


Cost may include:

- Time
- Capital
- Attention
- Complexity
- Opportunity Cost


Cost is descriptive.


It does not determine whether intervention should occur.


---


## Timing


The observable temporal window during which value may exist.


Some Opportunities are enduring.


Others are temporary.


Timing describes temporal context.


---


## Urgency


The observable degree to which delaying intervention changes expected value.


Urgency is descriptive.


It is not prioritization.


---


## Confidence


The degree to which available Evidence supports the evaluation.


Confidence reflects the quality and completeness of Evidence.


It does not measure the desirability of the Opportunity.

---

---


## Step 3

- Constitutional Alignment
- Optionality
- Risk
- Cost

---

---

# Evaluation Discipline


OPPORTUNITY intentionally excludes:


- solution design
- product definition
- technology selection
- pricing
- commercial positioning
- buyer identification
- implementation planning


Those responsibilities belong exclusively to downstream Framework documents.


Maintaining this separation preserves constitutional integrity.


---


# Fundamental vs Epoch Opportunities


Not every Opportunity possesses the same temporal character.


## Fundamental Opportunity


Exists because of enduring structural properties of Reality.


Independent of current technology.


Likely to remain valid across generations.


---


## Epoch Opportunity


Exists because of temporary technological, economic, political, or cultural conditions.


May disappear as constraints change.

# Relationship to FRICTION


FRICTION asks:


> Where does resistance exist?


OPPORTUNITY asks:


> Does reducing this resistance create enduring value?


FRICTION diagnoses.


OPPORTUNITY evaluates.


Neither prescribes solutions.


---


# Relationship to CATEGORY


CATEGORY never discovers Opportunities.


CATEGORY classifies evaluated Opportunities into meaningful market categories.


CATEGORY depends entirely upon OPPORTUNITY.


The dependency is one-way.


---


# Relationship to SYSTEMS


SYSTEMS defines the structural organization within which Opportunities exist.


OPPORTUNITY neither redefines Systems nor alters their structure.


It evaluates whether changing the behavior or organization of an identified System may reduce diagnosed Friction.


Every Opportunity must reference a constitutionally valid System.


---


# Relationship to FORCES


Strategic Forces continuously reshape Systems.


Some Forces generate persistent Friction.


OPPORTUNITY evaluates whether responding to those Forces through structural intervention creates enduring value.


FORCES explain why change occurs.


OPPORTUNITY evaluates whether responding to that change is justified.

# Constitutional Tests


Every Opportunity must satisfy the following tests.


## Reality Test


Is the Opportunity grounded in observable Reality?


---


## Evidence Test


Can the evaluation be supported by Evidence?


---


## Friction Test


Does diagnosed Friction exist?


---


## System Test


Is the affected System clearly identified?


---


## Explainability Test


Can another analyst reproduce the evaluation?


---


## Independence Test


Would the Opportunity exist regardless of who evaluates it?


---


## Resource Neutrality Test


Does the Opportunity remain valid even if no current organization can pursue it?


---


## Delta Test


Does the Opportunity clearly define:

- Current State
- Future State
- Expected Friction Reduction


---


## Evaluation Integrity Test


Does the evaluation remain independent of any proposed solution, organization, technology, or commercial interest?


If removing the evaluator changes the existence of the Opportunity, the evaluation is constitutionally invalid.


---


## Boundary Test


Does the document evaluate without prescribing solutions?


---


## Civilization Test


Would another civilization recognize the Opportunity as structural rather than cultural?


---


## 100-Year Test


Will the Opportunity remain conceptually valid regardless of future technologies?
