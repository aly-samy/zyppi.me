# 0. Purpose

Market entry is only the beginning.

A participant may discover Zyppi.

A user may create an identity.

A developer may call an API.

A company may deploy a carrier.

A customer may begin paying.

None of these events alone proves that Zyppi has created durable market value.

The Marketing Constitution therefore requires a doctrine governing what happens **after entry**.

`Marketing-004` establishes how Zyppi distinguishes:

- participation;
- adoption;
- activation;
- repeated use;
- experienced value;
- economic leverage;
- monetization;
- retention;
- expansion;
- compounding.

It also governs:

- how users may deepen through Zyppi over time;
- why free participation can remain strategically valuable;
- why not every participant must become a high-value customer;
- how buyer identity may change across maturity;
- why monetization should rise with leverage rather than arbitrary feature gates;
- why deeper usage must remain connected to the North Star;
- how horizontal, vertical, and temporal compounding reinforce one another.

The central rule is:

> **Zyppi SHALL not confuse entry with adoption, adoption with activation, activation with value, value with revenue, or revenue with durable market power.**

# 2. Participation Definition

**Participation** exists when a real external actor begins using, integrating with, deploying, or depending upon some Zyppi capability.

Participation may include:

- identity creation;
- API use;
- SDK use;
- MCP use;
- carrier deployment;
- resolver use;
- utility use;
- integration;
- policy configuration;
- receipt consumption;
- ecosystem interaction.

Participation may be:

- free;
- paid;
- subsidized;
- partner-mediated.

Participation is strategically important because it creates the possibility of future compounding.

Participation alone is not proof of meaningful value.

---

# 3. Adoption Definition

**Adoption** exists when participation becomes intentional and operational rather than merely exploratory.

Signals may include:

- completed setup;
- deployed use;
- repeated API calls;
- active identities;
- live configuration;
- production traffic;
- repeated utility use;
- embedded integration.

Adoption answers:

> **Did the participant actually put Zyppi into use?**

A registration is not necessarily adoption.

An account creation is not necessarily adoption.

A single test call is not necessarily adoption.

---

# 4. Activation Definition

**Activation** exists when the participant experiences the first meaningful Zyppi outcome relevant to the reason they adopted.

Activation is product-specific.

Examples might include:

- first successful resolution;
- first persisted interaction history;
- first policy-controlled outcome;
- first reusable state source;
- first verified Evidence result;
- first governed capability decision;
- first Receipt useful outside Zyppi.

Activation SHALL be defined by value, not by arbitrary UI completion.

---

# 5. Repeated Use Definition

**Repeated Use** exists when the participant returns to Zyppi or continues to depend upon Zyppi after initial activation.

Repeated use indicates that:

- value may persist;
- Zyppi may be entering an operational routine;
- accumulated context may begin to matter.

Repeated use is stronger than activation.

It is still not necessarily retention.

---

# 6. Experienced Value Definition

**Experienced Value** exists when a participant obtains an outcome that improves a real condition such as:

- lower effort;
- lower cost;
- faster execution;
- lower risk;
- better visibility;
- improved reliability;
- improved trust;
- improved control;
- improved interoperability;
- easier compliance;
- better customer experience;
- more effective automation.

Value SHALL be tied to the participant's actual job.

Traffic alone is not value.

---

# 7. Economic Leverage Definition

**Economic Leverage** exists when Zyppi creates a benefit whose importance or consequence exceeds mere technical operation.

Examples may include:

- reducing human work;
- reducing integration burden;
- preventing operational error;
- enabling trusted decisioning;
- reducing compliance risk;
- enabling governed automation;
- increasing operational throughput;
- creating reusable infrastructure across many workflows.

Economic leverage is the preferred basis for monetization.

The deeper the leverage, the stronger the justification for paid consumption.

---

# 8. Monetization Definition

**Monetization** is the commercial capture of a fair portion of the economic value created or consumed through Zyppi.

Monetization SHALL remain subordinate to `ZEA-001`.

Marketing may define:

- packaging;
- allowances;
- pricing presentation;
- billing experience;
- upgrade messaging.

Marketing SHALL NOT redefine:

- ECU;
- constitutional economic laws;
- open-standard protections;
- adoption-before-extraction doctrine.

---

# 9. Retention Definition

**Retention** exists when participants continue using Zyppi over an economically meaningful period because ongoing value persists.

Retention may be measured through:

- repeated activity;
- active deployments;
- recurring ECU consumption;
- recurring workflow use;
- continuing integration;
- continued subscription or contract;
- retained identities;
- retained policy use.

Retention SHALL distinguish:

```text
still registered
```

from:

```text
still receiving value
```

# 10. Expansion Definition

**Expansion** exists when an existing participant increases the depth or breadth of Zyppi use.

Examples:

- more applications;

- more retained history;
- additional integrations;
- Trust activation;
- governed execution;

- additional domains.

Expansion SHOULD correspond to increased value.

Expansion should not be manufactured through artificial restrictions.

# 19A. Capability Progression Is Not an Assumed Buyer Journey

The capability and consequence ladders in this doctrine describe **Zyppi platform depth**.

They SHALL NOT be interpreted as a forecast that one individual user, one buying center, or one department will naturally purchase every deeper layer.

For example:

```text
Developer adopts Access
```

does not imply:

```text
Developer will later purchase Enterprise Governance
```

A deeper capability may require:

- a different economic buyer;
- a new internal champion;
- a separate acquisition path;
- different evidence;
- different procurement;
- a partner or enterprise sales motion.

Any strategic plan that relies upon cross-layer economic expansion SHOULD explicitly identify the **Buyer Bridge**: how value and evidence move from the current user or team to the next relevant buying center.

If no credible Buyer Bridge exists, cross-layer expansion SHALL remain a hypothesis rather than an assumed funnel.

> **Platform depth may be continuous even when the buyer journey is discontinuous.**

# 40. Vertical Without Horizontal

# 85. Closing Principle

Zyppi does not win because someone creates an account.

It does not win because an identity exists.

It does not win because traffic increases.

It does not win because a customer pays once.

It wins progressively when:

```text
Participation becomes use.
Use becomes value.
Value becomes leverage.
Leverage becomes durable economics.
Durable economics compounds into dependence.
Dependence, when legitimately earned, becomes market power.
```

The Marketing branch therefore adopts:

> **Acquire participation cheaply. Activate real value quickly. Deepen only when consequence justifies it. Monetize increasing leverage fairly. Retain through usefulness. Expand through compounding.**

And:

> **Do not force every participant upward. Build a system in which the participants who need deeper Zyppi can move upward naturally, while the entire ecosystem strengthens through legitimate use.**
