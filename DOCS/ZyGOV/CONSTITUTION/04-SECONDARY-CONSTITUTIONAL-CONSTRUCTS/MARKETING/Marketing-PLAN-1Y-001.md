# 0. Executive Strategic Mandate

For the next twelve months, Zyppi SHALL focus operationally on establishing a real market position in Commerce through GS1 / GS1 Digital Link infrastructure while preserving the universal Reality Sync North Star.

The strategic job of this plan is not to prove that Zyppi can become universal infrastructure.

The job is to establish enough real market evidence that Zyppi can answer:

1. Can a standards-correct, low-friction, production-capable GS1 doorway acquire real external users?
2. Can those users reach meaningful production activation?
3. Can enough of them retain and create durable product / integration footprint?
4. Can Zyppi establish at least one repeatable distribution path?
5. Can value deepen from foundational resolution toward policy, history, Trust, workflow, or governed execution without taxing the standards doorway?
6. Can the entry user create a credible Buyer Bridge toward a deeper economic buyer?
7. Can Zyppi generate enough low-touch bootstrap cash to reduce survival pressure without corrupting the core market-entry strategy?
8. Does the resulting evidence justify the 3-year growth thesis?

The central 1-year principle is:

> **Establish one market honestly before pretending to own many. Preserve universal architecture underneath, but spend current attention on the evidence needed to make Commerce real.**

# 4. Current External Market Window

## 4.1 GS1 2D Transition

Current official GS1 material continues to frame the **end of 2027** as the industry goal for retail POS systems to be capable of reading and processing defined GS1-conformant 2D barcodes alongside existing linear barcodes.

GS1 explicitly describes the transition as:

- gradual;
- varying by region and industry;
- involving coexistence of 1D and 2D barcodes during transition;
- already active through implementation and pilots.

**Evidence Grade:** E2+  
**Source Class:** Primary / standards authority

Strategic implication:

> The next twelve months are a credible market-timing window for organizations preparing 2D product interaction infrastructure, but the 2027 transition does **not** mandate the purchase of Zyppi.

---

## 4.2 GS1 Digital Link Continues to Evolve

As of this plan baseline:

- GS1 Digital Link current standard: **1.1.4**, modified January 2026.
- GS1 Digital Link URI Syntax current version: **1.7.0**, ratified August 2026.
- GS1-Conformant Resolver Standard current version: **1.2.1**, ratified August 2026.

**Evidence Grade:** E2+  
**Source Class:** Primary / standards authority

Strategic implication:

> Standards correctness and maintenance remain a legitimate operational Job, especially for implementers who do not want to own the standards lifecycle internally.

This remains an entry value proposition.

It is not by itself a durable moat.

# 4.3 Commodity Resolver Competition — Explicit Baseline

The foundational resolver market already contains serious free alternatives.

Current external evidence includes:

- GS1 Global Office operates a free resolver service;
- GS1 publishes free developer resources;
- a free open-source GS1 Digital Link Resolver Community Edition exists, although its repository explicitly warns that it is community-maintained and should not be assumed fully conformant;
- GS1 maintains a resolver conformance test suite.

**Evidence Grade:** E2+  
**Strategic conclusion:** **PRESERVE**

Therefore the Year-1 proposition SHALL NOT be:

> "Use Zyppi because Zyppi has a GS1 resolver."

The actual entry challenge is:

> **Can Zyppi make production adoption, standards maintenance, continuity, contextual control, and progressive depth sufficiently easier and more coherent that a technical implementer rationally chooses Zyppi over free/self-hosted/incumbent alternatives?**

The resolver itself remains acquisition infrastructure.

It does not carry differentiation alone.

## H2 — Crowded Low-End GS1 Wedge vs Trojan-Horse Acquisition Role

**Historical source:** `GS1-TH-001B — Low-End Competitive Product Teardown v1.1`, dated **2 September 2026**.

That artifact reached two simultaneous conclusions:

1. the low-end GS1 market is dense, price-compressed, and already serves the simplistic small-brand resolver / generator proposition;
2. the **GS1 Trojan-Horse survives only with material rework** and a different entry-product / distribution shape.

Its formal decision was:

> **GS1-TH-001B — PASS WITH MATERIAL REWORK.**

It explicitly rejected the formulation:

> "A more generous, easier GS1 Digital Link generator/resolver for small brands and packaging agencies."

and preserved a narrower acquisition hypothesis around one focused entry surface implemented on a deeper reusable Zyppi core.

Therefore:

> **The earlier crowded-entry finding was not overturned. It was incorporated.**

The Year-1 plan does **not** claim that the free GS1 doorway is uncrowded or differentiated.

It deliberately tests whether a low-moat Layer-1 standards surface can still function as acquisition infrastructure when paired with:

- unusually low adoption friction;
- production continuity;
- standards maintenance;
- portability;
- a durable persistent identity / integration footprint;
- progressive access to deeper Zyppi capability without re-platforming.

**Disposition:** **PROVISIONAL PASS AS ACQUISITION ARCHITECTURE; CROWDED-ENTRY RISK REMAINS ACTIVE.**

`GS1-TH-001B v1.1` also weakened the idea that developer API / MCP availability itself is open whitespace. Therefore the current technical-implementer beachhead is a **testable adoption hypothesis**, not a claim that developers are unserved.

## H3 — Operational Reality Signals as Standalone Paid Starter

**Historical source:** `ZYPPI-GS1-MARKET-ENTRY-STRATEGY-001 v1.0`, dated **3 September 2026**, especially its section **“Signals as standalone Starter”** and subsequent Interaction Intelligence analysis.

# 14A. Runway Protection Gate — Subsidy Ceiling

Free foundational participation is strategic.

It is not permission to ignore existential cash constraints.

This plan establishes a provisional **Runway Protection Gate (RP-1)**.

RP-1 triggers a mandatory economic review when both are true:

1. projected unrestricted cash runway falls below **6 months**; and
2. the fully loaded variable cost of the foundational free Commerce surface either:
   - exceeds approximately **15% of monthly cash burn for two consecutive months**, or
   - is projected to remove more than **one month of runway over the following 90 days** if current growth continues.

These are **plan-level thresholds**, not constitutional pricing law.

When RP-1 triggers, actions SHALL be considered in the following order:

```text
1. remove / reduce exceptional free subsidies
2. tighten abuse and fair-use controls
3. reduce costly retention / history / analytics allowances
4. charge for exceptional capacity, infrastructure, support, or additional domains
5. pause new high-cost free capabilities
6. accelerate BUE / funding response
7. only if existentially necessary, re-rate new-account foundational allowances
```

The response SHALL preserve, where technically and financially possible:

- already deployed physical-reference continuity;
- minimum standards-correct resolution;
- portability;
- no arbitrary identity hostage.

Survival may constrain generosity.

It SHALL NOT silently mutate foundational standards participation into a scan tax or extortion mechanism.

# 16. Commerce Activation — Locked Working Definition

To prevent Account-Created or QR-Generated vanity metrics, this plan establishes a **working Commerce Activation event** for the first market cycle.

## Production Activation — CA-1

A qualified external organization reaches **CA-1** when:

1. it uses a non-internal Zyppi production surface;
2. at least one GS1 Digital Link / identity relevant to its legitimate use case is configured or invoked;
3. Zyppi successfully returns the intended standards-correct production resolution / resource outcome;
4. the interaction is not merely an internal demo, synthetic test, or Zyppi-owned sample.

This is the **working 1-year activation definition**.

It MAY be re-rated when real usage shows a better behavioral definition.

# 16A. Repeated Production Activation — CA-2

`CA-1` means **first real production value**.

It is not retained adoption.

A qualified organization reaches **CA-2 — Repeated Production Activation** when, after CA-1, it demonstrates meaningful independent production use again.

The initial operating definition is:

- meaningful production use on at least **3 distinct days within 30 days**; or
- at least **2 distinct natural operational cycles** where the Job's cadence is slower.

This threshold MAY be re-rated after the first cohorts reveal the real usage pattern.

# 20. Buyer Bridge Artifact — Reworked Hypothesis

The original operational-history artifact is insufficient if it merely says:

> "The API worked."

The Year-1 Bridge Artifact is therefore redefined as an:

# **Operational Risk & Continuity Brief**

Its job is to translate technical production use into consequences a deeper organizational buyer can understand.

## 20.1 Technical Basis

The brief MAY show real supported observations such as:

- production references under management;
- resolver / destination continuity;
- fallback events;
- destination failures detected;
- standards-version changes relevant to the integration;
- routing / policy changes applied without replacing the persistent identity;
- observed deviations from configured expectation;
- operational incidents and recovery history.

## 20.2 Business Translation

Where the evidence supports it, the artifact SHOULD translate the technical event into a business-relevant exposure such as:

- active physical references affected;
- customer / service journey at risk;
- operational change that would otherwise require intervention;
- continuity risk;
- compliance / standards-maintenance work surfaced;
- manual work actually avoided where measured.

It SHALL NOT claim:

- "loss prevented";
- "counterfeit stopped";
- "compliance guaranteed";
- "cost saved";

unless the causal / evidentiary basis supports that exact claim.

## 20.3 Bridge Trigger — BT-1

The Buyer Bridge SHALL have a forcing mechanism.

A **BT-1** trigger occurs when any of the following happens:

1. the organization reaches CA-R / a meaningful retained-production interval;
2. a material continuity / fallback / destination incident occurs;
3. a standards change materially affects the active integration;
4. an operational Reality Signal is generated with enough quality to warrant review;
5. the organization makes a meaningful destination / policy change around an active persistent identity.

At BT-1, Zyppi SHOULD generate or update the Operational Risk & Continuity Brief and create a low-friction opportunity for the entry user to:

- share it internally;
- identify the operations / IT / compliance owner;
- invite the appropriate deeper buyer into evaluation.

This does not guarantee enterprise conversion.

It makes the Buyer Bridge testable.

## 20.4 Incident Remediation Before Commercial Bridge

Where BT-1 is triggered by:

- a continuity failure;
- fallback incident;
- destination failure;
- service degradation;
- another event for which Zyppi has operational responsibility;

Zyppi SHALL sequence the interaction as:

```text
1. Detect
2. Remediate / contain
3. Communicate the incident honestly
4. Establish the known cause and current state
5. Restore the foundational continuity obligation where applicable
6. Only then, in a separate follow-up, evaluate whether the incident reveals a legitimate deeper Job
```

The incident itself SHALL NOT be used as a fear-based upsell or as:

> "Pay Zyppi to fix what the free layer just broke."

If the incident was caused by Zyppi failure, the Operational Risk & Continuity Brief SHALL distinguish:

- Zyppi-caused failure;
- customer configuration issue;
- external destination failure;
- standards / dependency change;
- unknown cause.

Only evidence-supported deeper value may be presented afterward.

The foundational continuity obligation remains a trust obligation, not a pricing tactic.
