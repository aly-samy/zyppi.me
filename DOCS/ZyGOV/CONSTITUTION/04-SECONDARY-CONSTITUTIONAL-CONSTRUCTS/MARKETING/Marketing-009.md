# 0. Purpose

A strong product without distribution is not a market strategy.

A differentiated system that buyers never discover has no market power.

A developer API with excellent documentation but no discovery path may remain unused.

A physical reference may become a powerful acquisition surface.

An integration may become a permanent channel.

A partner may become a distributor—or may become a dependency that controls the customer relationship.

Therefore distribution must be governed deliberately.

`Marketing-009` establishes the constitutional doctrine governing:

- direct distribution;
- self-service distribution;
- developer distribution;
- partner distribution;
- embedded distribution;
- integration-led distribution;
- physical distribution;
- marketplaces;
- standards ecosystems;
- sales-led motion;
- channel economics;
- channel dependency;
- acquisition loops;
- activation through distribution;
- distribution compounding;
- channel evidence;
- future-domain reuse.

The central rule is:

> **Distribution is not merely promotion. Distribution is the system by which useful Zyppi capability reaches the right actor with enough context, trust, and low friction to become real adoption.**

---

# 1. Governing Principle — Distribution Is Architecture

The Marketing branch adopts:

> **Distribution is architecture.**

A distribution channel can become structurally embedded in:

- APIs;
- SDKs;
- MCP;
- package managers;
- integrations;
- partner platforms;
- physical carriers;
- standards workflows;
- marketplaces;
- procurement channels.

The preferred distribution architecture becomes stronger when it is reusable and persistent.

---

# 2. Distribution Is Not Awareness

The Marketing branch distinguishes:

```text
Awareness
≠
Discovery
≠
Acquisition
≠
Activation
≠
Retention
```

A campaign may generate awareness.

A search result may create discovery.

A signup may create acquisition.

Only meaningful first value creates activation.

Distribution quality SHALL be judged by the progression it produces, not merely impressions or visits.

---

# 3. Distribution Surface Definition

A **Distribution Surface** is any persistent or repeatable mechanism through which a qualified participant may encounter, adopt, invoke, or embed Zyppi.

Examples include:

- public API;
- SDK;
- MCP server;
- package;
- integration;
- plugin;
- partner platform;
- QR / physical carrier;
- public reference;
- marketplace listing;
- standards implementation;
- documentation;
- developer content.

A Distribution Surface may also be a product surface.

---

# 4. Channel Definition

A **Channel** is the pathway through which a target actor moves from discovery toward adoption.

Examples:

- organic search;
- developer ecosystem;
- direct sales;
- partner referral;
- system integrator;
- marketplace;
- physical packaging;
- agency;
- standards community.

The channel and the Distribution Surface may be different.

---

# 5. Distribution Loop Definition

A **Distribution Loop** exists when use of Zyppi creates new opportunities for others to discover or adopt Zyppi.

Example:

```text
Developer integrates Zyppi
        ↓
Integration is shipped
        ↓
New users encounter Zyppi capability
        ↓
Some become Zyppi participants
```

A theoretical loop is not a real growth loop until the final adoption step occurs.

---

# 6. Constitutional Law — Channel Must Match Buyer

Distribution SHALL follow `Marketing-008`.

A channel is valid only if it reaches the actor relevant to the Job-To-Be-Done.

Examples:

```text
Developer buyer
→ developer search / docs / package ecosystem

Operations buyer
→ partner / workflow integration / direct outreach

Enterprise governance buyer
→ direct sales / integrator / institutional channel
```

A high-reach channel that reaches the wrong actor may be strategically weak.

---

# 7. Constitutional Law — Distribution Must Reduce Friction

A preferred distribution path SHOULD reduce one or more of:

- discovery friction;
- setup friction;
- technical integration;
- procurement;
- trust;
- migration;
- education.

The easier it is to move from discovery to first value, the stronger the channel.

---

# 8. Constitutional Law — Distribution Should Compound

A strong channel SHOULD create durable distribution assets such as:

- installed integration;
- developer dependency;
- physical reference;
- partner embed;
- marketplace presence;
- public artifact;
- recurring referral.

One-time advertising attention is less durable than embedded distribution.

---

# 9. Constitutional Law — No Single Channel Dependency by Default

A market program SHOULD avoid unnecessary dependence on one external channel when:

- the channel can change terms;
- the channel controls customer access;
- the channel can copy or replace Zyppi;
- the channel captures most economics;
- the channel can terminate distribution.

Single-channel concentration may be accepted where strategically justified.

It SHALL remain visible as a risk.

# 10. Distribution Modes

The Marketing branch recognizes several general distribution modes.

## 10.1 Product-Led

The product itself creates discovery and adoption.

## 10.2 Developer-Led

Developers discover and integrate Zyppi directly.

## 10.3 Partner-Led

Third parties distribute or embed Zyppi.

## 10.4 Sales-Led

Human sales activity drives adoption.

## 10.5 Embedded

Zyppi is built into another product or workflow.

## 10.6 Physical

A real-world carrier or object creates the interaction surface.

## 10.7 Marketplace-Led

A platform marketplace creates discovery and procurement.

A program may combine several modes.

# 11. Product-Led Distribution

Product-led distribution is preferred where:

- time to value is short;
- adoption is self-service;
- price is low or transparent;
- buying center is simple;
- integration is lightweight.

A product-led entry may reduce scarce human-resource consumption during Baby.

A self-service product may still require:

- documentation;
- search visibility;
- examples;
- partner surfaces;
- templates.

SDK availability MAY materially improve developer distribution.

# 27. Integration Is Not Distribution by Default

An integration only becomes distribution when it creates:

- activation;
- referral;

# 28. Integration Distribution Test

Ask:

1. Who discovers Zyppi through the integration?
2. What job are they doing?
3. What action activates Zyppi?
4. Does the partner have incentive to promote it?
5. Who owns the customer relationship?
6. What happens if the partner changes terms?

---

# 42A. Physical Deployment Gate

Before Zyppi actively encourages **irreversible or material-scale physical deployment** of a carrier or reference, the foundational continuity path SHALL be technically verified to the level appropriate for that deployment.

At minimum, the applicable program SHOULD verify that:

- the persistent identity/reference does not depend upon a discretionary paid feature merely to remain resolvable;
- an expired budget, lapsed subscription, or unavailable higher-order capability cannot silently strand the physical reference where a safe foundational fallback is constitutionally possible;
- fallback and backward-compatibility behavior are tested rather than merely documented;
- the commercial promise made at print/deployment time is consistent with `Marketing-005` and `Marketing-006`.

The required validation depth SHOULD increase with the irreversibility and scale of deployment.

The governing rule is:

> **Do not ask Reality to carry a durable Zyppi reference until Zyppi can honor the durable continuity promise attached to it.**

# 53. Time to First Value

A channel that creates traffic but requires weeks of setup may be weaker than a smaller channel that activates in minutes.

Distribution should be judged through activation.

- activation rate;

- expansion;
- compounding.

# 116. Closing Principle

A platform does not become infrastructure because it calls itself infrastructure.

It becomes infrastructure when useful capability becomes easy to discover, easy to adopt, repeatedly embedded, and increasingly difficult to avoid because ecosystems begin depending upon it.

Distribution therefore begins long before advertising.

It begins in:

```text
the API
the SDK
the integration
the partner
the physical reference
the marketplace
the workflow
the developer experience
```

The Marketing branch therefore adopts:

> **Build distribution into the product. Reach the buyer through the channel they already use. Reduce friction to first value. Prefer embedded and repeatable channels over temporary attention. Measure activation, not traffic. Let every successful integration, reference, and participant increase future distribution capacity where Reality supports it.**

And:

> **The best distribution channel is not the one that creates the most visibility. It is the one that repeatedly delivers the right participant into real Zyppi value at an economically sustainable cost.**
