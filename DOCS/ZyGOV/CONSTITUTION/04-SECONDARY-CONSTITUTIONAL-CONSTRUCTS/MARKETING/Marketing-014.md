# 0. Purpose

Zyppi is constitutionally universal.

Its market entry is not.

This creates a strategic tension.

If Zyppi stays forever inside its first wedge, the North Star collapses into a vertical product.

If Zyppi expands too early, before the first market is understood, the company fragments into many underdeveloped products and domains.

Therefore domain expansion requires constitutional discipline.

`Marketing-014` establishes the doctrine governing:

- when Zyppi may expand from one application to another;
- when it may expand from one market segment to another;
- when it may enter a genuinely new domain;
- what must be reused from the constitutional platform;
- what evidence grants permission to expand;
- how to distinguish architecture option value from market validation;
- how to prevent domain-specific semantics from contaminating the universal core;
- how to replicate distribution, buyer, economic, and operational learning;
- how to avoid premature multi-domain complexity;
- how to determine whether the platform is actually becoming universal.

The central rule is:

> **Architecture gives Zyppi the option to enter another domain. Only market evidence gives Zyppi permission to spend serious resources doing it.**

---

# 1. Governing Expansion Hierarchy

The Marketing branch distinguishes:

```text
APPLICATION EXPANSION
        ↓
SEGMENT EXPANSION
        ↓
MARKET EXPANSION
        ↓
DOMAIN EXPANSION
        ↓
CROSS-DOMAIN PLATFORM
        ↓
ECOSYSTEM / PROTOCOL-LIKE INFRASTRUCTURE
```

These are not equivalent achievements.

Each requires different evidence.

---

# 2. Application Definition

An **Application** is a specific market expression of Zyppi capability used to solve a particular Job or workflow.

Examples may include:

- GS1 Digital Link resolution;
- Digital Product Passport-related workflows;
- warranty-related product interactions;
- product-service routing.

Several applications may exist inside one broader domain.

---

# 3. Domain Definition

A **Domain** is a sufficiently distinct field of Reality, market behavior, terminology, authority, Evidence, buyer logic, and operational consequence that it requires its own market expression and domain mapping.

A domain is broader than one product feature or application.

Examples might eventually include:

- Commerce;
- property;
- mobility;
- infrastructure;
- institutional workflows.

These examples are illustrative.

Ratification SHALL NOT authorize entry into any particular future domain.

---

# 3A. Domain Is a Governed Classification

The word **Domain** carries strategic and constitutional meaning.

An initiative SHALL NOT obtain additional resources, expansion privilege, architectural exception, or strategic prestige merely by being labeled a new domain.

Before an initiative is treated as P2 Experiment or above under this doctrine, the responsible program SHOULD classify it explicitly as one of:

- Application;
- Segment expansion;
- Market expansion;
- Domain expansion.

The classification SHOULD state the reason in a lightweight record.

Misclassification SHALL NOT be used to bypass the expansion gates.

> **Vocabulary does not grant permission. Classification must follow Reality.**

# 4. Market Definition

A **Market** is the commercial environment in which a specific buyer, segment, Job, alternative, distribution system, and economic model interact.

One domain may contain many markets.

One market may involve several applications.

---

# 5. Constitutional Law — Entry Domain Is Not Permanent Identity

Commerce and GS1 may serve as the establishing market and first proving application.

They SHALL NOT constitutionally define:

- Zyppi's ultimate domain;
- all future buyers;
- all future evidence models;
- all future products;
- all future pricing;
- all future distribution.

This inherits `Marketing-000`.

---

# 6. Constitutional Law — Universality Does Not Grant Automatic Expansion

A universal architecture does not prove:

- buyers exist in another domain;
- the problem is urgent;
- Zyppi can distribute there;
- the economic model works;
- the domain can be entered without fragmentation.

Therefore:

> **Universal architecture creates strategic optionality, not automatic market entitlement.**

---

# 7. Constitutional Law — Expansion Must Be Earned

A new domain SHOULD be entered materially only when sufficient evidence supports:

- real problem;
- accessible buyer;
- clear Job;
- reusable Zyppi capabilities;
- plausible distribution;
- sustainable economics;
- acceptable human burden;
- limited constitutional fragmentation.

The required evidence strength increases with commitment.

---

# 8. Expansion Classes

The Marketing branch recognizes four primary expansion classes.

## E-Class 1 — Same Application, New Segment

The product remains substantially the same.

The buyer/segment changes.

Example:

```text
same core resolver
→ different manufacturer segment
```

---

## E-Class 2 — New Application, Same Domain

New Job or workflow.

Underlying domain remains the same.

Example:

```text
Commerce:
GS1 resolution
→ another product-interaction application
```

---

## E-Class 3 — Adjacent Domain

A new domain with substantial reuse of:

- primitives;
- semantics;
- buyer/distribution logic;
- infrastructure.

---

## E-Class 4 — Distinct Domain

A meaningfully different Reality domain requiring new:

- terminology;
- buyer architecture;
- Evidence/Authority patterns;
- distribution;
- market logic.

The higher the class, the stronger the required evidence and governance.

---

# 9. Application Proliferation ≠ Domain Expansion

Zyppi may support many applications inside Commerce.

This does not prove multi-domain platform replication.

The Marketing branch rejects:

```text
many applications
=
many domains
```

---

# 10. Bootstrap Utility Proliferation ≠ Domain Expansion

A webhook, state, link, or monitoring utility may serve a different buyer.

That does not automatically constitute a new Reality domain.

Bootstrap utilities remain governed by `Marketing-010`.

---

# 11. Customer Customization ≠ Domain Expansion

A bespoke enterprise workflow is not automatically a new domain.

Custom work MAY reveal a future domain hypothesis.

It SHALL not be constitutionalized merely because one customer requested it.

---

# 12. New Vertical ≠ New Platform

Entering another vertical industry does not automatically prove that Zyppi's universal platform replicated.

The test is whether the constitutional capability system remains coherent and reusable.

---

# 13. Replication Definition

**Market Replication** occurs when Zyppi can enter another market using substantially reusable:

- platform primitives;
- operational infrastructure;
- economic architecture;
- developer interfaces;
- governance;
- learning;

while adapting the external expression to the new domain.

Replication means:

> **Reuse the engine. Adapt the language, evidence, buyer, and market expression.**

---

# 14. Replication Is Not Copy-Paste

A new domain SHALL not mechanically inherit:

- Commerce terminology;
- GS1 assumptions;
- product-object semantics;
- current buyer;
- current pricing;
- current channel;
- current activation metric.

Replication preserves constitutional capability.

It adapts domain expression.

---

# 15. Canonical Core vs Domain Expression

The preferred pattern is:

```text
CANONICAL ZYPPI CORE
        ↓
DOMAIN-SPECIFIC MAPPING / PROFILE
        ↓
APPLICATION
        ↓
BUYER-SPECIFIC PRODUCT SURFACE
```

Marketing SHALL protect this separation.

This doctrine does not define the technical mechanism by which domain mapping is implemented.

---

# 16. Domain Vocabulary Principle

A new domain may require familiar domain-native language.

External interfaces SHOULD use terminology the domain understands.

The canonical meaning underneath SHALL remain coherent.

This follows:

> **Canonical underneath. Native on top. Explicit in between.**

---

# 17. Semantic Reuse Gate

Before entering a new domain, Zyppi SHOULD determine whether existing constitutional concepts still map coherently.

Examples include:

- Subject;
- Object;
- Place;
- Event;
- Evidence;
- Authority;
- Standing;
- Policy;
- Capability;
- Execution;
- Proof;
- Receipt.

The domain SHALL not force silent redefinition of these concepts.

---

# 18. Semantic Contamination Prohibition

A domain-specific meaning SHALL NOT silently mutate a canonical construct.

Example:

If one Commerce application treats `Object` as a product, the canonical platform SHALL NOT redefine Object as "product."

Domain language belongs above the canonical layer.

---

# 19. New Primitive Gate

If a new domain appears to require a new constitutional primitive, the request SHALL be reviewed through the relevant constitutional architecture process.

Marketing evidence alone cannot mutate the core.

The domain MAY remain blocked, adapted, or provisional until the architecture is resolved.

---

# 20. Capability Reuse Gate

A candidate domain SHOULD identify which existing Zyppi capabilities are reused.

Possible classes:

- identity;
- persistent reference;
- observation;
- Evidence;
- verification;
- Authority;
- Standing;
- policy;
- capability discovery;
- execution;
- Proof;
- Receipt;
- event history;
- developer interfaces;
- billing.

Higher reuse generally strengthens expansion.

---

# 21. Reuse Depth

Reuse SHOULD be assessed at several levels.

## R0 — Cosmetic Reuse
Brand or UI only.

## R1 — Infrastructure Reuse
Auth, billing, hosting, monitoring.

## R2 — Capability Reuse
Resolver, events, policy, state, Trust, execution.

## R3 — Constitutional Reuse
Same canonical semantics and governance.

## R4 — Cross-Domain Compounding
Value created in one domain strengthens another.

True platform replication becomes more credible as reuse moves upward.

---

# 22. Infrastructure Reuse Alone Is Not Universal Platform Proof

Two unrelated SaaS products sharing:

- authentication;
- billing;
- database;

do not prove a universal platform.

Constitutional and capability reuse matter.

---

# 23. Cross-Domain Compounding

The strongest future form of domain replication occurs when one domain creates value that another domain can reuse.

Examples conceptually may include:

- shared Authority;
- shared Evidence relationships;
- shared execution governance;
- shared identity relationships;
- shared capability infrastructure.

Cross-domain compounding is a future hypothesis until demonstrated.

---

# 24. Expansion Gate A — Core Market Stability

Before major E-Class 3 or 4 expansion, Zyppi SHOULD understand whether the establishing market has sufficient stability.

Possible evidence:

- repeatable activation;
- retention;
- reliable operation;
- known buyer;
- understood distribution;
- clear economics.

This does not require complete domination.

It requires enough stability that expansion is not simply escape from unresolved problems.

---

# 25. Escape Expansion Anti-Pattern

The Marketing branch prohibits using new domains to avoid confronting weakness in the current one.

Pattern:

```text
Commerce adoption weak
→ declare another domain more attractive
```

A new domain MAY still be valid.

The original weakness must remain honestly recorded.

---

# 26. Expansion Gate B — Problem Evidence

A candidate domain SHOULD have E2+ evidence of a meaningful problem before material build.

E3 evidence is preferred before large irreversible investment.

---

# 27. Expansion Gate C — Buyer Evidence

The candidate domain SHALL independently define:

- entry user;
- buyer;
- economic buyer;
- Job;
- why now;
- current alternative;
- Buyer Bridge.

No Commerce buyer assumption travels automatically.

---

# 28. Expansion Gate D — Distribution Evidence

A domain SHALL identify a plausible acquisition path.

Possible channels may differ completely from Commerce.

The domain SHALL not be authorized merely because the product can technically be built.

---

# 29. Expansion Gate E — Economic Evidence

The candidate SHOULD demonstrate a plausible economic model consistent with ZEA.

This includes:

- free participation boundary where relevant;
- emergent paid value;
- COGS;
- human burden;
- revenue density.

---

# 30. Expansion Gate F — Strategic Reuse

The domain SHOULD demonstrate enough platform reuse to justify Zyppi ownership.

A standalone attractive market with almost no reuse carries higher burden of proof.

---

# 31. Expansion Gate G — Anti-Fragmentation

The candidate SHALL show that entry will not create:

- duplicate semantics;
- parallel runtimes;
- incompatible economic systems;
- isolated account systems;
- separate Trust models;
- arbitrary domain forks.

A new market is allowed.

A new Zyppi inside Zyppi is not.

---

# 32. Expansion Gate H — Opportunity Cost

The program SHALL state what current work will be delayed.

A domain may be attractive yet premature because:

- core entry is not established;
- engineering capacity is scarce;
- distribution is unresolved.

---

# 33. Expansion Gate I — Evidence Target

Before material entry, the program SHOULD define what evidence would justify:

- continuing;
- scaling;
- stopping.

This connects to `Marketing-011`.

---

# 34. Expansion Permission Levels

A candidate domain may occupy one of five states.

## P0 — Observe
Research only.

## P1 — Explore
Limited reconnaissance and interviews.

## P2 — Experiment
Small reversible product/market test.

## P3 — Establish
Authorized focused entry program.

## P4 — Scale
Repeatable market evidence supports significant investment.

Movement between states SHALL be evidence-based.

---

# 35. Observe State

At P0, Zyppi may:

- monitor standards;
- collect market evidence;
- record opportunities.

No meaningful product investment is implied.

---

# 36. Explore State

At P1, Zyppi may conduct:

- buyer research;
- competitor attack;
- architecture mapping;
- small prototypes.

The objective is not market entry yet.

---

# 37. Experiment State

At P2, Zyppi may expose a small real-market test.

The experiment SHOULD maximize learning while minimizing irreversible domain commitment.

---

# 38. Establish State

At P3, a domain becomes an active strategic program.

It SHOULD receive:

- explicit owner;
- plan;
- buyer;
- distribution;
- metrics;
- economic model.

P3 is a significant strategic commitment.

---

# 39. Scale State

P4 requires repeatable E3/E4 evidence such as:

- retained adoption;
- channel repeatability;
- economic sustainability;
- manageable operations.

Scale SHALL not follow architecture excitement alone.

# 44. Domain Claims

# 45. Second Domain Significance

A genuine second domain is strategically important because it begins testing whether:

> **Zyppi's constitutional capability architecture can replicate beyond the market for which it was first implemented.**

The second domain is therefore a platform-validation event.

It is not automatically proof of universality.

---

# 46. Third Domain Significance

A successful third materially distinct domain may strengthen evidence that replication is systematic rather than accidental.

Even then, claims remain evidence-bound.

---

# 47. Cross-Domain Product-System Test

A multi-domain platform SHOULD demonstrate that domains share more than corporate ownership.

Ask:

1. Do they share canonical primitives?
2. Do they share governance?
3. Do they share economic architecture?
4. Do they share developer interfaces?
5. Does learning transfer?
6. Does infrastructure transfer?
7. Does any value compound across domains?

---

# 48. Domain Independence

A domain SHOULD remain independently understandable to its buyer.

The customer in Domain B SHALL not need to understand Domain A to adopt Zyppi.

---

# 49. Shared Platform, Independent Go-to-Market

The preferred pattern is:

```text
Shared constitutional core
+
domain-specific go-to-market
```

This allows universal architecture without universal marketing abstraction.

---

# 50. Domain-Specific Buyer Positioning

Each domain SHALL identify its own:

- category anchor;
- Job;
- buyer;
- proof;
- CTA.

"Reality Sync" may remain the company umbrella.

It need not be the first product category.

---

# 51. Domain-Specific Free Boundary

Different domains MAY have different foundational participation economics.

The doctrine of `Marketing-005` applies.

The exact free surface need not match Commerce.

---

# 52. Domain-Specific ECU Expression

All domains SHOULD remain compatible with the common economic architecture.

Customer-facing pricing may differ.

ECU meaning remains governed by ZEA.

---

# 53. Domain-Specific Distribution

Distribution channels MAY differ materially.

Examples:

```text
Commerce
→ developers / packaging ecosystem

Future Domain
```

This is expected.

---

# 54. Domain-Specific Activation

Activation SHALL reflect the domain's actual first meaningful value.

Commerce activation cannot be copied by default.

---

# 55. Domain-Specific Evidence

The authoritative sources and Evidence structures relevant to one domain may differ from another.

Marketing SHALL not transfer domain-specific authority assumptions.

---

# 56. Domain-Specific Regulation

Different domains may operate under distinct regulations.

Regulatory urgency does not eliminate buyer validation.

---

# 57. Domain-Specific Human Burden

A domain requiring high-touch implementation may be economically valid later.

It may still be a poor Baby-stage expansion.

Human-resource scarcity remains relevant.

---

# 58. Adjacent-Domain Preference

Where two opportunities have similar market potential, Zyppi SHOULD generally prefer the one with greater:

- capability reuse;
- semantic reuse;
- distribution reuse;
- economic reuse.

This strengthens platform learning.

---

# 59. High-Reuse Does Not Override Weak Market

A perfectly reusable domain with no buyer remains a bad expansion.

Market Reality retains authority.

---

# 60. High-Market Does Not Override Fragmentation Automatically

A large market requiring a completely separate product/company may be attractive.

It is not automatically appropriate for Zyppi.

The strategic burden of proof increases.

---

# 61. Domain Entry Through Existing Participants

Existing Zyppi participants MAY create expansion opportunities into another domain.

This may lower distribution cost.

The new Job still requires validation.

---

# 62. Partner-Led Domain Expansion

Partners MAY introduce Zyppi into a new domain.

Partner opportunity does not replace:

- buyer evidence;
- economics;
- architecture review.

---

# 63. Customer-Led Domain Expansion

A major customer request MAY reveal a domain opportunity.

One customer SHALL not define the domain roadmap automatically.

---

# 64. Standards-Led Expansion

New standards may create entry opportunities.

Standards adoption MAY reduce infrastructure friction.

It does not create guaranteed Zyppi demand.

---

# 65. Regulatory-Led Expansion

Regulation may create urgency.

Zyppi SHALL still identify:

- payer;
- workflow;
- alternative;
- distribution.

---

# 66. AI-Led Expansion

AI agents may create new demand for trusted capability access across domains.

This is strategically relevant.

AI enthusiasm SHALL not waive evidence gates.

---

# 67. Geographic Expansion vs Domain Expansion

Entering a new country with the same product is generally geographic expansion.

It may require:

- localization;
- regulation;
- distribution.

It is not necessarily a new domain.

---

# 68. Geographic Replication Gate

A new geography SHOULD validate:

- buyer behavior;
- pricing;
- regulatory fit;
- distribution.

Success in one geography does not automatically generalize.

---

# 69. Segment Expansion vs Domain Expansion

Moving from small manufacturers to large manufacturers may be segment expansion.

The buyer architecture and procurement may change substantially.

This can require a new GTM motion without being a new domain.

---

# 70. Enterprise Expansion vs Domain Expansion

Moving from developer self-service to enterprise governance can represent economic/capability deepening inside the same domain.

Do not misclassify it as a new domain.

---

# 80. Horizon Is Not Deadline

Evidence may accelerate, delay, or reject expansion.

---

# 81. Domain Expansion as Platform Test

Each genuinely new domain should answer a platform question:

> **What does this domain teach us about whether Zyppi is truly universal?**

This makes expansion a strategic validation tool, not just revenue diversification.

---

# 82. Expansion Learning

A new domain may reveal:

- overfit primitives;
- missing semantics;
- architecture assumptions;
- economic assumptions;
- distribution limits.

These findings SHOULD feed constitutional review where appropriate.

---

# 83. Constitutional Challenge From Domain Evidence

If repeated domain evidence reveals the Constitution is overfit, the correct response is:

- explicit constitutional review;
- documented amendment.

It is not silent implementation drift.

---

# 84. Domain Failure

A domain may fail because:

- problem weak;
- buyer absent;
- distribution impossible;
- reuse low;
- economics poor.

Failure SHALL be preserved as strategic evidence.

---

# 85. Domain Failure Does Not Disprove North Star Automatically

One failed domain does not prove Zyppi cannot be universal.

It may indicate:

- wrong domain;
- wrong timing;
- wrong entry application.

The scope of failure SHALL remain precise.

---

# 86. Repeated Replication Failure

If multiple materially distinct domains repeatedly fail because the canonical model cannot adapt without fragmentation, this SHALL become evidence against the universality thesis.

The North Star itself may require review if Reality repeatedly contradicts it.

---

# 87. Replication Success

Replication success becomes stronger when a new domain demonstrates:

- lower build effort due to reuse;
- faster activation due to shared infrastructure;
- shared economic system;
- coherent semantics;
- independent market adoption.

---

# 88. Replication Efficiency

Programs MAY measure:

```text
new-domain value created
÷
new domain-specific complexity introduced
```

The formula is conceptual.

Lower incremental complexity is a sign of platform leverage.

---

# 89. Domain Complexity Budget

Each new domain introduces complexity.

Programs SHOULD track:

- new semantics;
- new regulation;
- new buyers;
- new support;
- new infrastructure.

Expansion SHALL not assume complexity is free.

---

# 90. Domain Human-Attention Budget

A new domain may be rejected if it requires disproportionate founder or specialist attention relative to strategic value.

---

# 91. Domain Brand Architecture

Different domains may require distinct product names or narratives.

Brand architecture SHALL preserve:

- Zyppi identity;
- domain clarity;
- product comprehension.

Exact naming belongs to Brand governance.

---

# 92. Domain API Architecture

Domain-native APIs may express familiar vocabulary.

They SHOULD map to canonical Zyppi semantics underneath.

This doctrine governs market expression, not technical implementation details.

---

# 93. Domain Developer Experience

Developer experience SHOULD allow a developer to work naturally inside the domain without learning unrelated domains.

The platform's universality should reduce complexity internally, not export it to the user.

---

# 94. Domain Data Boundary

Entering a domain SHALL not justify turning Zyppi into an arbitrary content warehouse.

The broader principle remains:

> **Store truth. Reference content.**

---

# 95. Domain Ownership Boundary

Zyppi SHOULD own constitutional state relevant to:

- identity;
- relationships;
- Events;
- Evidence;
- policy;
- execution;
- receipts.

It SHOULD integrate with existing systems for domain content where appropriate.

---

# 96. Domain Integration Doctrine

Zyppi SHOULD generally integrate rather than replace established systems unless replacement is necessary for the Job.

This reduces Feature Gravity.

---

# 97. Domain Feature Gravity

Every new domain SHOULD identify which adjacent application categories may pull Zyppi away from infrastructure.

Examples:

```text
product identity
→ PIM

property verification
→ property management suite

workflow Trust
→ generic ERP
```

The exact examples are illustrative.

---

# 98. Domain Kill Conditions

A candidate domain SHOULD be reworked, demoted, or rejected when:

- no urgent Job;
- no accessible buyer;
- no viable distribution;
- reuse is superficial;
- new semantics duplicate canonical constructs;
- human burden excessive;
- economics weak;
- Feature Gravity dominates;
- opportunity cost unacceptable.

---

# 99. Domain Preservation

A rejected domain may preserve useful:

- primitive;
- integration;
- partner;
- evidence;
- market learning.

Rejection does not require deleting everything learned.

---

# 100. Re-entry Trigger

A rejected domain MAY be reconsidered when:

- regulation changes;
- buyer behavior changes;
- infrastructure matures;
- costs fall;
- new distribution appears;
- new evidence emerges.

---

# 101. No Expansion by Prestige

A domain SHALL not be prioritized because it appears:

- prestigious;
- futuristic;
- investor-friendly;
- politically important;

without market and capability evidence.

---

# 102. No Expansion by TAM Alone

Large theoretical market size SHALL not override:

- buyer;
- distribution;
- reuse;
- economics.

---

# 103. No Expansion by Customer Logo

One famous customer request SHALL not automatically authorize a new domain.

---

# 104. No Expansion by Architecture Excitement

The fact that Zyppi can model a domain elegantly is not sufficient.

Architecture earns option value.

Evidence earns permission.

---

# 105. No Expansion by Competitor Absence

An apparently empty market may indicate:

- opportunity;
- weak demand;
- difficult economics.

"No competitors" SHALL not be treated as proof.

---

# 106. No Domain Clone

A new domain SHALL not duplicate:

- auth;
- billing;
- policy;
- receipts;
- event ledger;

# 129. Constitutional Invariants

The following are proposed as ratifiable invariants.

## Invariant 1
Architecture creates expansion option value; market evidence creates expansion permission.

## Invariant 2
Application expansion, segment expansion, market expansion, and domain expansion SHALL remain distinct.

## Invariant 3
Bootstrap utility proliferation SHALL not be mistaken for multi-domain platform validation.

## Invariant 4
A new domain SHALL not inherit Commerce buyers, pricing, activation, distribution, or semantics automatically.

## Invariant 5
Canonical Zyppi concepts SHALL not be silently redefined to fit a domain.

## Invariant 6
New domains SHOULD reuse constitutional capabilities rather than create parallel systems.

## Invariant 7
Infrastructure reuse alone SHALL not be treated as universal platform proof.

## Invariant 8
Material domain entry SHALL require a real problem, buyer, distribution path, economics, and acceptable human burden.

## Invariant 9
Expansion SHALL not be used to escape unresolved weakness in the establishing market.

## Invariant 10
A second domain is a platform-validation event, not proof of universality.

## Invariant 11
Cross-domain compounding SHALL not be claimed until one domain demonstrably increases value in another.

## Invariant 12
Domain-specific market language MAY vary while canonical meaning remains stable.

## Invariant 13
High architecture reuse SHALL not override weak market evidence.

## Invariant 14
High market attractiveness SHALL not automatically override constitutional fragmentation.

## Invariant 15
Domain expansion SHALL remain subject to explicit opportunity-cost review.

## Invariant 16
Failed domain experiments SHALL remain preserved as strategic evidence.

## Invariant 17
Repeated cross-domain failure MAY constitute evidence against the universality thesis and SHALL not be ignored.

## Invariant 18
Future expansion SHALL proceed through staged permission—Observe, Explore, Experiment, Establish, Scale—rather than assumption.

---

## Invariant 19
The term Domain is a governed strategic classification and SHALL not be used to bypass application, segment, market, or expansion gates.

## Invariant 20
Domain classification governance SHALL remain lightweight for Observe/Explore work and SHALL scale only with strategic consequence.

# 133. Constitutional Success Criteria

`Marketing-014` succeeds when:

# 134. Closing Principle

Zyppi should be universal by architecture before it is universal by market.

That asymmetry is intentional.

The platform must preserve the ability to serve many domains long before the company has earned the right to pursue them all.

The mistake would be to confuse:

```text
can enter
```

with:

```text
should enter now
```

The Marketing branch therefore adopts:

> **Preserve universal capability. Focus market execution. Expand only when Reality earns the next domain. Reuse the canonical engine, adapt the domain expression, validate the buyer independently, and refuse every expansion that requires Zyppi to become a different platform underneath.**

And:

> **The second domain should prove that Zyppi can replicate. The third should begin to prove that replication is systematic. Only repeated cross-domain dependence can earn the right to speak of universal market infrastructure.**
