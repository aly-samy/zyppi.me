# 3. Advisor Findings Accepted and Incorporated

## 3.1 Baby-stage billing must remain simple

`Marketing-006 v1.1` now explicitly prohibits premature construction of a sophisticated ECU-native settlement engine merely because the architecture allows one. During Baby, the simplest wrapper capable of producing real E3 payment evidence is preferred.

The settlement doctrine was also strengthened so threshold billing cannot create indefinitely open receivables. The exact chronological backstop remains plan-level rather than constitutionally fixed.

## 3.2 Market Attacks require an operational clock

`Marketing-007 v1.1` now requires an explicit timebox for Standard/P1/P2 attacks. A universal 48-hour, 72-hour, or 14-day rule was **not** constitutionalized because attack complexity varies materially.

When the timebox expires and more E2 research is unlikely to change the decision, the protocol must move toward `PROVISIONAL PASS`, `HOLD`, or an E3 experiment rather than indefinite desk research.

## 3.3 Buyer Bridges should produce a legible transfer of value

`Marketing-008 v1.1` adds the **Bridge Artifact** concept. Where entry and economic buyers differ, the current program should identify a concrete report, proof, metric, audit artifact, or equivalent mechanism that makes entry-layer value legible to the next buying center.

## 3.4 Physical distribution requires verified continuity before scale

`Marketing-009 v1.1` adds a **Physical Deployment Gate**. Zyppi shall not actively encourage irreversible/material-scale printing or physical deployment until the relevant foundational continuity/fallback behavior is technically verified to the level appropriate for that deployment.

## 3.5 Successful bootstrap revenue does not waive Feature-Gravity controls

`Marketing-010 v1.1` now states explicitly that material expansion of a successful bootstrap utility into a new application category requires a fresh Market Attack, Feature-Gravity analysis, opportunity-cost analysis, and explicit strategic authorization. Revenue magnitude alone is not permission.

# 4. Advisor Findings Not Adopted as Stated

## 4.1 `Marketing-008` is incomplete — REJECTED AS FACTUALLY INAPPLICABLE TO THE CANONICAL FILE

Gemini's review was based on a stale or truncated copy.

The canonical `Marketing-008 v1.0` reviewed for ratification was complete through Section 112, including:

- Section 85 — Developer-Only Anti-Pattern;
- Minimum Viable Segmentation;
- Constitutional Invariants;
- Strategic and Program Requirements;
- Validation Gates;
- Success Criteria;
- Ratification Statement.

There was therefore no missing-text P0 blocker. The canonical file has nevertheless advanced to `v1.1` because of the Bridge Artifact addition.

## 4.2 Fixed 90-day settlement backstop — NOT CONSTITUTIONALIZED

The principle of bounded receivables is accepted.

A fixed maximum such as 90 days belongs in a current commercial program because:

- payment-processing costs vary;
- jurisdictions and currencies differ;
- some amounts may be economically irrational to settle individually.

The Constitution now requires a bounded policy without freezing the number.

## 4.3 Fixed attack duration such as 48–72 hours or 14 days — NOT CONSTITUTIONALIZED

The timebox principle is accepted.

One global duration is not. A simple pricing comparison and a regulatory/structural P0 attack cannot reasonably share one constitutional time limit.

## 4.4 Commerce-specific Bridge Artifact — DEFERRED TO CURRENT COMMERCE PROGRAM

The universal requirement is now constitutionalized. The exact artifact for Commerce/GS1 must be selected and validated in the current 1-year plan/program.

## 4.5 Exact Physical Continuity implementation — DEFERRED TO ARCHITECTURE / PROGRAM VERIFICATION

The constitutional gate is ratified. The actual technical fallback, resolver behavior, tests, operational reserve, and deployment acceptance criteria belong to product/architecture mandates and the current Commerce program.
