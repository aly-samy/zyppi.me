# 0. Purpose

`ZEA-001` establishes the Economic Consumption Unit as Zyppi's canonical economic measurement system.

The ECU is not a price.

The ECU is not a currency.

The ECU is not a token.

The ECU is not a commercial plan.

The ECU measures constitutional economic participation and resource consumption.

Commercial pricing then translates that measured consumption into market-facing economic terms.

`Marketing-006` governs that translation interface.

It exists to answer:

- how customers should encounter ECU-based economics;
- how Zyppi communicates consumption simply;
- how foundational use may remain commercially free while measured internally;
- how pricing remains fair from the smallest participant to the largest institution;
- how different kinds of economic activity may share one commercial accounting system;
- how credits, allowances, subsidies, volume economics, and enterprise contracts may coexist;
- how Zyppi avoids opaque billing and pricing fear;
- how budget controls protect participants;
- how micro-billing may remain economically efficient;
- how billing must interact with operational continuity;
- how Marketing may package Zyppi without redefining ECU governance;
- how future domains can inherit the same pricing logic.

The governing rule is:

> **The ECU defines what Zyppi consumes. Pricing defines how that consumption is commercially valued. Marketing explains that value without changing the ECU.**

# 2. ECU Definition by Reference

The Marketing branch adopts the Economic Architecture's definition:

> **The Economic Consumption Unit (ECU) is the canonical constitutional unit used to quantify the consumption of Zyppi resources and constitutional services.**

The ECU remains independent of:

- currency;
- geography;
- taxation;
- inflation;
- exchange rates;
- commercial packaging.

Commercial price may change.

Historical ECU consumption should remain comparable under constitutional governance.

---

# 3. ECU Is Not Money

The Marketing branch SHALL never describe ECU as:

- currency;
- cryptocurrency;
- balance-sheet asset;
- speculative token;
- payment instrument.

A customer may buy or receive commercial access to ECU-denominated consumption.

That does not make the ECU money.

The market-facing relationship is:

```text
Service Activity
        ↓
ECU Consumption
        ↓
Commercial Pricing Policy
        ↓
Currency Amount
        ↓
Settlement
```

---

# 4. Pricing Is an Expression of Policy

Pricing SHALL NOT independently determine constitutional value.

Pricing implements:

- economic policy;
- participation policy;
- resource policy;
- market strategy.

Therefore:

> **Commercial pricing derives from ECU policy; it does not redefine ECU policy.**

A price increase SHALL NOT be implemented by artificially increasing ECU consumption for the same constitutional operation.

- governance;

# 7. Small Participant Fairness

A small legitimate participant SHOULD be able to participate without being forced into economics designed for a large institution.

Examples:

- individual developer;
- family manufacturer;
- startup;
- nonprofit;
- experimental application.

A participant with low consumption may legitimately have:

```text
low or zero commercial charge
```

even if the same capability produces substantial revenue at large scale.

This is economic proportionality.

# 8. Large Participant Fairness

A large participant may consume:

- millions of operations;
- high retention;
- dedicated infrastructure;
- Trust evaluation;
- governed execution;
- enterprise controls.

Higher commercial cost is legitimate when it corresponds to:

```text
greater constitutional consumption
+
greater operational leverage
+
greater organizational requirements
```

The customer should be able to understand this relationship.

# 9. Measured vs Charged

```text
MEASURED
≠
```

An operation may consume ECUs internally while receiving:

# 11. Participation Credit

A **Participation Credit** is a commercial or constitutional allocation that offsets foundational ECU consumption.

Participation Credit may support:

- open-standard access;
- identity;
- low-cost developer use;
- market-entry infrastructure.

Participation Credit SHALL NOT alter the ECU definition.

It alters who bears the commercial cost.

# 12. Activation Credit

An **Activation Credit** may offset an initial quantity of higher-order consumption so a participant can experience real value.

Activation Credits may support:

- first policy use;
- first workflow;
- initial retained history;
- initial Trust test;
- first automation.

The purpose is:

> **experience before extraction**

not permanent subsidy of material economic leverage.

# 13. Promotional Credit

Promotional credits are tactical.

They may be:

- launch offers;
- partner grants;
- developer campaigns;
- migration incentives.

Promotional credits SHALL be distinguished from constitutional free-layer protections.

A promotion can expire.

A constitutional free boundary requires stronger governance.

- governance;

# 23. No Artificial ECU Inflation

Zyppi SHALL NOT increase ECU consumption merely to raise price.

---

# 24. No Hidden ECU Consumption

Every material ECU-consuming capability SHALL be documented.

- what consumes ECU;
- approximate ECU intensity;
- why.

---

# 25. No Dynamic Manipulation

Zyppi SHALL NOT change ECU consumption unpredictably based on:

- customer willingness to pay;
- short-term revenue goals;
- sales pressure.

Dynamic pricing may exist at the commercial price layer when constitutionally justified.

The ECU remains stable.

# 26. Price Can Change Without ECU Mutation

The market-facing price per ECU or commercial conversion policy may evolve.

For example:

```text
1,000 ECU
→ $X today
→ $Y later
```

provided:

- change follows governance;
- rationale is documented;
- notice is reasonable;
- historical ECU records remain intact.

This is preferable to rewriting past economic meaning.

# 27. Pricing Stability

Pricing SHOULD evolve gradually.

Participants should rarely be surprised.

Plans SHOULD favor:

- advance notice;
- transition periods;
- grandfathering where justified;
- clear rationale;
- backward-compatible settlement rules.

Trust in infrastructure depends partly upon pricing continuity.

# 40. Micro-Billing Problem

Very small bills can become inefficient because:

- payment processing has fixed cost;
- invoices create operational overhead;
- customer experience becomes annoying.

Zyppi SHOULD solve micro-billing operationally.

It SHOULD NOT solve it by imposing arbitrary subscription floors.

---

# 41. Settlement Threshold

Plans MAY establish a **Settlement Threshold**.

Conceptually:

```text
small monthly usage
→ accrued balance
→ charge when threshold reached
```

This preserves true consumption economics.

The threshold is a billing mechanic.

It is not a minimum usage fee.

---

# 42. Settlement Period and Bounded Receivables

Very low-use accounts MAY settle:

- monthly;
- quarterly;
- when threshold reached.

However, a threshold mechanism SHALL NOT create indefinitely open commercial receivables. Every accrued paid obligation SHALL have a bounded settlement, write-off, expiry, or prepaid-credit policy defined by the current commercial program.

The exact chronological backstop is **plan-level**, because payment-processing cost and regional economics may differ.

The commercial obligation remains based on actual consumption.

---

# 42A. Baby-Stage Billing Minimalism

During Baby, Zyppi SHOULD use the simplest commercial wrapper capable of collecting real payment and producing E3 willingness-to-pay evidence.

Acceptable early wrappers may include:

- prepaid credits;
- simple included allowances;
- conventional subscription packaging;
- manual or provider-managed invoicing where proportionate.

Zyppi SHALL NOT build a sophisticated ECU-native settlement engine merely because the constitutional architecture permits one. Complex settlement automation SHOULD follow demonstrated product demand, material transaction volume, or clear operational necessity.

The governing principle is:

> **Validate the thing people will buy before optimizing the machinery that bills them for it.**

---

# 99. Closing Principle

Zyppi's economic architecture is intended to outlive individual products, markets, currencies, and pricing plans.

The ECU therefore belongs beneath commerce.

Pricing belongs above it.

Marketing exists at the boundary between the two.

Its job is not to maximize how much Zyppi can extract from each participant.

Its job is to create a commercial interface where:

```text
Participation remains accessible.
Consumption remains measurable.
Value remains explainable.
Pricing remains fair.
Growth remains rewarding.
Revenue remains sustainable.
```

The Marketing branch therefore adopts:

> **Measure constitutionally. Price commercially. Explain simply. Subsidize participation deliberately. Charge increasing leverage fairly. Never manipulate the ECU to manufacture revenue.**

And:

> **A family manufacturer and a global enterprise should participate in the same economic system; they should pay differently only because the value, resources, consequence, and governance they consume are different.**
