# 2. Review Verdict

**RATIFY WITH TARGETED CONSTITUTIONAL CORRECTIONS.**

The five-document set is internally coherent, subordinate to the North Star, consistent with the Foundations, `MARKET-REALITY-001`, and `ZEA-001`, and appropriately separates timeless doctrine from time-bounded Commerce/GS1 strategy.

No blocker was found that requires rejection or structural collapse of the set.

---

# 3. Advisor Findings Accepted

Three advisor concerns were accepted in substance:

## 3.1 Governance must remain lightweight

`Marketing-001 v1.1` now explicitly requires **minimum viable governance**. Claim registries and Decision Receipts protect evidence quality but SHALL NOT become ceremonial bureaucracy or delay authorized market contact.

## 3.2 External entry positioning must lead with the buyer's immediate job

`Marketing-003 v1.1` now contains an **Entry-Stage External Positioning Firewall**. Broad Zyppi platform language remains constitutionally valid, but first-contact market positioning SHALL normally lead with the immediate buyer Job-To-Be-Done, recognizable category/context, and current product outcome.

## 3.3 Capability depth must not be confused with a linear buyer journey

`Marketing-004 v1.1` now states explicitly that capability progression is a platform-depth model, not an assumption that the same user or department naturally buys every deeper layer. Plans relying on cross-layer expansion SHOULD identify a credible **Buyer Bridge**.

---

# 4. Advisor Findings Not Adopted

## 4.1 Collapse the Marketing Constitution into three documents — NOT ADOPTED

The risk identified by Gemini—bureaucratic paralysis—is legitimate, but collapsing the constitutional branch is not required to solve it.

The constitutional response is:

- `Marketing-001` through `Marketing-005` form **Core Set A**;
- later documents `Marketing-006` through `Marketing-015` remain authorized by `Marketing-000` but are **not prerequisites to market execution**;
- they should be drafted when their governing subject becomes operationally relevant;
- documentation SHALL NOT substitute for customer contact, experiments, or execution.

Granular doctrine is acceptable when operational governance remains lightweight.

## 4.2 Fixed external label such as “Enterprise GS1 Digital Link Engine” — NOT CONSTITUTIONALIZED

The principle of narrow buyer-facing positioning is accepted.

A specific phrase or category label is not constitutional doctrine and remains subject to E3/E4 testing under `Marketing-001`.

## 4.3 Temporary Commerce activation event inside `Marketing-004` — NOT ADOPTED AT CONSTITUTIONAL LEVEL

Qwen's recommendation to define exact Commerce activation is correct operationally but belongs in the current Commerce plan / metrics program, not in a domain-neutral constitutional doctrine.

`Marketing-004` already requires activation to be product-specific and behavioral.

## 4.4 Numerical CMS Feature-Gravity threshold — DEFERRED TO PLAN / PROGRAM

The constitutional No-CMS / Minimum Destination / Store Truth; Reference Content boundaries are already present.

A numerical threshold such as engineering percentage belongs in the current Commerce program's kill conditions, where it can be measured and revised.

# 5. Review of the Five Documents

## Marketing-001

**Verdict:** PASS after lightweight-governance amendment.

Preserves the E0–E4 ladder, evidence-source discipline, negative evidence, contradiction handling, AI simulation boundary, claim strength, and hypothesis attack method.

## Marketing-002

**Verdict:** PASS without substantive amendment.

Correctly establishes that the first market does not define Zyppi, a Trojan Horse is immediate utility plus durable compounding, and the acquisition mechanism need not be the first cash register or moat.

## Marketing-003

**Verdict:** PASS after external-positioning amendment.

Preserves Feature ≠ Product System ≠ Moat while ensuring broad platform identity does not impose unnecessary cognitive burden on entry-stage buyers.

## Marketing-004

**Verdict:** PASS after Buyer-Bridge clarification.

The original draft already stated that progression is not always linear and different layers may have different buyers. Version 1.1 strengthens this by expressly prohibiting interpretation of capability depth as an automatic cross-department buyer journey.

## Marketing-005

**Verdict:** PASS without substantive amendment.

The measured-versus-charged doctrine, anti-standards-tollbooth rule, identity accessibility, fair-use distinction, strategic subsidy, and upward monetization boundary align strongly with `ZEA-001`.

# 6. Ratification Effects

Ratification of Set A establishes constitutional doctrine for:

```text
Market Truth
        ↓
Market Entry
        ↓
Positioning / Differentiation
        ↓
Adoption / Economic Deepening
        ↓
Free Participation / Market Capture
```

It does **not** ratify:

- any fixed Commerce/GS1 positioning phrase;
- any exact Commerce activation event;
- any current ECU weight or monetary rate;
- any exact free allowance;
- Interaction Intelligence pricing;
- Bootstrap Utility candidates;
- a 1-, 3-, or 5-year strategic plan;
- product-market fit;
- moat;
- market dominance.

Those remain governed by evidence and lower time-bounded instruments.

# 7. Execution Directive

The ratified Constitution SHALL accelerate market contact rather than delay it.

Accordingly:

> **No unratified future Marketing document is required merely to permit an already constitutionally authorized, reversible market experiment.**

Later constitutional documents should be drafted when their subject materially requires durable governance.

Operational Commerce/GS1 definitions—such as exact activation event, CMS kill threshold, entry positioning variants, and buyer bridges—belong in the 1-year plan and current programs.
