# 0. Executive Strategic Mandate

The 3-year horizon SHALL deepen Zyppi from a standards-native Commerce acquisition layer into a progressively more consequential trust and execution infrastructure **without forcing customers to migrate away from the systems they already use**.

The central strategic movement is:

```text
GS1 / PRODUCT IDENTITY
        ↓
DPP / LIFECYCLE STATE
        ↓
EVENT / INTEROPERABILITY
        ↓
EVIDENCE / AUTHORITY / STANDING / POLICY
        ↓
CONSEQUENTIAL PRODUCT ACTION
        ↓
DOMAIN-2 READINESS
```

But the external go-to-market SHALL NOT mirror that architecture as a sequence of separate Zyppi products.

The customer experience should instead become:

```text
Existing ERP / Commerce / PIM / PLM / WMS
        +
     Zyppi
        ↓
Capabilities appear progressively
inside the customer's existing system of work
```

The governing 3-year principle is:

> **Deepen capabilities before multiplying markets. Bring Zyppi to the customer's system of work; do not require the customer's system of work to move to Zyppi.**

By 2 September 2029, Zyppi should be able to answer:

> **Can the same constitutional engine that began with product identity become trusted lifecycle and execution infrastructure across existing enterprise systems, while remaining easy to adopt and without fragmenting into many disconnected products?**

A strong 3-year outcome would demonstrate:

1. retained Commerce adoption;
2. at least one successful Host-Native integration pattern;

The plan adopts:

Therefore:

- EPCIS may be an interoperability language.

# 6. Expansion Continuity Test

Every significant expansion SHALL be evaluated on three axes.

## Axis A — Capability Continuity

How much existing Zyppi capability is reused?

## Axis B — System-of-Work Continuity

Can the new capability appear inside the systems the customer already uses?

## Axis C — Buyer-Motion Continuity

Can the new capability be adopted through:

- existing account;
- existing champion;
- existing procurement relationship;

or does it require an entirely new buying center / sales motion?

The safer expansion is generally the one with stronger continuity across all three axes.

---

# 7. Expansion Modes

The plan recognizes four operating expansion modes.

## Mode A — Capability Deepening

Same account. Same system. Similar buying motion.

Example:

```text
GS1 resolution
→ contextual resolution
```

Lowest incremental adoption friction.

## Mode B — Host-Native Buyer Expansion

Same account and installed integration, but a new organizational buyer activates additional capability.

Example:

```text
Developer activates GS1
→ Compliance activates DPP
inside the same SAP / Shopify / ERP relationship
```

Preferred strategic expansion pattern.

## Mode C — Ecosystem Expansion

The workflow requires additional external systems or organizations.

Example:

```text
manufacturer
→ 3PL / distributor / supplier event participation
```

Higher operational and distribution burden.

## Mode D — Domain Expansion

A materially different Reality domain, buyer system, consequences, and market motion.

Example:

```text
Commerce
→ Healthcare
```

Full `Marketing-014` expansion discipline applies.

The 3-year plan establishes **Host-Native Adoption** as a primary strategic doctrine for execution.

Definition:

> **Host-Native Adoption is the strategy of delivering Zyppi capabilities inside the customer's existing system of work while minimizing migration, duplicated data, workflow displacement, retraining, and separate operational surfaces.**

The desired adoption sequence is:

```text
CONNECT
↓
DISCOVER
↓
MAP
↓
OBSERVE
↓

↓
GOVERN
↓
EXECUTE
```

```text

↓
MIGRATE
↓
REBUILD
↓
RETRAIN
↓
CUT OVER
```

# 9. Host-Native Adoption Objective

The strategic user experience should approach:

> **Install / authorize Zyppi → map existing state → activate useful capability.**

The plan MAY use "near-zero migration" as an internal design objective.

It SHALL NOT promise literal one-click integration where enterprise security, governance, or configuration make that untrue.
