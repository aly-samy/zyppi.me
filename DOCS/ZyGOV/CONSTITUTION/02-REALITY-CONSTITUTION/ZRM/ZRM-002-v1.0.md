# 1. Purpose

This document formalizes the constitutional primitives established by North Star v6.0 and the constitutional laws established by ZRM-001 into a coherent ontological model suitable for mathematical reasoning and downstream constitutional derivation.

**This document does not establish new constitutional truths.** Subject, Object, Place, Event, and Evidence are constitutional primitives established by North Star v6.0's Universal Reality Model. This document does not define them. It formalizes their ontological properties, their mutual independence, and their mathematical behavior within the Zyppi Reality Model.

This document additionally introduces three **Structural Constitutional Constructs** — Identity, Relationships, Attributes — which are not primitives, do not constitute Reality, and exist solely to organize how the five primitives relate to one another and to Representation.

---

# 2. Scope

This document defines, for each of the five primitives and three structural constructs:

- its constitutional nature (primitive vs. structural),
- its characteristics and invariants,
- its permitted constitutional relationships,
- and its explicit non-scope.

It also establishes the Constitutional Primitive Test governing any future proposal to add a primitive, and declares the current ontology closed pending such a test.

This document does **not** define business semantics, execution behavior, mathematics, storage, or implementation — see §9, Constitutional Non-Scope Summary.

# 4. Constitutional Primitives

Each primitive below is irreducible, is not derived from any other primitive, and exists independently of Observation, Representation, Identity, or Interpretation. None of the five may be redefined by this or any downstream document — only formalized.

## 4.1 Subject

### Definition

> *Derived from North Star v6.0, Universal Reality Model. This document does not define Subject; it formalizes it.*

A Subject is the constitutional primitive representing any autonomous constituent of Reality that exists independently and participates in Reality through its own intrinsic nature — capable of initiating, holding standing, or acting with intent.

Examples include Humans, Organizations, AI Agents, Autonomous Systems, and future autonomous entities. These examples are illustrative only; their detailed semantic classification belongs to the Semantic Projection tier of downstream constitutions, not to this document.

### Primitive Nature

The Subject primitive SHALL satisfy the following constitutional properties:

- Subject is irreducible and SHALL NOT be decomposed into lower constitutional primitives.
- Subject SHALL NOT be redefined by downstream constitutions.
- Subject exists independently of every Representation, every Identity relationship, and every semantic interpretation.

Nothing downstream may change the constitutional meaning of Subject.

### Characteristics

Every Subject:

- Exists within Reality and through Time.
- May participate in Events through one or more constitutional Event Roles.
- May be associated with Evidence.
- May occupy Places.
- May participate in Relationships.
- May be bound to one or more Representations through Identity relationships.

These characteristics describe constitutional existence only. They SHALL NOT imply behavior, capability, authority, ownership, legal status, or business meaning.

### Invariants

**S-1 — Primitive Integrity.** A Subject SHALL always remain a Subject. No relationship, representation, observation, or semantic projection may alter its constitutional type.

**S-2 — Representation Independence.** A Subject may be represented in many different ways. Representations may change, disappear, conflict, be incomplete, be incorrect, or be entirely absent. None of these conditions changes the constitutional existence of the Subject.

**S-3 — Identity Independence.** Identity is not an intrinsic property of Subject. Identity exists exclusively as a constitutional relationship between a Primitive and one or more Representations (see §5.1). The existence or absence of Identity relationships SHALL NOT alter the constitutional existence of the Subject.

**S-4 — Observation Independence.** A Subject exists whether or not it has ever been observed. Observation discovers Reality; it does not create Reality.

**S-5 — Semantic Independence.** Business meaning or biological classification SHALL NOT modify the Subject primitive. Examples such as Human, Animal, Plant, Organization, Autonomous AI, Customer, Employee, Patient, Citizen, Driver, and Owner are semantic projections defined only in downstream constitutions.

### Relationships

A Subject may participate in constitutional relationships including participation in Events, association with Evidence, occupation of Places, relationships with other Primitives, and Identity relationships binding the Subject to one or more Representations. This document defines only the structural existence of these relationships; their semantics, constraints, permissions, capabilities, and business interpretations belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define capabilities, permissions, roles, employment, citizenship, ownership, authority, trust, security, authentication, credentials, legal status, cultural meaning, business classifications, psychological properties, biological classification, species, taxonomy, execution behavior, or implementation details for Subject. These belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.

## 4.2 Object

### Definition

> *Derived from North Star v6.0, Universal Reality Model. This document does not define Object; it formalizes it.*

An Object is the constitutional primitive representing any non-autonomous constituent of Reality possessing persistent existence and participating in Reality solely through external causation — a relationship with a Subject, not through its own intent.

### Primitive Nature

The Object primitive SHALL satisfy the following constitutional properties:

- Object is irreducible and SHALL NOT be decomposed into lower constitutional primitives.
- Object SHALL NOT be redefined by downstream constitutions.
- Object exists independently of every Representation, every Identity relationship, and every semantic interpretation.

### Characteristics

Every Object exists within Reality and through Time; may participate in Events; may be associated with Evidence; may occupy Places; may participate in Relationships; and may be bound to one or more Representations through Identity relationships. These characteristics describe constitutional existence only and SHALL NOT imply capability, authority, ownership, legal status, business meaning, or execution behavior.

### Invariants

**O-1 — Primitive Integrity.** An Object SHALL always remain an Object.

**O-2 — Autonomous Independence.** An Object possesses no intrinsic autonomy; its participation in Reality occurs only through external causation. An Object SHALL NOT independently originate Events. Event participation roles are defined exclusively within the Reality Mathematics tier.

**O-3 — Representation Independence.** An Object may possess many Representations; none of their conditions changes the constitutional existence of the Object.

**O-4 — Identity Independence.** Identity is not an intrinsic property of Object; it exists exclusively as a constitutional relationship between a Primitive and one or more Representations.

**O-5 — Observation Independence.** An Object exists whether or not it has ever been observed.

**O-6 — Semantic Independence.** Business meaning, technological implementation, and domain classification SHALL NOT modify the Object primitive. Examples such as Product, Vehicle, Building, Device, Equipment, Inventory Item, Digital Asset, Document, Package, and Infrastructure are semantic projections defined within the Semantic Projection tier.

### Relationships

An Object may participate in constitutional relationships including participation in Events, association with Evidence, occupation of Places, relationships with other Primitives, and Identity relationships. Semantics, permissions, constraints, capabilities, ownership, and business interpretations belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define capabilities, permissions, ownership, authority, business classifications, legal classifications, product categories, inventory models, asset management, execution behavior, workflows, implementation details, storage mechanisms, database structures, or serialization formats for Object.

## 4.3 Place

### Definition

> *Derived from North Star v6.0, Universal Reality Model. This document does not define Place; it formalizes it.*

A Place is the constitutional primitive representing a persistent spatial or contextual environment within Reality in which Subjects and Objects exist and interact, within which other Primitives may participate through constitutional Relationships, and within which Events may occur.

### Primitive Nature

The Place primitive SHALL satisfy the same irreducibility, non-redefinition, and independence properties as Subject and Object (§4.1–4.2), applied to Place.

### Characteristics

Every Place exists within Reality and through Time; may participate in Relationships with Subjects, Objects, and other Places; may be associated with Events and Evidence; and may be bound to one or more Representations through Identity relationships. These characteristics SHALL NOT imply ownership, jurisdiction, access, geography, navigation, political meaning, execution behavior, or hierarchical containment.

### Invariants

**P-1 — Primitive Integrity.** A Place SHALL always remain a Place.

**P-2 — Context Independence.** A Place exists independently of the Primitives participating in Relationships with it. The capacity to participate in Relationships is intrinsic to Place and does not require the presence of any Subject, Object, Event, or Evidence. Changes in the Relationships associated with a Place SHALL NOT alter its constitutional existence.

**P-3 — Representation Independence.** A Place may possess many Representations without its constitutional existence changing.

**P-4 — Identity Independence.** Identity is not an intrinsic property of Place; it exists exclusively as a constitutional relationship between a Primitive and one or more Representations.

**P-5 — Observation Independence.** A Place exists whether or not it has ever been observed.

**P-6 — Semantic Independence.** Business, political, legal, geographical, and topological interpretation SHALL NOT modify the Place primitive. Examples such as Country, City, Warehouse, Office, Room, Server Rack, Cloud Region, and Digital Workspace are semantic projections defined within the Semantic Projection tier.

### Relationships

A Place may participate in Relationships with Subjects, Objects, other Places, and may be associated with Events and Evidence, and bound to Representations through Identity. Semantics, permissions, ownership, hierarchy, geography, topology, routing, and business interpretation belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define geographic coordinates, addresses, political boundaries, jurisdiction, navigation, routing, ownership, access control, containment semantics, hierarchical models, capabilities, permissions, workflows, execution behavior, implementation details, storage mechanisms, mapping technologies, or serialization formats for Place.

## 4.4 Event

### Definition

> *Derived from ZRM-001 Constitutional Law II ("Every Change in Reality Is an Event") and North Star v6.0's Universal Reality Model. This document does not define Event; it formalizes it for ontological and mathematical purposes.*

An Event is the constitutional primitive representing an occurrence within Reality that produces one or more changes at a Valid Time. Every constitutional change in Reality SHALL occur exclusively through one or more Events.

### Primitive Nature

Event is irreducible, SHALL NOT be decomposed into lower constitutional primitives, and SHALL NOT be redefined by downstream constitutions. Event exists independently of Representation, Observation, Identity relationships, and semantic interpretation.

### Characteristics

Every Event exists within Reality; occurs at a Valid Time; may involve one or more Subjects, Objects, or Places; produces Evidence; may participate in Relationships; and may be bound to one or more Representations through Identity relationships. These characteristics SHALL NOT imply execution, sequencing, processing, causation algorithms, business workflows, permissions, or implementation behavior.

### Invariants

**E-1 — Primitive Integrity.** An Event SHALL always remain an Event.

**E-2 — Exclusivity of Change.** *Derived from ZRM-001 Law II.* Every constitutional change in Reality SHALL occur exclusively through one or more Events. No Subject, Object, Place, Relationship, Attribute, Identity, Representation, or downstream construct may constitutionally change except through participation in one or more Events. If no Event exists, no constitutional change has occurred.

**E-3 — Temporal Existence.** An Event exists at a Valid Time. The Valid Time of an Event SHALL NEVER change. Observation Time belongs exclusively to Observation and SHALL NOT alter the constitutional existence of the Event.

**E-4 — Representation Independence.** An Event may be represented in many different ways without its constitutional existence changing.

**E-5 — Observation Independence.** An Event exists whether or not it has ever been observed.

**E-6 — Semantic Independence.** Business meaning — purchase, shipment, payment, login, deployment, approval, registration — SHALL NOT modify the Event primitive. These are semantic projections defined exclusively within downstream constitutions.

### Relationships

An Event may participate in constitutional relationships including participation of Subjects, Objects, and Places, production of Evidence, relationships with other Primitives, and Identity relationships. Sequencing, permissions, capabilities, execution behavior, and business interpretations belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define event processing, event sourcing architecture, workflows, execution order, causation algorithms, business processes, commands, transactions, messaging, queues, orchestration, automation, state transition mathematics, event sequencing, distributed execution, APIs, storage mechanisms, or implementation details for Event.

## 4.5 Evidence

### Definition

> *Derived from ZRM-001 Constitutional Law III ("Every Event Leaves Evidence") and North Star v6.0's Universal Reality Model. This document does not define Evidence; it formalizes it.*

Evidence is the constitutional primitive representing an immutable constituent of Reality produced by an Event. Every Event SHALL generate one or more Evidence primitives. Evidence exists independently of Observation, Verification, Truth, Interpretation, Representation, and Meaning. Evidence never changes Reality; it only exists as a consequence of Reality.

### Primitive Nature

Evidence is irreducible, SHALL NOT be decomposed into smaller constitutional concepts, and SHALL NEVER be redefined by downstream constitutions. Identity may reference Evidence; Representation may describe Evidence; neither constitutes Evidence.

### Characteristics

- **Exists within Reality** — Evidence is never merely conceptual.
- **Produced by Events** — Evidence SHALL originate exclusively from one or more Events; it never exists without a constitutional Event origin.
- **Temporal Existence** — Evidence inherits the Valid Time of its originating Event, and separately possesses an Observation Time representing when it became known. Observation Time SHALL NEVER modify Evidence's constitutional existence.
- **Association** — Evidence may be associated with Subjects, Objects, Places, and Events, without implying ownership or semantic meaning.
- **Structural Participation** — Evidence may participate in constitutional Relationships; their semantics belong to downstream constitutions.
- **Passive Nature** — Evidence performs no constitutional action. It initiates nothing, changes nothing, verifies nothing, decides nothing. Evidence simply exists.

### Invariants

**EVD-1 — Primitive Integrity.** Evidence SHALL always remain Evidence.

**EVD-2 — Observation Independence.** Evidence exists independently of whether any Observer discovers it. Observation discovers Evidence; it never creates Evidence.

**EVD-3 — Truth Independence.** Evidence SHALL NOT be equivalent to Truth. Evidence may support Verification; Verification determines Knowledge's approximation of Truth. Truth belongs exclusively to ZRM-001 §6.5.

**EVD-4 — Representation Independence.** Representations may describe Evidence; they SHALL NOT constitute it.

**EVD-5 — Identity Independence.** Identity may reference Evidence. Identity SHALL NEVER become Evidence; Evidence SHALL NEVER become Identity.

**EVD-6 — Semantic Independence.** Business meaning — receipt, photograph, audit log, invoice, contract, certificate, signature, sensor reading — SHALL NOT alter the constitutional identity of Evidence.

### Relationships

Evidence may participate in constitutional relationships with Events, Subjects, Objects, Places, Relationships, Identity, and Representation. These relationships establish constitutional structure only and SHALL NOT imply truth, ownership, authenticity, legal status, or business meaning.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define Verification algorithms, Truth determination, Confidence, Probability, Trust, Authenticity, digital signatures, cryptography, hashes, audit systems, logging systems, observation mechanisms, sensor technologies, evidence collection, chain of custody, legal admissibility, compliance, storage, databases, APIs, messaging, security, access control, workflows, business documents, or file formats for Evidence.

# 5. Structural Constitutional Constructs

Identity, Relationships, and Attributes are not Constitutional Primitives. They do not constitute Reality. They exist solely to organize how the five primitives relate to Representation, to each other, and to descriptive detail.

## 5.1 Identity

### Definition

> *This section has been substantively rewritten from the original ZRM-002 draft, which defined Identity as intrinsic and independent of Representation — directly contradicting ZRM-000 v1.1's Constitutional Dictionary entry. ZRM-000 is the higher authority on reserved terminology; this section now conforms to it.*

Identity **is** a Structural Constitutional Construct representing the relationship between a Primitive's intrinsic Uniqueness and one or more Representations that refer to it.

Uniqueness belongs inherently to the Primitive itself and exists independently of every Representation.

Identity does not create that uniqueness.

Identity is the constitutional relationship that binds an already unique Primitive to one or more Representations.

Identity **points** to Reality.

Identity never constitutes Reality.

---

### Constitutional Nature

Identity does not constitute Reality — the Primitive does. Identity organizes how that Primitive is referred to. Every Primitive that has been represented possesses exactly one constitutional Identity relationship per Representation lineage it participates in; a single Primitive may accumulate multiple such relationships over time (e.g., multiple identifiers issued across its history), but this document does not mandate a single permanent Identity per Primitive — that cardinality question belongs to downstream constitutions (e.g., the Reality Mathematics tier), consistent with North Star v6.0's own model of multiple Identities converging on one Referent.

### Characteristics

Identity:

- exists as a relationship between a Primitive and one or more Representations;
- does not describe, classify, verify, or authenticate a Primitive;
- does not itself determine semantic meaning;
- applies equally to Subject, Object, Place, Event, and Evidence.

### Invariants

**ID-1 — Structural Nature.** Identity SHALL remain a Structural Constitutional Construct and SHALL NOT be treated as a Primitive.

**ID-2 — Relational Dependence.** Identity SHALL NOT be claimed to exist independently of Representation. Where no Representation exists, no Identity relationship exists yet for that lineage.

**ID-3 — Attribute Independence.** Attributes describe constitutional constructs; Identity relates them to Representations. No Attribute SHALL determine which Identity relationship applies, and no change to any Attribute SHALL sever an existing Identity relationship.

**ID-4 — Relationship Independence.** Structural Relationships (§5.2) connect constitutional constituents; they SHALL NOT create, modify, replace, merge, or divide Identity relationships.

**ID-5 — Identity vs. Identifier.** An **Identifier** is the specific token, string, or symbol within a Representation that concretely instantiates an Identity relationship. Identity is the constitutional relationship; an Identifier is its representational instance. No Identifier alone constitutes the full Identity relationship it participates in.

**ID-6 — Semantic Independence.** Identity possesses no constitutional semantic meaning. It SHALL NOT imply Customer, Employee, Product, Vehicle, Sensor, Invoice, Asset, Organization, or User — semantic interpretation belongs exclusively to downstream constitutions.

### Relationships

Identity exists in relation to Subject, Object, Place, Event, and Evidence. Identity is referenced by Representations and used by Relationships to distinguish participating constitutional constituents.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define UUIDs, database primary keys, URIs, DIDs, QR codes, RFID tags, cryptographic hashes, digital certificates, usernames, account numbers, business identifiers, authentication, authorization, storage, databases, APIs, indexing, lookup mechanisms, naming, labels, semantic categories, or business meaning for Identity.

## 5.2 Relationships

### Definition

A Relationship is a Structural Constitutional Construct — classified as a **Structural Relationship**, not a Primitive — that establishes a structural connection between two or more constitutional constituents of Reality. Relationships do not create constitutional constituents; they connect constituents that already exist. Relationships neither constitute nor modify Reality; they express structural connectivity within it.

### Characteristics

Relationships connect constitutional constituents; preserve the Identity relationships of every participating constituent; and exist independently of Representation, semantic interpretation, implementation technology, and Observation. Relationships SHALL NOT create or destroy constitutional constituents; merge, divide, or modify constitutional Identity; determine semantic meaning; or perform execution.

### Invariants

**REL-1 — Relationship Integrity.** A Relationship SHALL remain a Relationship throughout its constitutional existence.

**REL-2 — Identity Preservation.** Relationships SHALL preserve the constitutional Identity relationships of every participating constituent. No Relationship SHALL create, merge, divide, replace, or modify them.

**REL-3 — Primitive Independence.** Relationships SHALL NOT create constitutional Primitives. Every participating constituent SHALL exist independently before participating in a Relationship.

**REL-4 — Representation Independence.** Relationships exist independently of every Representation. Representations may describe Relationships; they SHALL NEVER constitute them.

**REL-5 — Semantic Independence.** Relationships possess no constitutional semantic meaning; business meaning belongs exclusively to downstream constitutions.

**REL-6 — Technology Independence.** Relationships SHALL remain independent of all execution mechanisms and storage technologies — graph databases, foreign keys, references, object pointers, APIs, storage engines, communication protocols.

**REL-7 — Structural Neutrality.** Relationships SHALL connect constitutional constituents without implying ownership, control, permission, dependency, hierarchy, causality, or sequence. Such interpretations belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.

### Relationships (permitted pairings)

Relationships MAY connect any pairing among Subject, Object, Place, Event, and Evidence, including a primitive with another instance of its own type (Subject↔Subject, Object↔Object, etc.). Relationships SHALL reference constitutional Identity and SHALL NOT depend upon Representation, Attributes, other Relationships, semantic classification, or implementation mechanisms for their own constitutional existence.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define ownership, parent-child structures, hierarchies, permissions, roles, capabilities, dependencies, business relationships, semantic relationships, graph traversal, query languages, graph databases, foreign keys, object references, APIs, storage, serialization, messaging, workflows, execution, or state transitions for Relationship.

## 5.3 Attributes

### Definition

An Attribute is a Structural Constitutional Construct — not a Primitive — that describes exactly one aspect of exactly one constitutional construct (a Primitive or a Relationship). Attributes describe constitutional existence; they do not constitute it, and possess no independent constitutional existence of their own.

### Characteristics

Attributes describe and characterize constitutional constructs, express one descriptive aspect, belong to exactly one construct, and exist independently of Representation, semantic interpretation, and implementation technology. Attributes remain distinct from Identity and Relationships. Attributes SHALL NOT distinguish Identity, create or destroy constitutional constructs, connect constructs, classify constructs, determine semantic meaning, perform execution, or imply business meaning.

### Invariants

**ATR-1 — Attribute Integrity.** An Attribute SHALL remain an Attribute throughout its constitutional existence.

**ATR-2 — Construct Dependence.** Every Attribute SHALL belong to exactly one constitutional construct and SHALL NOT exist independently.

**ATR-3 — Identity Independence.** No Attribute SHALL create, replace, merge, divide, or modify an Identity relationship. Two constitutional constructs MAY possess identical Attributes while remaining constitutionally distinct through their respective Identity relationships; likewise, a construct MAY change every Attribute it possesses while its Identity relationships remain unaffected.

**ATR-4 — Relationship Independence.** No Attribute SHALL create, replace, modify, or eliminate a Relationship; no Relationship SHALL replace the descriptive function of an Attribute. When an Attribute belongs to a Relationship, it describes the Relationship itself, not the participating constructs.

**ATR-5 — Representation Independence.** Attributes exist independently of every Representation. Representations may describe Attributes; they SHALL NEVER constitute them.

**ATR-6 — Mutability.** Attributes MAY change during the constitutional existence of the construct they describe. Such changes SHALL NOT affect Identity relationships.

**ATR-7 — Semantic Independence.** Attributes possess no constitutional semantic meaning — Customer, Employee, VIP, Active, Premium, etc. are business interpretations belonging exclusively to downstream constitutions.

**ATR-8 — Technology Independence.** Attributes SHALL remain independent of all implementation technologies — database columns, object properties, JSON fields, XML elements, programming-language members, storage schemas, serialization formats, APIs, messaging protocols.

### Relationships

Attributes SHALL describe exactly one constitutional construct — Subject, Object, Place, Event, Evidence, or Relationship — and SHALL NOT describe multiple constructs simultaneously. Multiple constructs MAY possess Attributes with identical descriptive values; such similarity SHALL NOT imply shared Identity.

### Non-Scope

This document SHALL NOT define names, labels, titles, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, prices, salaries, status, customer types, business categories, ownership, permissions, roles, capabilities, validation rules, units of measurement, business meaning, database columns, programming-language properties, JSON fields, XML elements, storage schemas, serialization, APIs, execution logic, or workflows for Attribute.

# 6. Ontology Completeness & Constitutional Primitive Test

## 6.1 Completeness

The Constitutional Ontology consists exclusively of:

- **Constitutional Primitives:** Subject, Object, Place, Event, Evidence
- **Structural Constitutional Constructs:** Identity, Relationships, Attributes

Together these constitute the complete constitutional foundation for representing Reality. Every constitutional representation SHALL derive exclusively from these constructs. No additional constitutional category is required.

## 6.2 Minimality and Sufficiency

Every Constitutional Primitive is irreducible: each defines a unique constitutional category that cannot be expressed as any combination of the remaining Primitives or Structural Constructs. Removing any Primitive makes some aspect of Reality constitutionally unrepresentable.

Every constitutional phenomenon SHALL be representable exclusively through one or more Primitives together with Identity, Relationships, and Attributes. Reality requires no additional constitutional foundation. Nothing outside this ontology may serve as a constitutional basis for Reality.

## 6.3 Constitutional Primitive Test

A proposed new Constitutional Primitive SHALL satisfy every one of the following. Failure of any single requirement permanently disqualifies the proposal.

**CPT-1 — Existence Independence.** The proposed Primitive SHALL exist independently of every existing Primitive and Structural Construct.

**CPT-2 — Irreducibility.** The proposed Primitive SHALL NOT be representable as any composition of Subject, Object, Place, Event, Evidence, Identity, Relationships, or Attributes.

**CPT-3 — Constitutional Necessity.** The proposed Primitive SHALL represent a category of Reality that cannot otherwise be represented.

**CPT-4 — Observation Independence.** The proposed Primitive SHALL exist regardless of whether it has been observed.

**CPT-5 — Representation Independence.** The proposed Primitive SHALL exist regardless of any Representation. Representations may describe Reality; they SHALL NOT create it.

**CPT-6 — Semantic Independence.** The proposed Primitive's constitutional meaning SHALL be independent of business interpretation, domain terminology, or human convention.

**CPT-7 — Technology Independence.** The proposed Primitive's constitutional meaning SHALL remain valid regardless of technology, implementation, or computational system.

**CPT-8 — Cross-Domain Universality.** The proposed Primitive SHALL remain constitutionally valid across every domain of Reality — physical, biological, digital, social, and future forms of existence.

**CPT-9 — Constitutional Demonstration.** A formal constitutional demonstration SHALL establish that Reality becomes constitutionally incomplete without the proposed Primitive. Failure to demonstrate this permanently rejects the proposal.

## 6.4 Constitutional Closure

The Constitutional Ontology is closed. No additional Primitive exists beyond the five defined in §4. No additional Structural Constitutional Construct exists beyond the three defined in §5. Every future constitutional concept SHALL derive exclusively from this ontology, except through the amendment path in §6.5.

## 6.5 Primitive Amendment Requirements

A new Constitutional Primitive MAY be introduced only through formal amendment of ZRM-001 and this document, requiring: successful completion of the Constitutional Primitive Test (§6.3); a formal constitutional demonstration of incompleteness; a constitutional impact assessment; a migration strategy for all downstream constitutions; and unanimous Constitutional Council approval. Failure of any requirement rejects the amendment.
