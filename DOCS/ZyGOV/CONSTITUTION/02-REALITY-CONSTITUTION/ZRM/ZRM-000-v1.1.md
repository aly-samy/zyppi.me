# 1. Purpose
 
ZRM-000 establishes the constitutional language, terminology, naming conventions, and writing standards for every document that belongs to the Zyppi Reality Model (ZRM).
 
Its purpose is to ensure that every constitutional document speaks the same language, assigns exactly one meaning to every concept, and remains timeless, technology-independent, and internally consistent.
 
ZRM-000 governs language.
 
It does not define Reality itself.

## Rule 3.1 — Parent Non-Binding

A document SHALL NOT bind its own completeness to the identity of a specific subordinate document.

ZRM-000 sits at the root of the ZRM document series. It governs the terminology used by every ZRM-tier document that follows it — ZRM-001, ZRM-002, and all future numbered documents in the series.

Because of this, ZRM-000 SHALL NOT reserve a term by naming a specific downstream ZRM document as that term's authoritative home. Doing so would make the parent's own correctness contingent on which document happens to occupy a given number — a dependency that breaks the moment the ZRM series is renumbered, split, merged, or reordered, none of which should ever require amending ZRM-000 itself.

A reserved term MAY instead cite:

- a document **above** ZRM-000 in the authority chain (e.g. North Star, Founding Principles), since referencing a superior authority is always permitted, or
- a general constitutional **tier or domain** (e.g. "Reality Mathematics," "Capability Constitution") rather than a specific document number, leaving the exact document that fulfills that tier free to be assigned, renamed, or restructured without touching this document.

This rule is why Section 6 does not name ZRM-001, ZRM-002, or any other specific ZRM-series document number.
  
# 4. Constitutional Writing Principles
 
## Rule -1 — Constitutional Independence
 
ZRM describes Reality.
 
It does not describe software.
 
It does not describe databases.
 
It does not describe programming languages.
 
It does not describe AI.
 
It does not describe APIs.
 
It does not describe implementation.
 
Reality exists independently of every technology.
 
Technology must adapt to Reality.
 
Reality shall never be adapted to technology.
  
## 4.1 Reality First
 
Every sentence shall describe Reality before describing any representation of Reality.
  
## 4.2 Technology Independence
 
No constitutional statement shall depend upon any specific technology, platform, language, protocol, storage engine, database, framework, or AI model.
  
## 4.3 Timelessness
 
Every constitutional statement shall remain valid regardless of technological evolution.
  
## 4.4 Precision
 
Every important term shall possess exactly one constitutional meaning.
 
Synonyms shall not be used interchangeably.
  
## 4.5 Minimalism
 
Every sentence shall communicate exactly one constitutional idea.
 
Complexity shall emerge through composition rather than verbosity.
  
## 4.6 Language Serves Reality
 
Language exists to describe Reality.
 
Reality never changes to satisfy language.
 
Language shall never redefine Reality.

## 4.7 Single Authority

Every constitutional concept SHALL possess exactly one authoritative defining document.

Other constitutional documents MAY reference or restate a concept for contextual or terminological convenience but SHALL NOT redefine, contradict, or compete with its authoritative definition.

This principle preserves semantic consistency throughout the constitutional corpus and ensures that every concept has a single constitutional source of truth.
  
# 5. Constitutional Dictionary
 
## Existence
 
Existence is the property of being part of Reality.
 
Anything that exists possesses a state at some Valid Time, whether observed or not.
  
## Reality
 
Reality is the actual state of existence.
 
Reality exists independently of every Observer.
 
Reality precedes every Representation.
 
Reality is the ultimate constitutional authority.
  
## Observer
 
An Observer is any entity capable of acquiring Evidence about Reality.
 
Observers may include humans, sensors, instruments, AI systems, or future forms of observation.
 
The definition of an Observer is independent of technology.
  
## Observation
 
Observation is the act by which an Observer acquires Evidence about Reality.
 
Observation changes the Observer's Knowledge.
 
Observation never changes Reality.
  
## Representation
 
A Representation is any recorded description, identifier, measurement, symbol, model, abstraction, or record referring to Reality.
 
Representations may be:
 
 
- Accurate
 
- Incomplete
 
- Uncertain
 
- Conflicting
 
- False
 
A Representation is never Reality itself.
  
## Event

An Event is an occurrence or change in Reality involving one or more constitutional primitives.

Events exist independently of observation.

Events become part of Zyppi's constitutional information model only when supported by recorded Evidence.

**Derivation**

Event is one of the five constitutional primitives established by the North Star's Universal Reality Model and formally represented by the Information Architecture Bible.

This definition is restated here solely for terminological consistency.
  
## Evidence

Evidence is the immutable record that attests to an Event.

Evidence does not create Events.

Evidence enables Verification.

Multiple independent Evidence records MAY attest to the same Event.

**Derivation**

Evidence is one of the five constitutional primitives established by the North Star's Universal Reality Model and formally represented by the Information Architecture Bible.

This definition is restated here solely for terminological consistency.
  
## Information
 
Information is recorded Observation.
 
Information has not necessarily been verified.
 
Information becomes Knowledge through verification.
  
## Knowledge
 
Knowledge is verified understanding of Reality derived from Evidence.
 
Knowledge evolves as new Evidence becomes available.
 
Reality does not.
  
## Truth

Truth exists independently of software.

Software cannot determine absolute Truth.

Within Zyppi, software performs Verification over available Evidence and derives Trust from verified Evidence.

Truth therefore remains a philosophical property of Reality rather than a computational primitive.
  
## Confidence
 
Confidence expresses certainty regarding Evidence or Knowledge.
 
Confidence never changes Reality.
  
## Trust
 
Trust is derived from verified Evidence.
 
Trust is never stored.
 
Trust is never part of Reality.
 
Trust is an emergent conclusion.
  
## Valid Time
 
Valid Time is the time at which an Event occurred in Reality.
  
## Observation Time
 
Observation Time is the time at which Evidence was acquired by an Observer.
 
Observation Time and Valid Time are distinct constitutional concepts.
  
## Identity

Identity is a Semantic Projection derived from verified Evidence.

Identity is not a constitutional primitive and is not part of Reality itself.

Identity represents the platform's current confidence that a particular Subject is authorized to control, represent, or interact with a particular Object or Place.

The constitutional semantics of Identity are defined by the Information Architecture Bible.
