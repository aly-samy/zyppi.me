## 1. Purpose

Every other ZRM document defines Reality. This one defines how the documents *about* Reality are allowed to change. It exists because a constitution that can't say how it evolves eventually evolves badly — by ad hoc convention, by whoever drafted last, or (as the mining exercise repeatedly demonstrated) by the same ID being reused for two different things three documents apart.

## 2. Scope

Governs: constitutional lifecycle, authority and citation rules, amendment, supersession, deprecation, versioning, document granularity, ID/registry numbering, dependency rules, and the re-derivation procedure that Rule 3.2 requires. Does not govern the mathematical or semantic content of any other ZRM document — this document has no opinion on what Identity or Intent *is*, only on how documents that define such things are managed.

# Part I — Governance

## 3. Constitutional Lifecycle

Every constitutional document SHALL progress through exactly these states, in this order, with no skipping:

```
Draft → Council Review → S6 Falsification → Ratification → Freeze
```

- **Draft** — content exists, not yet reviewed.
- **Council Review** — multi-party review for consistency, citation accuracy, and scope compliance.
- **S6 Falsification** — adversarial validation per the S6 framework (§13). No document proceeds past this state without a completed campaign.
- **Ratification** — Chair-level acceptance following a clean or accepted-with-amendments S6 result.
- **Freeze** — the document becomes immutable. Further change requires either an Amendment (§5) or a full Re-Derivation (§12), never a silent edit.

No document may claim Ratified or Frozen status without evidence of having passed through S6 Falsification specifically — a clean Council Review is not a substitute.

## 4. Constitutional Authority

Authority is organized into Tiers (roadmap §1), not into per-document dependency lists. Restated here as the governing rule rather than the roadmap's summary of it:

- A document's `Authority:` header MAY cite a Tier, but only when the Tier being cited is fully ratified and stable, and only for the coarse, document-level declaration.
- Any individual clause, theorem, or derivation inside a document MUST cite the specific source clause or theorem it depends on — never a Tier. This is not a style preference; S6 falsification and consistency audits need something concrete to attack, and "depends on Tier 2" gives them nothing to check.
- A document may never cite a Tier or document *below* its own tier as authority. Authority flows downward only, per ZRM-000 §3 and Rule 3.1.

## 5. Amendment Procedure

An Amendment modifies a Frozen document without triggering a full Re-Derivation, when the change is additive or corrective rather than foundational.

**Eligibility.** A change qualifies as an Amendment only if it does not alter any already-ratified theorem's truth value, does not change any axiom, and does not remove content another Frozen document depends on. Anything else requires Re-Derivation (§12).

**Procedure.** Proposal → Council Review → S6 Falsification of the amendment specifically (not the whole document) → Ratification → the amendment is appended with its own version marker; the base document's original ratified content is never rewritten in place.

**Precedent already in the corpus:** WS-01A's Amendment A-001 (Intelligence Single-Root Model) and WS-01A's second Amendment A-001 (Relationship Reality Principle) both followed exactly this shape before this document existed to require it. Nothing new is being invented here — it's being made mandatory and explicit.

## 6. Supersession Procedure

Supersession replaces an entire document's authority with a new one, rather than amending it in place.

**When it applies:** a newer document in the same numbered position (ZRM-002 v2.0 superseding ZRM-002A/B/C), or a Tier 1/2 document absorbing a previously independent document (this document absorbing CMP-001; ZRM-002 absorbing its historical A/B/C predecessors).

**Requirement:** every superseded document SHALL receive a formal notice — not silent removal — recording what superseded it and why. ZRM-002's own Historical Freeze Record is the model to follow. CMP-001 receives an equivalent notice upon this document's ratification.

## 7. Deprecation & Versioning

**Deprecation** marks a construct as no longer recommended for new use while remaining valid for historical interpretation — this mirrors WS-04B's own Predicate Lifecycle (`PROPOSED → UNDER_REVIEW → ACTIVE → DEPRECATED → RETIRED`), generalized here as the standard lifecycle for any constitutional construct, not just predicates.

**Versioning:** minor version increments (1.0 → 1.1) are backward-compatible additions; major version increments (1.x → 2.0) are breaking changes requiring the full Re-Derivation procedure. This mirrors the Version Compatibility Matrix discipline already established in the ZRM Implementation Roadmap for compiler versions — applied here to constitutional documents themselves.

# Part II — Constitutional Integrity

## 8. Document Granularity Principle

*(Established in the roadmap, restated here as its permanent home.)*

A document splits into separate constitutional artifacts (e.g., ZRM-003A/003B) only when its responsibilities are genuinely orthogonal and each half is independently falsifiable — not merely large. A document consolidates multiple sub-documents into one (e.g., ZRM-002 absorbing its A/B/C predecessors) when fragmentation has produced redundant or drifting definitions of the same concept. Size alone justifies neither move.

## 9. Constitutional ID / Registry Numbering Policy

This section exists because mining the legacy corpus surfaced four distinct numbering failures, all avoidable with a single rule the legacy corpus never had:

- ZRM-002's ratified Identity invariants ID-4/5/6 (Relationship Independence, Identity vs. Identifier, Semantic Independence) collided with legacy Stage-3B axioms independently labeled ID-4/5/6 for different concepts (Representation Independence, Attribute Independence, Relationship Independence).
- Time Mathematics theorem candidates were renumbered mid-pipeline — TH-T002 meant "Temporal Precedence" at Discovery and "Concurrency" by the final Registry Freeze.
- A legacy Time Mathematics document's own header called itself "ZRM-005," colliding with the actual, unrelated ZRM-005 (Semantic Projection).
- Three different, mutually inconsistent "003A/B/C/D" schemes existed across the legacy corpus for entirely different partitions.

---

### **RULE ID-1 — Single Central Registry.** 
Every constitutional ID — theorem, axiom, invariant, document number, cluster code — SHALL be checked against one central registry before assignment. No document may mint an ID independently, the same way RI-001's ZE-ID Allocator (M-504) already enforces this discipline for registry entities. This document extends that existing pattern from entities to constitutional mathematics.

### **RULE ID-2 — Candidate Labels Are Not IDs.** 
During Discovery (per ZRM-003A §8's Proof Forms and the DP-1–DP-8 discovery principles), a theorem candidate carries a temporary, local, disposable label only. It receives a permanent ID from the Central Registry only at Ratification — never before. This is what would have prevented the Time Mathematics renumbering problem: a candidate that gets renamed during purification was never supposed to have had a permanent ID yet.

### **RULE ID-3 — Permanence.** 
Once a Central Registry ID is assigned, it is never reused, never reassigned to a different concept, and never removed — even if the theorem it names is later superseded. This mirrors WS-02 §9's already-ratified rule for ZE identifiers ("Identifiers are immutable. Identifiers may never be reused. Deleted entities remain reserved forever.") extended to theorems and axioms.

### **RULE ID-4 — Format.** 
Provisional working format, pending a dedicated registry implementation: `ZT-[FAMILY]-[SEQUENCE]` for theorems (e.g. `ZT-ID-001` for the first permanently-registered Identity theorem), `ZA-[FAMILY]-[SEQUENCE]` for axioms, `ZD-[DOCUMENT]` for document-level identifiers. Family codes themselves (ID, AT, RL, GR, TP, TM, ST, PJ, TR, CN, EV, EVD, …) are registered once and never reused for a different family.

### **RULE ID-5 — Document-Level Discipline Too.** 
The same rule applies to document numbers, not just theorem numbers — a document draft SHALL check the Central Registry before referring to itself by any ZRM-00X label, precisely to prevent the "this Time Mathematics document calls itself ZRM-005" collision from recurring.

---

Every theorem currently marked with provisional IDs in ZRM-003B and ZRM-004 (`ID-T01`, `TM-T01`, `ST-T01`, `EV-T01`, etc.) is provisional under RULE ID-2 until it clears S6 falsification and receives its permanent Central Registry ID at Ratification.

## 10. Dependency Rules

A document's dependency on another SHALL be:

- **Downward only** — no document depends on anything in a higher tier or an earlier document number within its own tier that hasn't yet been ratified.
- **Explicit** — stated in the `Depends on` line, not left implicit.
- **Non-circular** — if Document B depends on Document A, Document A SHALL NOT depend on anything in Document B, even indirectly. This was flagged as a live risk during ZRM-005/006 drafting (whether ZRM-003B needs a concept only defined in ZRM-005) and resolved there by keeping the abstract Projection Function in ZRM-003B — this rule is why that resolution was necessary, not optional.

## 11. Authority & Citation Rules

Every derivation, theorem, or axiom SHALL carry an explicit citation to the specific clause it derives from — a document name and section number at minimum, per the pattern ZRM-002 and ZRM-003A/B already established (*"Derived from: ZRM-001 Law I..."*). A restatement without a citation is indistinguishable from an independent (and therefore unauthorized) redefinition, per ZRM-000 Rule 4.7 (Single Authority).

Citations to a Tier (§4) are permitted only at the document header level, never at the clause level — this rule and §4 are the same rule stated from two angles; it's stated twice because citation drift was the single most common defect found during mining, and it deserves to be impossible to miss.

## 12. Document Reopening / Re-Derivation Procedure

The operational mechanics of Rule 3.2 (Constitutional Evolution), which this document formally houses:

1. Identify what the document being reopened actually depends on — the specific clause, not a tier (§4, §11).
2. Remove concepts that no longer possess constitutional authority under the current, ratified parent documents.
3. Rewrite surviving concepts using current constitutional terminology — never preserve legacy naming for compatibility's own sake.
4. Re-derive all higher-order constructs from the current constitution, not from the document's own prior version.
5. Simplify wherever constitutional clarity improves, per the Document Granularity Principle (§8).
6. Validate against the current Global Consistency Audit findings.
7. Run a full S6 Falsification campaign for the reopened document.
8. Ratify under a new version number, following the Deprecation & Versioning rule (§7).
9. Freeze. The prior version receives a Supersession notice (§6), never silent deletion.

This is the exact sequence already applied, in practice, to ZRM-003A/B and ZRM-004/005 during the mining exercise — codified here so future reopenings don't have to reconstruct the method from precedent each time.

## 12A. Legacy Corpus Boundary (Sunset Clause)

Upon ratification of the ZRM constitutional corpus (ZRM-000 through ZRM-008), all pre-ZRM constitutional documents (WS series, RI series, P4-000, SEC-001, POL-001, RI-006, SDK/Platform specs, CMP-001) are reclassified as **Historical Evidence**.

**Historical Evidence:**
- possesses zero constitutional authority
- SHALL NOT be cited to justify, override, or fill a gap in any ZRM-tier document
- MAY be consulted for provenance during a Re-Derivation (§12) — recovering a specific proof, rationale, or design decision — but the recovered content has no standing until it clears the Re-Derivation procedure and is ratified into its proper tier

**This is a rule about authority, not about content erasure.** The Tier 3 Legacy Revision Pass (roadmap Phase 5) still applies: WS-series taxonomies, cluster models, and registry rules are not duplicated into ZRM-000 through ZRM-006 by design, and remain intended, planned content for Tier 3 once re-derived. A concept absent from Tier 2 is not "constitutionally nonexistent" — it is **without independent authority** until it is formally specialized from a Tier 2 derivation (per ZRM-006 §6, Constitutional Extension Rules) or introduced through the Amendment Procedure (§5).

## 12B. Dependency Cascade Audit

A Re-Derivation (§12) of any Tier 1 or Tier 2 document SHALL trigger a mandatory **Review Required** status on every document that explicitly depends on it, immediately and automatically — not at the discretion of whoever performs the re-derivation.

**Procedure:**
1. Identify every document whose `Depends on` line cites the reopened document, directly or transitively.
2. Mark each as Review Required — this status blocks that document from being cited as stable authority (§4) until cleared.
3. Each flagged document SHALL be checked against the reopened document's new content before Review Required is lifted — this may result in no change, a citation update, or its own Re-Derivation.
4. Only once every cascaded document clears Review Required does the original reopened document return to Frozen status.

This is the same discipline software already applies when a base interface changes and every dependent module must be recompiled and re-tested — applied here to constitutional documents instead of code.

## 12C. Constitutional Authority Is Human-Only

Constitutional Lifecycle progression (§3) specifically at the **Ratification** and **Freeze** stages requires Human authority. This is absolute and does not weaken as the platform becomes more agentic.

AI (including Claude, in its Governance & Semantic Architecture role) MAY:
- propose amendments and drafts
- perform Council Review
- execute S6 Falsification
- identify gaps, contradictions, and dependency risks

AI SHALL NEVER:
- ratify a constitutional document
- freeze a constitutional document
- become a constitutional authority in its own right

The constitution is the human contract that governs the machine. It is never the reverse. This principle is permanent and is not subject to the Amendment Procedure (§5) — it may only be changed, if ever, through a process outside this document's own authority to define, since a document cannot be the sole author of the limits on its own authority.

# Part III

## 13. Relationship to S6

S6 is a parallel constitutional subsystem, not a subsection of this document. ZRM-007 defines the *lifecycle rule* that requires S6 Falsification (§3); S6 itself defines *how* falsification is performed (Laboratories, Evaluation Criteria, Failure Classification, Rejection Cascade — see the S6 Methodology document). Keeping S6 separate avoids bloating this document with operational detail that will change independently of governance rules, per the same Document Granularity Principle (§8) that justified splitting ZRM-003 into 003A/003B.

## 14. Constitutional Boundaries

This document SHALL NOT define:

- The mathematical or semantic content of any other ZRM document
- S6's internal methodology (Laboratories, Evaluation Criteria, Failure Classification) — see the S6 Methodology document
- Runtime, Policy, Security, or Federation governance — Tier 5's own documents
- Anything about Domain (Tier 3) or Implementation (Tier 4) constitutions beyond the boundary already fixed by ZRM-006
