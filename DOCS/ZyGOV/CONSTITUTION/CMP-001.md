# CMP-001 — Constitutional Master Plan
 
## The Constitutional Evolution Framework for Zyppi
 
**Document ID:** CMP-001 **Version:** Draft v0.1 **Status:** Draft for Ratification **Classification:** Constitutional Governance Document **Authority:** AI Constitutional Council **Depends On:** Founding Principles, NORTH-STAR, ZRM v1.1, Constitutional State of the Union v1.1
  
# 1. Purpose
 
CMP-001 defines **how the Zyppi Constitution evolves**.
 
While the Constitution defines the architecture of Zyppi, this document governs the architecture **of the Constitution itself**.
 
It establishes the permanent governance model that directs every future constitutional phase, amendment, roadmap, roundtable, and ratification.
 
This document SHALL remain independent of individual implementation projects.
 
It governs constitutional evolution only.
  
# 2. Mission
 
The mission of CMP-001 is to guarantee that Zyppi evolves without sacrificing:
 
 
- Ontological consistency
 
- Deterministic execution
 
- Constitutional traceability
 
- Cryptographic reproducibility
 
- Architectural coherence
 
- Long-term maintainability
 

 
Every future constitutional document SHALL conform to this Master Plan.

# 4. Constitutional Evolution Philosophy
 
The Constitution evolves according to five immutable principles.
 
## Principle 1 — Preserve Reality
 
Reality SHALL remain immutable.
 
New constitutions MAY extend execution but SHALL NOT redefine reality.
  
## Principle 2 — Extend, Never Replace
 
Every constitutional phase SHALL build upon previous phases.
 
Rewrites are prohibited.
 
Supersessions SHALL preserve compatibility whenever possible.
  
## Principle 3 — Determinism Before Capability
 
New capabilities SHALL NEVER reduce determinism.
 
Functionality is subordinate to constitutional correctness.
  
## Principle 4 — Security Before Convenience
 
Security SHALL be designed before runtime behavior.
 
No execution document may weaken constitutional trust guarantees.
  
## Principle 5 — Explainability By Design
 
Every constitutional decision SHALL remain explainable through constitutional artifacts.
 
Explainability is not a feature.
 
It is a constitutional property.
