# 1. Purpose

This document translates Zyppi's constitutional vision into executable product decisions.

The North Star explains **why Zyppi exists.**

The Founding Principles explain **how Zyppi must evolve.**

The PRD explains:

- What we build
- Who we build for
- Which capabilities we prioritize
- How customers adopt the platform
- How products evolve
- How engineering makes trade-offs

Every product decision must strengthen the constitutional mission of Zyppi.

---

# 2. Product Thesis

Software understands data.

It does **not** understand reality.

Businesses already have:

- ERPs
- CRMs
- CMSs
- Warranty systems
- Dealer portals
- Identity providers
- AI systems

What they lack is a trusted execution layer between the physical world and those systems.

" **Zyppi provides that missing layer.** "

Every physical identity becomes:

- Observable
- Context-aware
- Routable
- Verifiable
- Auditable
- AI-ready

Our strategy is simple:

> Users discover Zyppi through simple tools.

↓

> They remain because routing creates intelligence.

↓

> They expand because identity, trust, and execution become infrastructure.

# 3. Product Strategy

Deliver immediate value within minutes.

- GS1 Digital Link Generator

- Warranty QR
- Product Landing Page

**Goal:**
```

```
Time to First Value should be under five minutes.

Reveal intelligence created by usage.

**Examples:**

- Context Routing
- Event History
- Analytics
- Routing Policies
- Product Identity Management
- Health Monitoring

**Goal:**
```
Retention
```
Customers realize Zyppi is more than a QR generator.

---

## Phase 3 — Infrastructure

Become embedded in enterprise operations.

**Examples:**

- APIs
- Integrations
- Event Ledger
- Policy Engine
- Trust Services
- AI Capabilities
- Enterprise Workflows

**Goal:**
```
Dependency
```
Removing Zyppi becomes operationally expensive.
