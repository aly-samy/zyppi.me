# NORTH STAR v7.0
 
## Zyppi — The Universal Trust Execution Platform
 
**Brand Expression:** **Zyppi — Reality Sync**
 
**Version:** 7.0 **Status:** **RATIFIED — CLOSED** **Ratification Date:** 25 August 2026 **Supersedes:** NORTH STAR v6.0 **Classification:** Foundation **Normative Level:** Vision **Amendment Class:** Clarification and Expression **Locking Authority:** Chair
 
**Change Summary:** Introduces **Reality Sync** as the North Star abstraction for the verifiable relationship Zyppi establishes between Reality and the digital world, and **Zyppify** as its human expression. This revision clarifies and polishes the existing North Star without changing Zyppi’s constitutional Reality model, Universal Execution Pipeline, Trust model, Moat, AI Native principle, or Platform Principles.
  
# Vision
 
The digital world cannot directly perceive reality.
 
Software cannot know whether a Subject is genuine, whether an Object is authentic, whether a Place is legitimate, or whether an Event truly happened.
 
AI cannot safely act without trusted Evidence.
 
**Zyppi exists to bridge this gap.**
 
We enable software and AI to perceive reality, verify trust, and transform trusted interactions into secure, intelligent, and auditable actions.
  
# The Problem
 
Today's software operates inside digital systems.
 
Reality exists outside them.
 
The physical world is largely invisible to software.
 
As a result:
 
 
- AI hallucinates because it lacks trusted reality.
 
- Businesses duplicate verification everywhere.
 
- Identity is fragmented.
 
- Objects cannot prove authenticity.
 
- Places cannot express context.
 
- Every organization rebuilds trust independently.
 

 
The digital world is disconnected from reality.
 
More fundamentally, digital systems are not inherently accountable to the changing state of Reality they represent.
  
# Our Belief
 
Every economic system is fundamentally built on trust.
 
Money is trust. Identity is trust. Ownership is trust. Contracts are trust. Payments are trust. Commerce is trust.
 
AI is only as trustworthy as the reality it can verify.
 
The future economy will not be built on information.
 
It will be built on **verifiable trust.**
  
# Our Mission
 
**Enable the digital world to perceive reality, verify trust, and execute actions with confidence.**
  
# Reality Sync
 
**Zyppi synchronizes digital understanding with verifiable Reality.**
 
Reality changes through Events.
 
Events leave Evidence.
 
Observation acquires Evidence.
 
Verification tests that Evidence.
 
Trust is derived from what can be substantiated.
 
**Reality Sync** is the North Star abstraction for maintaining a verifiable relationship between Reality and the digital systems that must understand and act upon it.
 
Reality remains authoritative.
 
The digital representation remains accountable to Reality — never the reverse.
 
Reality Sync does **not** mean:
 
 
- copying Reality into a database;
 
- claiming software can determine absolute truth;
 
- guaranteeing that an observed claim is genuine;
 
- continuously replicating Reality into digital systems;
 
- requiring real-time synchronization, polling, or streaming;
 
- introducing a new constitutional primitive or execution stage.
 

 
Reality Sync may be established or updated whenever relevant Evidence becomes available and may be expressed through real-time, event-driven, periodic, on-demand, or future mechanisms without altering its constitutional meaning.
 
Its purpose is simple:
 
 
**Keep digital understanding accountable to what Reality can verifiably support.**
 
  
# Zyppify
 
**To Zyppify is to bring something into, or update it within, a verifiable digital relationship with Reality so Trust can be computed and appropriate Action can safely follow.**
 
**Zyppification** is the human expression of the Reality Sync transformation Zyppi exists to enable.
 
Zyppification does not replace any constitutional primitive, lifecycle stage, semantic construct, policy boundary, or technical contract.
 
It is not itself an execution step.
 
It does not mean merely to:
 
 
- scan;
 
- identify;
 
- register;
 
- resolve;
 
- verify;
 
- authenticate;
 
- certify;
 
- approve;
 
- generate a QR code;
 
- create a digital twin;
 
- invoke a particular API operation.
 

 
Any of those activities may participate in a Zyppi-governed interaction, but none alone defines Zyppification.
 
Zyppification does not mean making something true or trustworthy.
 
A Zyppi-governed interaction may conclude that Evidence is insufficient, a claim is false, an authority is invalid, standing is absent, or an intended Action must be denied.
 
Such an outcome does not represent failure of Reality Sync.
 
It represents Reality Sync functioning correctly.
 
**Reality Sync and Zyppify create no new runtime stage, data model, API operation, persistence entity, or implementation requirement.**
 
They express the purpose of the constitutional machinery that already exists.
  
# What Zyppi Really Is
 
Zyppi is **not** a QR platform. Zyppi is **not** an NFC platform. Zyppi is **not** an identity platform. Zyppi is **not** an AI platform.
 
Zyppi is the constitutional execution layer that connects:
 
**Reality → Trust → Intent → Action**
 
Reality Sync describes the foundational relationship that makes this progression possible.

- Is this Subject real?

# Universal Reality Model
 
Every interaction Zyppi perceives exists within **five constitutional dimensions.**
 
## Subject
 
An autonomous constituent of reality — anything capable of initiating, holding standing, or acting with intent.
 
Includes:
 
 
- Human
 
- Organization (acting through delegated human authority)
 
- AI Agent
 
- Autonomous System
 
- Future autonomous entities not yet invented
 

 
Every Human is a Subject. Not every Subject is a Human.
 
This broadening is intentional: Zyppi's AI Native principle already assumes AI Agents carry standing and authority comparable to humans within constitutional bounds — Subject is the name for the category both belong to.
 
Concerned with:
 
 
- Identity
 
- Authority
 
- Standing
 
- Delegation
 
- Credentials
 
- Trust
 

  
## Object
 
A non-autonomous constituent of reality that participates through relationship with a Subject, not through its own intent.
 
Includes:
 
 
- Products
 
- Assets
 
- Devices
 
- Documents
 
- Vehicles
 
- Packages
 
- Digital Twins
 

 
Concerned with:
 
 
- Authenticity
 
- Ownership
 
- Lifecycle
 

  
## Place
 
The spatial or contextual environment within which Subjects and Objects exist and interact.
 
Includes:
 
 
- Stores
 
- Hospitals
 
- Airports
 
- Factories
 
- Borders
 
- Warehouses
 
- Homes
 

 
Concerned with:
 
 
- Jurisdiction
 
- Context
 
- Presence
 

  
## Event
 
A change occurring within reality, involving one or more Subjects, Objects, or Places.
 
Events are how reality becomes knowable at all — nothing is perceived except through something happening.
 
An Event is not itself proof; it is the occurrence that Evidence later attests to.
  
## Evidence
 
The trace an Event leaves behind — the material from which Trust is computed.
 
Evidence may originate from:
 
 
- QR
 
- NFC
 
- Government credentials
 
- Verifiable Credentials
 
- BLE
 
- GPS
 
- Biometrics
 
- Digital Signatures
 
- IoT
 
- Sensors
 
- Enterprise Systems
 
- Future trust technologies
 

 
Zyppi is carrier-agnostic.
 
Any trusted Evidence becomes a constitutional input.
 
Reality emerges from the relationships between all five dimensions across time.

# Universal Trust Model

Zyppi is carrier-agnostic.
 
Any trusted Evidence becomes a constitutional input, regardless of which Subject, Object, or Place it concerns.
 
Reality Sync therefore does not manufacture Trust.
 
It maintains the verifiable relationship through which Trust can be computed from available Evidence.
