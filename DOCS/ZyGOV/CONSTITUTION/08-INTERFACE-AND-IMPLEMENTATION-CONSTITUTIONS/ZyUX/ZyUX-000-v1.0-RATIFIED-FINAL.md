## Brand Continuity Rule

This document is an **Unfict-era** ratified document. The canonical `ZyUX-*` identifier is retained to preserve citation, governance, repository, and historical continuity. Legacy documents are cited by their actual historical titles. In active prose, **Unfict** is the master brand.

Brand succession does not alter constitutional meaning. No business, epistemic, Authority, Standing, Policy, Trust, Evidence, Runtime, Receipt, API, or historical semantics are changed merely because the public master brand changed.

# 0. Purpose and Constitutional Position

`ZyUX-000` establishes the master experience architecture for **Unfict**.

Unfict is intentionally deep under the hood. Its users SHALL NOT be required to carry that depth cognitively.

> **Easy in front. Deep, governed, and auditable underneath.**

ZyUX exists so radically different Subjects, organizations, relationships, Jobs, hosts, applications, and eventual domains can share one experience doctrine without becoming:

- a maze of permanent menus;
- a collection of unrelated persona products;
- a conventional RBAC console pretending to be the product model;
- a consumer social-identity network;
- a separate semantic system per host or interface;
- a separate UX constitution per domain;
- a universal dashboard exposing the entire platform.

ZyUX governs how already-governed concepts are **experienced**. It does not redefine Reality, Subject, Object, Place, Event, Evidence, Identity, Authority, Standing, Policy, Trust, Runtime, Registry Truth, Receipt, or Capability semantics.

> **ZyUX is the experience projection layer through which Unfict's constitutional depth becomes simple, contextual, authority-aware, and useful.**

## Identity Authority Boundary

ZyUX does **not** own Identity semantics.

Identity cardinality, Identity relationship topology, referent resolution, Representation lifecycle, consolidation/supersession, and Identity mutation are imported from the **currently operative constitutional Identity authority**.

`ZRM-ID-AMD-001 v0.9.1` may inform future alignment but, while it remains draft, this series SHALL NOT treat it as active law. Ratification of this series does not ratify that amendment.

Accordingly, ZyUX speaks normatively about **Subject continuity, authentication/access continuity, current relationships, current Authority/Standing, disclosure, and historical attribution** without inventing Identity lifecycle law.

# 1. Experience Vocabulary

For experience purposes:

- **Subject** — the constitutional actor/autonomous constituent according to the governing ontology.
- **Participant** — a product term for a Subject currently participating in a governed relationship, interaction, or process; not a new Reality primitive.
- **User** — a human or software Subject directly operating an authorized interface.
- **Account** — an access-management construct; not the Subject and not Identity.
- **Authentication Context** — a login/credential/IdP context through which access may be established.
- **Relationship** — a governed connection that may supply context; it does not itself grant permission.
- **Role / Capacity** — human-readable context for how a Subject acts; it does not self-execute Authority.
- **Authority** — governed empowerment to establish, approve, delegate, alter, or cause something.
- **Standing** — whether a Subject currently qualifies to exercise relevant Authority.
- **Disclosure Scope** — the governed boundary of what may be revealed to the current Subject/context.
- **Experience Context** — the bounded Job/object/relationship/place/time/host/application situation that determines what is useful now.
- **Capability** — a governed Unfict ability exposed through authorized surfaces.
- **Affordance** — the visible UX control through which a capability may be invoked; it is not the capability itself.

# 2. Governing Experience Equation

The experience SHALL NOT be determined by a static persona alone.

```text
EXPERIENCE
=
SUBJECT
× JOB
× RELATIONSHIP CONTEXT
× APPLICABLE AUTHORITY
× CURRENT STANDING
× APPLICABLE POLICY
× DISCLOSURE SCOPE
× CURRENT CONTEXT
× INTERFACE / HOST
```

This is a conceptual product abstraction, not a replacement authorization formula.

Two safeguards are mandatory:

1. **Relationship is context, not permission.**
2. **Authority need not be delegated.** A Subject may act under valid Authority it directly holds where governing law permits. ZyUX SHALL NOT manufacture synthetic self-delegation.

# 3. Master Experience Laws

## ZyUX-L01 — Invisible Complexity

> **Constitutional rigor belongs underneath the experience. Users interact with familiar Jobs, understandable actions, clear reasons, and appropriate proof.**

## ZyUX-L02 — Contextual Capability Disclosure

> **Unfict SHALL ordinarily reveal only the capabilities, controls, settings, information, and proof legitimately relevant to the current Job and governed context.**

Disclosure SHALL NOT be inferred merely from product availability, relationship existence, host presence, role label, or navigation state.

## ZyUX-L03 — Minimum Necessary Onboarding

> **Unfict SHOULD ask only for information necessary to establish the current legitimate access/context and reach first useful value.**

Do not front-load departments, roles, modules, dashboards, industries, or permission matrices where legitimate context already supplies the answer.

## ZyUX-L04 — Object / Context First

> **The experience SHOULD begin from what the Subject is working with and what they need to do, not from a permanent inventory of platform modules.**

## ZyUX-L05 — Progressive Explanation

> **Where explanation is useful, Unfict SHOULD reveal depth progressively: Answer / Action → Reason → Basis → Proof.**

## ZyUX-L06 — Scope-Bounded Visibility

> **A Subject SHALL see only information, relationships, controls, capabilities, and proof legitimately disclosable within the current governed scope.**

## ZyUX-L07 — Bidirectional Scope Isolation

> **Scope isolation applies in every direction. No organization, Subject, partner, host, administrator, regulator, or agent gains unrelated visibility merely because a governed relationship exists.**

## ZyUX-L08 — Existing IAM Coexistence

> **Enterprise authentication SHOULD remain native to the enterprise where appropriate. Unfict SHALL NOT require replacement of a legitimate existing IdP merely to participate.**

## ZyUX-L09 — Subject Continuity; Authority Change

> **Credential, job, employer, role, department, geography, Standing, Authority, and host changes SHALL NOT by themselves replace the underlying Subject or rewrite historical attribution.**

Any effect on Identity / Representation relationships remains owned by the operative Identity authority.

## ZyUX-L10 — No Deep Menu as Complexity Storage

> **Navigation depth SHALL NOT be the primary mechanism for containing platform complexity.**

## ZyUX-L11 — Host-Native Bias

> **Where practical, Unfict SHOULD meet the user in the system where the Job already occurs instead of forcing migration into a standalone workspace.**

## ZyUX-L12 — Surface Semantic Parity

> **Human UI, REST, SDK, MCP, agent, public Resolution, and Host-Native surfaces MAY differ in expression, but SHALL NOT become different semantic systems.**

## ZyUX-L13 — Frictionless Entry

> **Signup, login, invitation acceptance, joining, claiming, and continuation from a scan/deep link SHOULD be as direct as legitimately possible.**

## ZyUX-L14 — Federation Is Additive

> **Federation may compound value, but Day-1 utility SHALL NOT depend on an already-populated network where legitimate standalone value can be delivered.**

## ZyUX-L15 — Universal Architecture, Narrow GTM

> **ZyUX MAY model many domains and participant types internally without authorizing simultaneous market expansion across those domains.**

## ZyUX-L16 — Self-Execution Preservation

> **Where a Subject legitimately holds Authority to act directly, the experience SHALL permit self-execution without inventing a delegation relationship merely to fit an organizational UX model.**

Delegation is one source/form of Authority, not the universal prerequisite for action.

# 4. Front-Door Doctrine

The canonical entry family is:

```text
Sign up / Log in
        ↓
Create / Join / Claim / Accept invitation / Continue from scan
        ↓
First useful action
```

If the initiating context already establishes the intended path, the interface SHOULD continue it rather than re-ask what is already legitimately known.

Public information SHALL remain accountless where authentication is unnecessary.

# 5. Organization Doctrine

Organizations SHALL NOT be assumed to be one permanent tree or one person's account.

ZyUX must accommodate, without changing doctrine:

- sole traders;
- small organizations;
- multinationals;
- government authorities;
- regulators;
- agencies;
- contractors;
- consortia;
- logistics providers;
- cross-organization programs;
- future organizational forms.

Organizational visibility and control SHALL be bounded by applicable Authority, Standing, Policy, and disclosure rules—not by the mere existence of a relationship or a role label.

# 6. Contextual Experience Doctrine

The user SHOULD navigate the **Job**, not the platform.

A useful contextual sequence is:

```text
ARRIVAL
  ↓
CONTEXT ANCHOR
  ↓
RELEVANT INFORMATION
  ↓
DISCLOSABLE CAPABILITIES
  ↓
CURRENT ACTION STATE
  ↓
RESULT / NEXT CONTEXT
```

Global control planes are reserved for genuinely cross-context Jobs.

# 7. Progressive Explanation Doctrine

Unfict SHALL preserve distinctions such as:

```text
Information ≠ Evidence
Evidence ≠ Verified Evidence
Verification ≠ Trust
Trust ≠ Policy
Policy ≠ Authorization
Authorization ≠ Execution
Execution ≠ Physical Reality
Receipt ≠ Omniscient proof of Reality
Unknown ≠ False
Unavailable ≠ False
Conflicting ≠ Resolved
```

Experience simplification SHALL NOT erase these boundaries.

# 8. Host-Native Doctrine

> **Integrate with the titan. Do not rebuild the titan.**

Host context is input and distribution context. It is not Authority, Trust, Reality, or semantic ownership merely because it came from SAP, Shopify, an ERP, PIM, PLM, WMS, government system, IdP, or another established host.

# 9. Lifecycle Doctrine

Current state may change completely without rewriting what was historically true, authorized, observed, decided, or executed.

The experience SHALL distinguish current actionability from historical attribution.

Ending one relationship SHALL NOT automatically end unrelated contexts.

# 10. Series Architecture

The ratified horizontal foundation is:

```text

ZyUX-001 — Identity, Account & Entry Experience
ZyUX-002 — Organization, Relationship, Scope & Delegation Experience
ZyUX-003 — Contextual Navigation & Capability Disclosure
```

Archetype/application profiles MAY be created later, including Developer, Organization Admin, Public Observer, Customer, Agency, Operator, Compliance, Auditor, and Agent profiles.

Profiles inherit `ZyUX-L01` through `ZyUX-L16` and SHALL NOT redefine them.

# 11. Interface / Capability Boundary

Every exposed Unfict capability SHALL resolve to a governed semantic boundary beneath all authorized interfaces.

A surface MAY adapt expression.

It SHALL NOT independently implement or strengthen business, epistemic, Evidence, Trust, Policy, authorization, failure, or Receipt semantics.

# 12. Marketing Boundary

The internal experience architecture may use examples from pets, agriculture, logistics, government, commerce, healthcare, legal, devices, or other future domains to test universality.

Those examples SHALL NOT be interpreted as authorization to market or build all domains simultaneously.

# 13. Success Criteria

ZyUX success should be measured through outcomes including:

- time to first useful action;
- onboarding completion;
- task completion;
- wrong-context action rate;
- permission/authorization confusion;
- navigation depth;
- time to locate legitimate action;
- safe delegation;
- safe offboarding;
- proof comprehension;
- host workflow displacement;
- developer time-to-first-success;
- cross-scope leakage incidents;
- accessibility of dynamic state changes.

# 14. Drift Prohibitions

ZyUX SHALL NOT become:

- a design-system-only series;
- a visual style guide;
- a persona-marketing folder;
- a duplicate ontology;
- a duplicate Authority/Identity/Trust system;
- a universal dashboard mandate;
- a menu tree representing the platform;
- a reason to expose all controls;
- a semantic fork per host;
- a justification for broad GTM expansion without evidence.

# 15. Downstream Conformance

Downstream application, DPP, Resolution, Host, API, and public-experience documents SHALL consume this doctrine rather than create parallel experience laws.

Pending drafts such as CAW-DPP may remain drafts, but before their own ratification/implementation they SHALL align with:

- Identity non-ownership;
- Role ≠ permission;
- disclosure ≠ invocation;
- requester-context non-inference;
- Answer/Reason/Basis/Proof;
- one governed capability semantic boundary;
- lifecycle/historical separation.

# 16. Ratification
