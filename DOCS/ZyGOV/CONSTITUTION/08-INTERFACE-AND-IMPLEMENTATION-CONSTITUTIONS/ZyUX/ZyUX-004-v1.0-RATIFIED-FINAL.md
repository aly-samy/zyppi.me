# 0. Scope

`ZyUX-004` governs how Unfict explains what it knows, does not know, what supports a conclusion, what Policy/Authority/Standing mattered, what was executed, and what a Receipt proves—without overwhelming ordinary users or overstating certainty.

> **Answer simply first. Explain honestly second. Prove precisely when needed.**

# 1. Explanation Stack

```text
LEVEL 1 — ANSWER / ACTION
What should I know or do?

LEVEL 2 — REASON
Why is Unfict saying this?

LEVEL 3 — BASIS
What Evidence / Policy / Authority / time basis supports it?

LEVEL 4 — PROOF
Show reconstructible provenance, evaluation, and Receipt where legitimately disclosable.
```

No Subject is automatically entitled to every layer.

# 2. Binding Laws

## ZyUX-004-S01 — Answer First, Proof on Demand

Unfict SHOULD provide the smallest useful answer first and allow legitimate progressive inspection.

## ZyUX-004-S02 — Never Simplify by Lying

Unknown, unavailable, unverified, conflicting, inferred, interpreted, authorized, executed, and physically occurred states SHALL NOT be collapsed into stronger certainty.

## ZyUX-004-S03 — Evidence Presence Is Not Verification

The existence/upload of Evidence SHALL NOT be presented as verification.

## ZyUX-004-S04 — Trust Is Not Authorization

A trusted result SHALL NOT be presented as permission to act unless applicable Authority/Policy separately establishes that permission.

## ZyUX-004-S05 — Policy ALLOW Is Not Trust

An ALLOW decision SHALL NOT imply underlying Evidence is trusted.

## ZyUX-004-S06 — Authorization Is Not Execution

Permission to act SHALL NOT be displayed as proof action occurred.

## ZyUX-004-S07 — Execution Is Not Physical Reality

A digital execution record SHALL NOT be presented as universal proof of an external physical event.

## ZyUX-004-S08 — Receipt Is Governed Process Proof

Receipt presentation SHALL reflect what the Receipt actually proves about Unfict's governed process, attributable inputs, decision/execution, and references—nothing stronger.

## ZyUX-004-S09 — Material Uncertainty Must Be Visible

Missing, stale, degraded, conflicting, or uncertain basis SHALL surface at an appropriate experience depth where material.

## ZyUX-004-S10 — Conflict Is Not Silently Resolved

Conflicting Evidence remains conflicting until a proper authority/process resolves it.

## ZyUX-004-S11 — Trust Degradation Should Be Explainable

Where disclosure permits, users SHOULD be able to inspect material factors reducing confidence/trust.

## ZyUX-004-S12 — Explanation Is Scope-Bounded

The right to receive an answer SHALL NOT automatically grant access to every source, Evidence record, relationship, identity detail, policy secret, or Authority chain behind it.

## ZyUX-004-S13 — Public Explanation Is Minimum Necessary

Public surfaces SHOULD expose only the basis necessary for correct comprehension without leaking protected internals.

## ZyUX-004-S14 — Historical Explanation Uses Historical Basis

Historical actions SHALL be explained from the Authority, Standing, Policy, Evidence, versions, and valid-time context that applied then—not current state.

## ZyUX-004-S15 — Same Meaning Across Surfaces

Human UI, REST, SDK, MCP, host extensions, public Resolution, and audit views may differ in depth but SHALL preserve underlying meaning.

# 3. Epistemic State Vocabulary

Experience SHALL preserve:

```text
UNKNOWN ≠ FALSE
UNAVAILABLE ≠ FALSE
UNVERIFIED ≠ VERIFIED
CONFLICTING ≠ RESOLVED
INFERRED ≠ OBSERVED
INTERPRETED ≠ REALITY
AUTHORIZED ≠ EXECUTED
EXECUTED ≠ PHYSICALLY OCCURRED
```

# 4. Evidence Presentation

Evidence MAY be projected progressively:

```text
Summary
→ Material Evidence
→ Evidence State
→ Provenance
→ Full Record (if disclosable)
```

Counts must be literal. Do not label all Evidence `verified` unless each item actually satisfies the governing verification semantics.

# 5. Freshness and Time

Where material, distinguish:

```text
valid time
observation/retrieval time
last verification/check time
policy version
source version
```

Stale Evidence should not look current.

# 6. Source Language

Where useful, distinguish:

```text
Observed by sensor
Reported by supplier
Asserted by user
Verified against registry
Inferred by governed process
```

Source/provenance changes meaning.

# 7. “Why Can't I?”

A blocked action may be explained as:

- required Evidence missing;
- approval required;
- Standing expired;
- current Policy denies action;
- service unavailable;
- relationship ended;

The explanation SHALL remain within disclosure scope.

# 8. Proof Disclosure

Experience-level proof scopes MAY include Public, Relationship-scoped, Organization-scoped, Privileged, Audit-only, or Non-disclosable classifications if aligned with governing policy.

The exact taxonomy remains owned by the applicable data/disclosure authority; ZyUX does not create universal privacy law.

# 9. AI Explanation Boundary

AI MAY summarize governed results where authorized. AI-generated explanation SHALL NOT become Evidence, override authoritative fields, or hide uncertainty/conflict.

# 10. Explicit Non-Scope

This document does not define Trust algorithms, Evidence admissibility, Receipt schema, policy engine, authorization engine, Runtime, cryptographic verification, replay mechanics, legal evidentiary status, or court admissibility.

# 11. Drift Prohibitions

Do not drift into:

- one green check for everything;
- Evidence = truth;
- uploaded = verified;
- Trust = authorization;
- Policy ALLOW = TRUSTED;
- authorized = executed;
- executed = occurred;
- Receipt = physical truth;
- unknown/unavailable = false;
- conflict = generic error;
- inference = observation;
- AI summary = Evidence;
- raw Evidence dump as default UX;
- proof access bypassing disclosure.
