## Identity Gate — CLOSED FOR ZYUX BY NON-OWNERSHIP

The ratified series does not rely on `ZRM-ID-AMD-001 v0.9.1` being operative.

It permanently defers Identity topology/cardinality/lifecycle to the currently operative Identity authority and speaks normatively about Subject continuity, access, Authority, disclosure, and historical attribution only.

Therefore ZyUX ratification neither assumes nor ratifies the draft amendment.

Any future operative Identity amendment is consumed through this boundary without automatically reopening ZyUX unless it changes experience requirements.

## CAW-DPP Corrections — DOWNSTREAM CONFORMANCE GATE

CAW-DPP remains a future execution draft and is not an authority over ZyUX.

Before CAW-DPP ratification or implementation, it must conform to ratified ZyUX, including:

- role/capacity does not self-authorize;
- requester-context absence does not infer privilege;
- Identity wording follows operative Identity authority;
- verification does not equal Authority;
- access classes are inputs, not grants;
- DPP human/host surfaces import ZyUX-003/004/005/006 as applicable.

These pending downstream corrections do not block the horizontal foundation.

# 4. Locked Foundation Invariants

The ratified series locks the following experience invariants:

1. **Easy in front; deep and governed underneath.**
2. **Subject continuity is distinct from credentials, roles, jobs, and current access.**
3. **ZyUX does not own Identity semantics.**
4. **Relationship and role provide context; they do not self-grant Authority or visibility.**
5. **Self-held Authority does not require synthetic delegation.**
6. **Disclosure and invocation are separate axes.**
7. **Organizational scope is Authority/Policy bounded.**
8. **No hidden master role or universal recovery superuser.**
9. **Navigation is not authorization.**
10. **The user navigates the Job, not platform complexity.**
11. **Answer → Reason → Basis → Proof is progressive and scope-bounded.**
12. **Trust, Policy, authorization, execution, physical Reality, and Receipt are not interchangeable.**
13. **Host-native means native experience, not host-owned semantics.**
14. **One governed capability semantic boundary serves all interfaces.**
15. **Historical attribution can persist after current access ends.**
16. **Universal experience architecture does not authorize universal GTM.**

# 5. Supersession

The complexity belongs underneath; meaning remains with its constitutional owner; history remains reconstructible; and every interface remains a faithful projection of governed capability.**
