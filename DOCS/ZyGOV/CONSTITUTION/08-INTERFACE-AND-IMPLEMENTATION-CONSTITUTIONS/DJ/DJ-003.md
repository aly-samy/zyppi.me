# DJ-003 — Developer Journey 003
 
## Transfer Ownership
 
**Document ID:** DJ-003 **Layer:** Layer 4 — Developer Platform **Status:** Canonical Developer Journey **Depends On:** DJ-001, DJ-002, DEV-ARCH-001, ZRM, ARM, CL, POL, SEC, RI
  
# 1. Purpose
 
This journey teaches developers how to securely transfer the ownership of a digital or physical asset using Zyppi.
 
Unlike Product Authentication, which answers **"Is this product genuine?"**, and the Digital Product Passport, which answers **"What is this product?"**, this journey answers:
 
 
**"Who legitimately owns this product right now?"**
 
 
Ownership transfer is one of the most common lifecycle operations supported by Zyppi and demonstrates how constitutional policies, identity, attestation, and the Runtime work together to produce trusted state transitions.
  
# 2. Business Problem
 
Modern products change hands many times during their lifecycle.
 
Examples include:
 
 
- Retail purchases
 
- Warranty registration
 
- Second-hand marketplaces
 
- Equipment leasing
 
- Vehicle sales
 
- Luxury goods
 
- Collectibles
 
- Industrial assets
 

 
Organizations need a trusted way to record these transfers without relying on isolated databases or manual verification.

# 7. Typical Workflow
 
A customer purchases a product.
 
The merchant's application:
 
 
- verifies the product
 
- confirms merchant authority
 
- identifies the buyer
 
- requests a transfer
 
- receives a signed execution receipt
 

 
The buyer immediately becomes the recognized owner according to the current Reality View.
