# DJ-004 — Developer Journey 004
 
## Execute a Blueprint
 
**Document ID:** DJ-004 **Layer:** Layer 4 — Developer Platform **Status:** Canonical Developer Journey **Depends On:** DJ-001, DJ-002, DJ-003, DEV-ARCH-001, RSN, POL, SEC, RI
  
# 1. Purpose
 
This journey teaches developers how to execute a Zyppi Blueprint from their applications.
 
Unlike previous journeys, which focus on individual operations, this journey introduces **orchestration**.
 
A Blueprint coordinates multiple constitutional capabilities into a governed business workflow while guaranteeing deterministic execution, policy compliance, and auditable outcomes.
 
The developer does not manually orchestrate complex logic.
 
The developer simply requests the execution of a Blueprint.
  
# 2. Business Problem
 
Real business operations rarely consist of a single action.
 
Examples include:
 
 
- Product recall
 
- Warranty claim
 
- Product onboarding
 
- Equipment inspection
 
- Digital passport publishing
 
- Product certification
 
- Factory quality approval
 
- Customs clearance
 
- Asset retirement
 

 
Each workflow involves multiple validations, policies, observations, and decisions.
 
Blueprints package these into reusable business capabilities.

# 7. Blueprint Philosophy
 
A Blueprint is a governed business workflow.
 
It defines:
 
 
- required inputs
 
- required identities
 
- required permissions
 
- policy checkpoints
 
- execution sequence
 
- expected outputs
 

 
Applications invoke Blueprints rather than implementing constitutional logic themselves.
