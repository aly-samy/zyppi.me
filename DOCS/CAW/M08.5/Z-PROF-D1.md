# 1. Purpose  This document records the final constitutional closure of 
**Dimension 1 — Constitutional Fit** of the Z-PROF investigation.  

The question of D1 was:  
> **Does Z-PROF fit inside the existing Zyppi constitutional architecture, and exactly where does its authority begin and end?**  

D1 establishes the constitutional jurisdiction, prohibitions, ownership boundaries, and invariants that all subsequent Z-PROF dimensions and the final `Z-PROF-001` Constitution SHALL respect.  

D1 does not define implementation technology, implementation contracts, or the complete Profile Composition model.  

Those matters belong to the subsequent dimensions and the final integrated Z-PROF Constitution.

# 2. Final Constitutional Finding  
The investigation establishes:  
> **Z-PROF is constitutionally compatible with Zyppi provided that it remains a governed composition, interrogation, interpretation, and domain-participation mechanism subordinate to the existing constitutional substrate.**  

Z-PROF is **not**:  
- a new Reality model; 
- - a new ontology; 
- - a replacement for ARM; 
- - a replacement for Semantic Projection; 
- - a Reasoning engine; 
- - a Policy engine; 
- - a Security/Trust system; 
- - a Runtime; - a Registry; 
- - an Evidence authority; 
- - or a competing constitutional receipt system.  
- 
- The fundamental constitutional direction is:

Z-PROF binds existing constitutional capabilities.

# 4. D1 Findings
 
## F1 — Z-PROF Does Not Require a New Ontology
 
The existing ZRM and ARM architecture provides the ontological substrate required by Z-PROF.
 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT introduce alternative foundational primitives.
 
The five Constitutional Primitives remain:
 
 
- Subject;
 
- Object;
 
- Place;
 
- Event;
 
- Evidence.
 

 
Z-PROF operates over these existing constitutional concepts.
  
## F2 — Z-PROF SHALL NOT Duplicate Semantic Projection
 
Semantic Projection is already established as a constitutional capability within the ZRM/PRJ architecture.
 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT create a competing Semantic Projection Engine.
 
The constitutional relationship is:
 `ZRM / PRJ     │     │ Constitutional projection capability     ▼ Z-PROF     │     │ governed profile/domain declarations,     │ mappings and composition     ▼ Domain / Projection ` 
ZRM/PRJ owns the underlying projection capability.
 
Z-PROF may govern its authorized application to a domain.
  
## F3 — ARM-001 Already Establishes the Profile Concept
 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT redefine the fundamental meaning of Profile.
 
ARM Profile remains the constitutional specialization of ARM for an Asset Class.
 
Its fundamental question is:
 
 
**What kind of Asset Reality is this?**
 
 
Z-PROF addresses a different question:
 
 
**How does existing Reality participate in a particular governed domain or use case?**
 
 
This distinction is fundamental to preventing Profile Explosion.
  
## F4 — Profiles Remain Subordinate and Isolated
 
The existing ARM Profile rules remain binding.
 
Profiles SHALL:
 
 
- extend rather than redefine ARM;
 
- remain independently governed;
 
- remain isolated from other Profiles;
 
- not acquire constitutional authority merely because they are created first;
 
- not modify canonical Reality.
 

 
The first domain/application SHALL NOT acquire constitutional privilege.
 
In particular:
 `GS1 ≠ Product Profile DPP ≠ Product Profile Customs ≠ Product Profile Logistics ≠ Product Profile ` 
These represent domain participation, projection, interpretation, or composition over existing Reality.
  
## F5 — Domain Outputs Remain Derived
 
GS1, DPP, Customs and future domain representations remain derived representations of Reality.
 
They SHALL NOT become canonical Reality.
 
The constitutional direction is:
 `Reality    ↓ Projection / Interpretation    ↓ Domain Participation ` 
Never:
 `Domain    ↓ Reality ` 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT convert a domain representation into canonical Reality merely because a domain requires it.
  
## F6 — POL Remains Authorization Authority
 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT become a Policy or Permission engine.
 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT:
 
 
- authorize;
 
- deny;
 
- override;
 
- weaken;
 
- reinterpret;
 
- or supersede
 

 
a POL decision.
 
A domain composition may declare a Policy dependency.
 
A CompositionManifest may reference governed Policy requirements.
 
The authority remains with POL.
  
## F7 — SEC Remains Security and Trust Authority
 
Z-PROF SHALL consume SEC-governed mechanisms for:
 
 
- identity;
 
- trust;
 
- attestation;
 
- provenance;
 
- integrity;
 
- security boundaries.
 

 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT create:
 
 
- an alternative identity model;
 
- an alternative trust model;
 
- an alternative attestation authority;
 
- an alternative cryptographic authority.
 

  
## F8 — RI Remains Execution Authority
 
Z-PROF SHALL NOT become a Runtime.
 
RI remains responsible for constitutional execution.
 
Z-PROF may supply validated declarative requirements and references.
 
The Runtime SHALL continue to receive explicit inputs and remain subject to its existing constitutional purity requirements.
 
D1 therefore establishes:
 `Application / Assembly           │           │ explicit inputs           ▼          RI           │           │ constitutional execution           ▼  Constitutional Result           │           ▼        Z-PROF           │           ▼  Domain Interpretation ` 
The precise relationship between future Z-PROF artifacts and RI/RSN remains a subsequent architectural question and SHALL NOT be inferred from D1.
  
## F9 — CEngS SHALL Mechanically Enforce the Separation
 
The distinction between constitutional responsibilities SHALL NOT exist merely as documentation.
 
Where applicable, CEngS SHALL mechanically enforce:
 
 
- package boundaries;
 
- dependency direction;
 
- Runtime purity;
 
- prohibited infrastructure dependencies;
 
- constitutional ownership boundaries.
 

 
Architectural intent that cannot be mechanically protected is insufficient for long-term constitutional stability.
  
## F10 — The Genuine Z-PROF Gap Is Governed Domain Composition and Interpretation
 
D1 establishes that the genuine architectural gap is not another Runtime, Registry, Policy engine, Security system, or Reality model.
 
The strongest candidate responsibility for Z-PROF is:
 
 
**Governed, reusable, domain-specific composition, interrogation, and interpretation over existing constitutional Reality.**
 
 
This is the narrowest formulation consistent with the existing constitutional substrate and the future need to support many domains.

# 6. Golden Question Invariant
 
The **Golden Question** is adopted as a vertical semantic invariant across the Z-PROF architecture:
 
 
**Who did what, to whom, where, when, and how do we know?**
 
 
Its constitutional mapping is:
 
  
 
Golden Question
 
Zyppi Constitutional Dimension
 
   
 
**Who?**
 
Subject
 
 
 
**Did what?**
 
Event
 
 
 
**To whom?**
 
Object
 
 
 
**Where?**
 
Place
 
 
 
**When?**
 
Valid Time
 
 
 
**How do we know?**
 
Evidence
 
  
 
Time remains a constitutional dimension expressed through **Valid Time**.
 
It does not become a sixth foundational primitive.
  
# 7. Naked Reality Principle
 
The Golden Question SHALL NOT be interpreted as a requirement to produce a complete-looking answer at any cost.
 
The constitutional requirement is the opposite:
 
 
**The answer SHALL never be stronger than the Reality and Evidence supporting it.**
 
 
Therefore:
 `UNKNOWN       ≠ FALSE UNAVAILABLE   ≠ FALSE CONFLICTING   ≠ RESOLVED INFERRED      ≠ OBSERVED INTERPRETED   ≠ REALITY AUTHORIZED    ≠ OCCURRED ` 
If Reality establishes that an event occurred, governance does not erase that event.
 
If an event was unauthorized, its unauthorized status is a governance fact; it does not negate the occurrence itself.
 
If Evidence is unavailable, Zyppi SHALL preserve the absence of evidence rather than fabricate certainty.
  
# 8. Governance Is Not Reality
 
Authority, jurisdiction, authorization, security and governance protect and constrain participation in Reality.
 
They do not constitute Reality itself.
 
Therefore:
 
 
**Whether an event was authorized does not determine whether the event happened.**
 
 
The architecture SHALL preserve the distinction between:
 `Reality:     What happened?  Governance:     Was it permitted?  Security:     Who is entitled to interact with it?  Reasoning:     What can legitimately be concluded?  Experience:     How should the established result be presented? ` 
No governance layer may rewrite ontological history to make an authorization decision appear to be a Reality statement.
  
# 9. Temporal Separation
 
The Golden Question's **When** refers to **Valid Time**:
 
 
when the Event occurred in Reality.
 
 
It SHALL NOT be silently replaced by:
 
 
- Runtime execution time;
 
- observation time;
 
- ingestion time;
 
- registry time;
 
- presentation time.
 

 
Those may be independently relevant temporal dimensions, but they do not replace Valid Time when answering the Reality question.
 
Future Runtime and replay architecture SHALL preserve this distinction.
  
# 10. Epistemic Separation
 
Z-PROF SHALL preserve the distinction between:
 `Reality     ≠ Evidence     ≠ Interpretation     ≠ Policy Decision     ≠ Execution Result ` 
A Reasoning output may establish a legitimate conclusion.
 
It does not thereby become the Event that it interprets.
 
A Policy decision may establish whether an action is authorized.
 
It does not thereby become evidence that the underlying action occurred.
 
An ExecutionReceipt may establish that Zyppi executed a governed composition.
 
It does not automatically become proof of the domain event itself.
  
# 11. Experience Boundary
 
The Golden Question is ultimately experienced by the user, but Z-PROF does not become a UI system.
 
The Experience/Application layer is responsible for rendering governed results.
 
However, downstream presentation SHALL preserve the Golden Question invariant.
 
The user-facing experience SHALL NOT:
 
 
- hide material uncertainty;
 
- transform `UNKNOWN` into `FALSE`;
 
- transform `UNAVAILABLE` into `FALSE`;
 
- present Interpretation as Reality;
 
- present Policy as Reality;
 
- present Execution as Event;
 
- invent missing dimensions of the Golden Question.
 

 
The constitutional requirement is therefore:
 
 
**Simple presentation is permitted. Semantic simplification that changes Reality is not.**
 
  
# 12. The Golden Question as a Vertical Invariant
 
The Golden Question SHALL be traceable through the architectural lifecycle.
 `Domain Requirement         │         ▼ Domain Template / Composition         │         │ What must be known?         ▼ Constitutional Interrogation         │         ▼ Existing Reality / Evidence         │         ▼ Governed Projection / Reasoning         │         ▼ Domain Result         │         ▼ Experience         │         ▼ Golden Question Answer ` 
At each stage, the system may refine representation.
 
It SHALL NOT increase the epistemic strength of the underlying Reality.

# 19. Final D1 Constitutional Position
 
The central conclusion of D1 is:
 
 
**Z-PROF is viable because the problem it solves is smaller than the creation of another constitutional system.**
 
 
Zyppi already has constitutional Reality, Asset Reality, Projection, Reasoning, Policy, Security and Runtime foundations.
 
The missing capability is the governed mechanism through which these existing capabilities may participate coherently in domain-specific applications without being duplicated or semantically contaminated.
 
Therefore:
 `Z-PROF SHALL NOT CREATE REALITY.  Z-PROF SHALL NOT REDEFINE REALITY.  Z-PROF SHALL NOT BECOME THE AUTHORITY OF REALITY.  Z-PROF SHALL NOT AUTHORIZE ACCESS TO REALITY.  Z-PROF SHALL NOT REPLACE CONSTITUTIONAL EXECUTION.  Z-PROF SHALL NOT CREATE A PARALLEL SECURITY MODEL.  Z-PROF SHALL NOT CREATE A COMPETING ONTOLOGY.  Z-PROF SHALL NOT MAKE DOMAIN INTERPRETATIONS CANONICAL.  Z-PROF SHALL COMPOSE GOVERNED CONSTITUTIONAL CAPABILITIES FOR DOMAIN PARTICIPATION. ` 
The Golden Question provides the vertical semantic discipline:
 
 
**Who did what, to whom, where, when, and how do we know?**
 
 
The answer must remain grounded in naked Reality.
 
Governance may determine whether an action was permitted.
 
Security may determine who may access it.
 
Reasoning may determine what may legitimately be concluded.
 
Experience may determine how clearly the result is presented.
 
None of these may rewrite what Reality establishes.
