# 1. Purpose

`M08.5-PLAN` defines the controlled implementation-governance path for the Z-PROF architecture established through the completed Z-PROF investigation corpus.

Its purpose is to convert the Council's ratified semantic and architectural decisions into a disciplined implementation sequence without allowing the planning artifact itself to become an implementation mandate.

The plan establishes:

1. the implementation sequence;
2. the architectural boundaries that SHALL remain invariant;
3. the relationship between existing constitutional substrate and Z-PROF;
4. the controlled transition from semantic closure to contract closure;
5. the conditions under which an AMS may authorize implementation;
6. the verification and closure requirements for the resulting implementation.

The plan does **not** itself authorize production implementation.

# 2. Constitutional Position

Z-PROF exists to provide a generalized mechanism for governed domain composition and interpretation across the existing Zyppi constitutional substrate.

Z-PROF SHALL extend the existing substrate.

It SHALL NOT become a parallel Constitution.

Accordingly:
`Z-PROF SHALL NOT:  - create Reality; - redefine Reality; - replace ZRM; - replace ARM; - replace Registry truth; - replace Evidence verification; - replace PRJ projection authority; - replace RSN / Intelligence authority; - replace POL authorization; - replace SEC trust authority; - replace RI constitutional execution; - create a competing ontology; - create a competing Policy engine; - create a competing Security authority; - create a competing Runtime; - create a competing constitutional receipt. `
Z-PROF exists as **connective constitutional architecture**.

Its purpose is to bind, compose, govern and expose relationships among existing constitutional capabilities without acquiring ownership of those capabilities.

# 3. Golden Question Invariant

The Golden Question is an ecosystem-wide semantic invariant:

**Who did what, to whom, where, when, and how do we know?**

Its constitutional mapping is:

Golden Question

Constitutional meaning

Who?

Subject

Did what?

Event

To whom?

Object

Where?

Place

When?

Valid Time

How do we know?

Evidence

The Golden Question does not introduce a sixth primitive.

**Valid Time remains a temporal dimension of Reality rather than a new constitutional primitive.**

Governance and Authority are not additional Reality primitives.

They determine whether an action was permitted, authorized or governed.

They do not determine whether an Event occurred.

Therefore:

**Governance protects and evaluates Reality; it does not constitute Reality.**

Z-PROF SHALL participate in answering the Golden Question only through authorized access to existing constitutional Reality and Evidence.

It SHALL NOT modify Reality to produce a more complete answer.

# 4. Naked Reality Principle

Every Z-PROF-derived representation SHALL remain epistemically bounded by the Reality and Evidence supporting it.

The following invariant is mandatory:

**Z-PROF SHALL NEVER CAUSE A DOMAIN REPRESENTATION, INTERPRETATION, POLICY DECISION, OR USER-FACING PRESENTATION TO BECOME STRONGER THAN THE REALITY AND EVIDENCE SUPPORTING IT.**

Therefore:
`UNKNOWN       ≠ FALSE UNAVAILABLE   ≠ FALSE CONFLICTING   ≠ FALSE UNVERIFIED    ≠ VERIFIED INTERPRETATION ≠ EVENT POLICY RESULT ≠ REALITY EXECUTION TIME ≠ VALID TIME `
Where the underlying evidence cannot establish a proposition, the downstream representation SHALL preserve that epistemic limitation.

This principle is the constitutional basis of the **Naked Reality Gate**.

# 7. M08 Runtime Boundary

The M08 Runtime boundary remains unchanged.

The constitutional Runtime remains:
`Application      │      │ Explicit Constitutional Inputs      ▼ M08 Runtime      │      ├── Admission      ├── Bundle Discovery      ├── Bundle Verification      ├── Dependency Resolution      ├── Compatibility Validation      ├── ACV Activation      ├── Resolution Graph Construction      ├── Active Execution      └── Receipt Generation      │      ▼ ExecutionOutput + ExecutionReceipt `
The Runtime SHALL remain:

- synchronous;

- deterministic;

- explicit-input driven;

- zero-I/O;

- infrastructure-independent;

- constitutionally bounded.

Z-PROF domain semantics SHALL NOT be placed into `packages/runtime` merely because they can be implemented deterministically.

Z-PROF SHALL enter Runtime only through the already-authorized constitutional execution contracts.

# 8. Application Boundary

Application infrastructure remains responsible for:

- external retrieval;

- infrastructure interaction;

- domain data acquisition;

- resolution of external references;

- construction of authorized constitutional inputs;

- orchestration around the pure Runtime boundary.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT acquire infrastructure data directly.

Z-PROF declarations SHALL remain independent of:

- SQL;

- GraphQL;

- REST;

- filesystem access;

- network access;

- database-specific schemas;

- vendor-specific retrieval mechanisms.

The Application layer may translate Z-PROF semantic requirements into concrete retrieval operations.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT prescribe the infrastructure mechanism used to satisfy them.

# 9. Architecture Boundary

The ratified Z-PROF architecture is governed by the following principles:

### 9.1 Semantic Independence

Domain semantics SHALL remain independent of infrastructure representation.

### 9.2 Profile Isolation

A domain composition SHALL NOT mutate or redefine the underlying ARM Profile or Reality.

### 9.3 Factorization

Multiple domains SHALL be capable of participating in the same underlying Reality without requiring duplication of that Reality.

Conceptually:
`                   Shared Reality                          │               ┌──────────┼──────────┐               ▼          ▼          ▼              GS1        DPP      Customs               │          │          │               ▼          ▼          ▼         Domain Composition / Interpretation`
The existence of multiple domain views SHALL NOT imply multiple canonical Realities.

### 9.4 Disappearance Test

If Z-PROF disappears, the underlying:

- ZRM Reality;

- ARM Profiles;

- PRJ capabilities;

- RSN capabilities;

- POL decisions;

- SEC capabilities;

- RI Runtime;

- Registry;

- Evidence;

SHALL remain constitutionally valid and independently usable.

Z-PROF SHALL be connective architecture, not an indispensable owner of the capabilities it composes.

# 10. Composition Boundary

The Z-PROF composition mechanism SHALL operate through explicit, version-bound references to existing constitutional artifacts.

A composition SHALL NOT silently:

- invent a missing capability;

- infer an unauthorized capability;

- replace a required capability with an unrelated capability;

- modify the semantics of a referenced artifact;

- bypass policy;

- bypass security;

- bypass Runtime admission;

- create an alternate evidence system.

Composition is therefore a governed binding operation.

It is not an alternative source of constitutional authority.

# 11. Interrogation Boundary

Domain interrogation SHALL express **what semantic information is required**, not **how infrastructure should retrieve it**.

An interrogation requirement may express requirements such as:

- required Subject participation;

- required Event relationship;

- required Object relationship;

- required Place;

- required Valid Time;

- required Evidence;

- required Profile relationship;

- required projection;

- required provenance.

It SHALL NOT express:

- SQL queries;

- database joins;

- API endpoints;

- network calls;

- vendor-specific retrieval procedures.

This preserves the separation between semantic requirements and physical retrieval.

# 12. Evidence and Provenance

The Golden Question requires that "How do we know?" remain traceable.

Therefore every governed Z-PROF composition SHALL preserve the provenance necessary to identify:

1. the Reality being interpreted;

2. the constitutional artifacts used;

3. the Evidence supporting the relevant Reality;

4. the applicable composition;

5. the applicable version bindings;

6. the resulting interpretation;

7. the execution context where applicable.

Domain provenance and execution provenance SHALL remain distinct.

The Z-PROF layer SHALL NOT expand the RI ExecutionReceipt into a substitute domain evidence model.

The Runtime Receipt proves constitutional execution.

The domain Evidence/Provenance chain establishes the epistemic basis for the domain interpretation.

# 13. Temporal Boundary

Z-PROF SHALL preserve the distinction between:

- **Valid Time** — when an Event occurred in Reality;

- **Execution Time** — when the Runtime evaluated an operation;

- **Observation Time** — when a representation was obtained or observed.

These values SHALL NOT be substituted for one another.

A user asking:

"When did this happen?"

must receive the applicable **Valid Time**, where established.

Runtime execution time SHALL NOT be presented as the Event's Valid Time merely because it is readily available.

Historical interpretation and replay SHALL remain bound to the appropriate historical constitutional context.

# 14. Epistemic Boundary

Z-PROF SHALL preserve the distinction between:
`Reality    │    ▼ Evidence    │    ▼ Authorized Interpretation    │    ▼ Domain Representation `
A Domain Judgment, where produced, SHALL remain a derived interpretation.

It SHALL NOT become a new constitutional primitive.

It SHALL NOT become canonical Reality.

It SHALL NOT overwrite the Event that gave rise to it.

RSN / Intelligence remains responsible for interpretation according to its governing authority.

Z-PROF SHALL compose and govern the semantic context required for such interpretation without silently becoming the owner of Intelligence.

# 15. Policy and Security Boundary

POL and SEC remain authoritative for governance and trust.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT evaluate authorization as a substitute for POL.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT establish trust as a substitute for SEC.

A composition may declare that a policy or security capability is required.

The relevant constitutional authority SHALL perform the actual evaluation.

An Event that occurred without authorization remains an Event.

Its unauthorized status is a governance fact associated with that Reality.

Governance SHALL NOT rewrite or erase the underlying Event.

# 16. Registry Boundary

The Registry remains the authoritative mechanism for governed registration of constitutional artifacts.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT create a competing registry.

Any future Registry extension required to support multiple domains SHALL:

1. preserve existing Registry authority;

2. remain domain-general rather than GS1-specific;

3. preserve deterministic identity and version semantics;

4. preserve existing validation and canonicalization rules;

5. be authorized through the appropriate AMS.

A successful GS1 implementation SHALL NOT automatically authorize Registry changes for unrelated domains.

# 20. Scale and Factorization Requirement

Z-PROF SHALL be evaluated against the domain multiplication problem.

The architecture SHALL support:
`1 Reality      │      ├── Domain A      ├── Domain B      ├── Domain C      ├── Domain D      └── Domain N `
without requiring:
`Reality × Domain A Reality × Domain B Reality × Domain C ... `
The architectural objective is therefore:

**Factorize domain participation around shared Reality rather than multiply Reality by domain.**

The existence of hundreds of domains SHALL not require hundreds of competing ontologies.

The existence of hundreds of applications SHALL not require hundreds of independent constitutional substrates.
