## 1. Purpose

D4 determines whether the Z-PROF architectural direction survives materially different applications and domains without becoming domain-specific infrastructure, a collection of application engines, or a parallel constitutional system.

The objective is not to design GS1, DPP, Customs, Healthcare, Aviation, Finance, Education, E-commerce, or any other application.

The objective is to determine whether the proposed Z-PROF abstraction is sufficiently general to support an expanding universe of domain participation while preserving Zyppi's constitutional boundaries.

The central D4 question is:

**Can the same Zyppi constitutional substrate support fundamentally different domain interpretations without creating a new constitutional architecture for each application?**

The answer established by D4 is:

**YES.**

The stress tests demonstrate that the domain is not the fundamental architectural variable.

The invariant is the governed relationship between:
`Constitutional Reality         ↓ Domain Requirements / Interrogation         ↓ Authorized Reality Subgraph         ↓ Semantic Projection / Interpretation         ↓ Domain Judgment         ↓ Domain Consumption or Governed Composition `

# 2. Constitutional Baseline

D1 established the constitutional jurisdiction of Z-PROF.

D2 established that the existing Zyppi repository already provides substantial constitutional machinery that Z-PROF must consume rather than recreate.

D3 established that the missing capability is best understood as a governed semantic composition mechanism rather than a new Runtime, Policy engine, Registry, Security model, or unrestricted reasoning engine.

D4 therefore tests that conclusion against materially different domains.

The existing constitutional direction remains:
`ZYPPI REALITY       │       ▼ Existing Constitutional Capabilities       │       ├── ARM       ├── PRJ       ├── RSN       ├── POL       ├── SEC       ├── RI       └── Evidence / Registry / Context       │       ▼     Z-PROF       │       ▼ Governed Domain Composition       │       ▼ Domain Interpretation / Judgment `
Z-PROF does not become authoritative over the capabilities it composes.

# 3. Stress-Test Principle

Every application was evaluated against five common questions.

### S1 — Reality

**What Constitutional Reality does the application consume?**

### S2 — Interrogation

**What does the application need to establish or retrieve from Reality before a legitimate domain interpretation can occur?**

### S3 — Projection

**How does Constitutional Reality become a domain-specific semantic representation?**

### S4 — Judgment

**What domain-specific conclusion can legitimately be produced from that representation?**

### S5 — Composition

**Can the application coexist with other domain profiles over the same Reality without contaminating, redefining, or mutating that Reality?**

These five questions constitute the D4 stress-test framework.

# 5. Test A — GS1

## Question

Can the proposed architecture support the existing CAW-006 GS1 application without making GS1 assumptions constitutional?

### Expected flow

`GS1 Digital Link       ↓ M06 Resolution       ↓ Registry State       ↓ ACV + Evidence       ↓ M08       ↓ Z-PROF       ↓ GS1 semantic projection       ↓ GS1 domain judgment / response `

### Result

**PASS.**

GS1 demonstrates that a carrier-specific identifier can be resolved into a more general constitutional representation before downstream interpretation.

GS1 tests:

- identifier resolution;

- registry retrieval;

- evidence;

- semantic projection;

- domain interpretation.

It does not sufficiently test:

- multi-profile composition;

- conflicting jurisdictions;

- temporal legal state;

- high-dimensional evidence;

- competing interpretations;

- cross-domain composition.

### Ratified D4 Finding

**GS1 is an effective application wedge and a valid first constitutional test, but GS1 is not the definition of Z-PROF.**

This is essential to the Commerce Atlas strategy.

CAW may contain many applications and wedges. Z-PROF must remain invariant beneath them.

# 15. The Most Important D4 Finding

The application domains are not the fundamental architectural variable.

The domains differ primarily in **what they ask of Reality**.

Across the stress tests, the recurring pattern is:
`What is it?        ↓ What is related to it?        ↓ What happened?        ↓ Where and when did it happen?        ↓ What evidence supports it?        ↓ What is established under this domain's jurisdiction?        ↓ What does that mean for this domain?        ↓ What judgment may legitimately be issued? `
This confirms that Z-PROF does not need:
`GS1 Engine DPP Engine Customs Engine Healthcare Engine Finance Engine ... `
It needs one constitutional mechanism capable of supporting many domain compositions.

The correct abstraction is therefore:
`                   ZYPPI REALITY                          │                ┌─────────┴─────────┐                │                   │         Domain Requirements   Evidence Requirements                │                   │                └─────────┬─────────┘                          ▼                 Bounded Interrogation                          │                          ▼                 Authorized Reality                     Subgraph/View                          │                          ▼                 Semantic Projection                          │                          ▼                   Domain Judgment                          │                  ┌───────┴───────┐                  ▼               ▼              Consumer        Composition`
The domain-specific portion is primarily **declarative semantic jurisdiction**, not infrastructure.

# 16. Golden Question Invariant

D4 formally incorporates the Zyppi Golden Question as a cross-dimensional semantic invariant:

**Who did what, to whom, where, when, and how do we know?**

Its constitutional mapping remains:

Golden Question

Constitutional Dimension

**Who?**

Subject

**Did what?**

Event

**To whom?**

Object

**Where?**

**When?**

Valid Time

**How do we know?**

Time remains a constitutional dimension of Reality, not a sixth primitive.

Governance and Authority remain outside the Golden Question's Reality ontology.

They determine whether Reality was permitted, governed, authorized, or compliant.

They do not determine whether an Event occurred.

Therefore:

**Governance protects and evaluates Reality; it does not constitute Reality.**

D4 confirms that every domain composition must preserve this distinction.

# 17. Naked Reality Constraint

The D4 stress tests also ratify the following epistemic constraint:

**No domain representation, interpretation, judgment, or user-facing presentation SHALL claim more than the Reality and Evidence supporting it establish.**

Therefore:
`UNKNOWN      ≠ FALSE UNAVAILABLE  ≠ FALSE CONFLICTING  ≠ FALSE UNVERIFIED   ≠ VERIFIED `
A domain composition SHALL NOT manufacture a missing Golden Question dimension merely because the consuming application expects a complete response.

In particular:

- missing Subject SHALL remain missing;

- missing Event SHALL remain missing;

- missing Object SHALL remain missing;

- missing Place SHALL remain missing;

- missing Valid Time SHALL remain missing;

- missing Evidence SHALL remain missing.

The appropriate downstream representation is an explicit epistemic state, not fabricated completeness.

# 18. Temporal Boundary

D4 confirms that:

**Valid Time and Execution Time are distinct.**

When answering the Golden Question:

**When?** refers to the Valid Time of the Event in Reality.

It does not refer to:

- Runtime execution time;

- query time;

- observation time;

- retrieval time;

- receipt creation time.

Execution metadata may establish when Zyppi evaluated or materialized a composition, but it SHALL NOT be substituted for the Event's Valid Time.

This distinction is especially important for:

- Legal;

- Customs;

- Healthcare;

- Aviation;

- Finance;

- historical audit and replay.

# 19. Evidence and Judgment Boundary

D4 establishes:
`Evidence    ↓ supports Reality    ↓ Reality    ↓ Projection / Interpretation    ↓ Domain Judgment `
The reverse direction is prohibited:
`Domain Judgment       X       ↓ Constitutional Reality `
A Domain Judgment may explain what a domain concludes from established Reality.

It cannot retroactively establish the Event merely because the domain requires such an Event to exist.

# 20. Composition Boundary

D4 establishes that the architecture must support:
`                   One Reality                         │           ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐           ▼             ▼             ▼       Domain A       Domain B       Domain C           │             │             │           ▼             ▼             ▼      Judgment A    Judgment B    Judgment C`
These judgments may be:

- independent;

- concurrent;

- overlapping;

- jurisdictionally different;

- temporally different.

They do not become one Reality merely because they concern the same Asset.

Where compositions are combined, the combination itself must be explicit and governed.

The distinction remains:
`ARM Profile "What kind of Asset Reality is this?" `

`Domain Composition "How does this existing Reality participate in this domain?" `
Therefore:

- GS1 is not an ARM Product Profile;

- DPP is not an ARM Product Profile;

- Customs is not an ARM Product Profile;

- Logistics is not an ARM Product Profile;

- Healthcare is not an ARM Product Profile;

- Finance is not an ARM Product Profile.

This distinction is foundational to the scalability of the architecture.

**If Z-PROF were removed, the underlying Zyppi Reality, ARM Profiles, Registry, Evidence, PRJ, RSN, POL, SEC and RI capabilities must remain constitutionally valid.**

Z-PROF is connective architecture.

It is not a new source of authority.

It is not a new Runtime.

It is not a new Security model.

It is not a replacement for existing semantic-projection capability.

If deletion of Z-PROF would invalidate an underlying constitutional capability, Z-PROF has exceeded its jurisdiction.
