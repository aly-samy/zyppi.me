M08.5-PREP does **not** define the Z-PROF constitutional architecture.

# 3. Golden Question
 
The entire Z-PROF architecture is subordinate to the Zyppi Golden Question:
 
 
**"Who did what, to whom, where, when, and how do we know?"**
 
 
The Golden Question is a **semantic invariant**, not a new subsystem and not a sixth ontological primitive.
 
Its constitutional mapping is:

**Did what?**

**To whom?**

The Council explicitly rejects adding Authority/Governance as a Reality primitive.
 
Authority and Governance remain protective and evaluative mechanisms supplied by POL/SEC and related constitutional layers.
 
They answer questions such as:
 
 
**Was this action permitted?**
 
 
They do not determine:
 
 
**Did this event happen?**
 
 
### Constitutional invariant
 
 
**Governance SHALL protect and evaluate Reality; it SHALL NOT redefine Reality.**
 
 
An event that occurred remains an Event whether it was authorized, unauthorized, compliant, non-compliant, or subsequently prohibited.
  
# 4. Naked Reality Principle
 
Z-PROF and all downstream representations SHALL remain bounded by the Reality and Evidence actually established.
 
Therefore:
 
 
**No Z-PROF composition, interpretation, reasoning output, or user-facing representation SHALL make a stronger epistemic claim than is supported by the underlying Reality and Evidence.**
 
 
In particular:
 
 
- `UNKNOWN` SHALL NOT become `FALSE`;
 
- `UNAVAILABLE` SHALL NOT become `FALSE`;
 
- absence of Evidence SHALL NOT become Evidence of absence;
 
- Interpretation SHALL NOT become Event;
 
- Projection SHALL NOT become Reality;
 
- Policy judgment SHALL NOT become Reality;
 
- Execution Time SHALL NOT be presented as Valid Time;
 
- an inferred fact SHALL NOT be presented as an established fact.
 

 
Where the Golden Question cannot be answered completely, the system SHALL expose the epistemic limitation rather than manufacture completeness.
  
# 5. Temporal Boundary
 
M08.5-PREP records the following permanent distinction:
 
### Valid Time
 
The time at which an Event occurred in Reality.
 
### Execution Time
 
The time at which Zyppi evaluated or executed a constitutional operation.
 
### Observation / Presentation Time
 
The time at which information is observed or presented.
 
These dimensions SHALL NOT be conflated.
 
In particular:
 
 
**Runtime execution time SHALL NEVER silently substitute for the Valid Time of a Reality Event.**
 
 
Historical events remain historical facts even if the governing policy, capability, or execution environment subsequently changes.
  
# 6. Evidence Discipline
 
All repository findings in M08.5-PREP are classified according to evidence strength.
 
### FACT
 
Directly established by repository evidence or an authoritative governing artifact.
 
### INFERENCE
 
A technically reasonable conclusion derived from evidence but not itself constitutional law.
 
### UNKNOWN
 
The available evidence does not establish the fact.
 
### CONSTITUTIONAL DECISION
 
A matter that cannot legitimately be decided by repository reconnaissance or implementation convenience.
 
M08.5-PREP SHALL NOT convert an inference into a constitutional requirement.
 
It SHALL NOT convert an absence of evidence into a statement of non-existence beyond the investigated boundary.

# 12. Factorization Requirement
 
The preparation investigation establishes the scaling problem that Z-PROF must solve.
 
The system must be capable of supporting:
 `One Asset Reality        │        ▼    ARM Profile        │        ├── GS1        ├── DPP        ├── Customs        ├── Logistics        ├── Recall        ├── Sustainability        ├── Consumer Experience        └── future domains ` 
without creating:
 `Product-GS1-Profile Product-DPP-Profile Product-Customs-Profile Product-Logistics-Profile ... ` 
as independent constitutional Profiles.
 
The architectural objective is therefore:
 `Asset Reality     + ARM Profile     + Domain     + Projection     + Context     + Evidence / Epistemic Requirements     + Reasoning where required     = Governed Domain Participation ` 
This permits many domains to participate in the same Reality without multiplying the foundational ontology.
