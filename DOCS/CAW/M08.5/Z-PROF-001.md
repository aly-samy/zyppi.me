# 1. Constitutional Purpose

Z-PROF establishes the constitutional architecture through which Zyppi may represent, register, compose, interrogate, and govern domain-specific requirements over the existing Zyppi constitutional substrate.

Z-PROF exists to solve one problem:

> **How can an open-ended family of domains, applications, regulatory contexts, projections, and interpretations operate over the same constitutional Reality without creating a new ontology, new Reality model, new Profile system, new execution system, or new constitutional authority for every domain?**

Z-PROF answers this through:

- Domain Template Cards;
- declarative epistemic requirements;
- declarative CompositionManifests;
- governed references to existing constitutional capabilities;
- structural composition;
- explicit provenance;
- strict epistemic boundaries;
- separation of Reality from interpretation;
- separation of governance from Reality;
- factorization rather than domain/profile multiplication.

Z-PROF is therefore a **composition architecture**, not a new foundational ontology.

# 4. The Golden Question

Zyppi's universal semantic question is:

> **Who did what, to whom, where, when, and how do we know?**

The Golden Question is a **vertical semantic invariant** across Zyppi.

It is not:

- a new primitive;
- a new ontology;
- a database schema;
- an execution contract;
- an API;
- an interrogation language;
- a Profile;
- a Runtime subsystem.

It is the semantic test by which representations remain traceable to Reality.

# 5. Golden Question Mapping

The Golden Question maps to the existing constitutional Reality model:

| Golden Question | Constitutional Dimension |
| --------------- | ------------------------ |
| Who?            | Subject                  |
| Did what?       | Event                    |
| To whom?        | Object                   |
| Where?          | Place                    |
| When?           | Valid Time               |
| How do we know? | Evidence                 |

Time remains a constitutional dimension of Events and does not become a sixth foundational primitive.

Governance and Authority are not additional Reality primitives.

They answer a different question:

> **Was the action permitted, authorized, governed, or within applicable jurisdiction?**

Governance protects and evaluates Reality.

It does not define whether Reality occurred.

# 6. Naked Reality Principle

Z-PROF SHALL preserve the distinction between:

> **What happened**

and:

> **What is permitted, inferred, interpreted, or concluded about what happened.**

If an Event occurred, the fact of its occurrence SHALL NOT be altered merely because:

- the Event was unauthorized;
- the policy governing it later changed;
- the actor lacked permission;
- the current user lacks access;
- a domain interpretation disagrees with it;
- a later Profile version interprets it differently;
- a later policy revokes the capability that permitted it;
- an AI system cannot explain it completely.

Governance may restrict access to Reality.

Governance SHALL NOT rewrite Reality.

# 7. Naked Reality Gate

Any user-facing or downstream representation governed by Z-PROF SHALL preserve epistemic fidelity.

No Z-PROF composition SHALL cause a representation to claim more than the available Reality and Evidence establish.

Therefore:

> **Z-PROF SHALL NEVER CAUSE A DOMAIN REPRESENTATION, INTERPRETATION, POLICY DECISION, OR USER-FACING PRESENTATION TO BECOME STRONGER THAN THE REALITY AND EVIDENCE SUPPORTING IT.**

The following distinctions are constitutional:

```text
UNKNOWN       ≠ FALSE
UNAVAILABLE   ≠ FALSE
UNVERIFIED    ≠ VERIFIED
CONFLICTING   ≠ RESOLVED
INFERRED      ≠ OBSERVED
INTERPRETED   ≠ REALITY
AUTHORIZED    ≠ OCCURRED
EXECUTED      ≠ AUTHORIZED
```

A missing answer SHALL remain missing.

A conflicting answer SHALL remain conflicting until legitimately resolved.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT silently complete an incomplete Golden Question.

# 8. Temporal Separation

The Golden Question's:

**When?**

refers to **Valid Time**:

when the Event occurred in Reality.

It SHALL NOT be silently replaced by:

- Execution Time;

- Observation Time;

- Retrieval Time;

- Registration Time;

- Evaluation Time;

- Publication Time.

Runtime execution time may be recorded as execution provenance.

It does not become the Event's Valid Time.

Historical Reality SHALL therefore remain distinguishable from the time at which Zyppi subsequently evaluated or represented it.

# 9. Epistemic Separation

Z-PROF SHALL preserve the distinction between:
`Reality    ↓ Evidence    ↓ Projection    ↓ Reasoning / Interpretation    ↓ Domain-specific result `
A downstream interpretation SHALL NOT be promoted into Reality merely because it was produced by a constitutional process.

A reasoning result is not automatically an Event.

A Projection is not automatically an Event.

A Domain result is not automatically an Event.

Only the appropriate Reality-governing mechanisms establish Reality.

# 11. ARM Profile Boundary

The term **Profile** already has a constitutional meaning under ARM.

An ARM Profile is an asset-class specialization.

It may declare:

- asset-class-specific attributes;

- projection support;

- state vocabulary;

- legitimate specialization of an Asset Reality.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT redefine the ARM Profile.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT create a second generic constitutional Profile system merely to represent domains.

Therefore:
`Asset Reality       ↓ ARM Profile       ↓ domain participation `
does not mean:
`Asset Reality       ↓ one new Profile for GS1 one new Profile for DPP one new Profile for Customs one new Profile for Logistics ... `

# 12. Factorization Principle

Z-PROF SHALL factor independent dimensions rather than multiply them into new constitutional objects.

The preferred conceptual model is:
`Asset Reality       × ARM Profile       × Domain       × Projection       × Context       × Policy       × Evidence `
rather than:
`Asset × Domain × Projection × Context × Policy         ↓ new Profile `
The architecture SHALL therefore support:

- one Product Asset Reality;

- one Product ARM Profile;

- many domain applications;

- many projections;

- many regulatory contexts;

- many jurisdictions;

- many compositions.

Without requiring a new constitutional Profile for every combination.

# 14. Domain Template Card

A **Domain Template Card (DTC)** is the governed authoring and registration artifact through which a domain declares what constitutional capabilities it requires.

The DTC is:

- declarative;

- non-executable;

- non-ontological;

- non-authoritative over underlying constitutional capabilities.

The DTC does not implement the domain.

It declares the domain's constitutional requirements.

# 15. Domain Template Card Responsibilities

A DTC MAY declare:

- domain identity;

- domain scope;

- semantic vocabulary;

- applicable Asset Profiles;

- required projections;

- required epistemic information;

- required Evidence classes;

- required Context dimensions;

- required Policy references;

- required Security references;

- required Reasoning capabilities;

- compatibility requirements;

- lifecycle metadata;

- version constraints;

- provenance requirements.

A DTC SHALL NOT:

- create Reality;

- mutate Reality;

- redefine ZRM primitives;

- authorize access;

- execute Runtime operations;

- retrieve infrastructure data;

- replace Evidence verification;

- implement cryptography;

- redefine Projection;

- replace Reasoning;

- create a new ontology.

# 16. Domain Admission

Every governed domain SHALL possess an approved Domain Template Card before it may participate in the governed Z-PROF composition architecture.

The DTC therefore provides the domain-level constitutional declaration:

**What does this domain require from Zyppi?**

The DTC does not answer the domain question by itself.

It defines the requirements needed to answer it.

# 17. CompositionManifest

A **CompositionManifest** is the concrete declarative artifact that binds a Domain Template Card to existing governed constitutional capabilities.

Conceptually:
`Domain Template Card         ↓ constitutional validation         ↓ CompositionManifest         ↓ Bound Constitutional Payload         ↓ existing Zyppi capabilities `
The CompositionManifest is:

- declarative;

- structural;

- version-bound;

- provenance-preserving;

- non-executable;

- non-authoritative over the internal semantics of referenced capabilities.

# 18. CompositionManifest Responsibilities

A CompositionManifest SHALL identify, as applicable:

- originating Domain Template Card;

- domain version;

- required ARM Profile references;

- required Projection references;

- required Evidence requirements;

- required Reasoning references;

- required Context references;

- required Policy references;

- required Security capabilities;

- compatibility constraints;

- version constraints;

- dependency topology;

- provenance requirements.

The Manifest binds capabilities.

It does not implement them.

# 19. Composition Is Not Dependency

Z-PROF distinguishes:

### Constitutional dependency

`Capability A       ↓ requires Capability B `
from:

### Structural composition

`Composition  ├── Capability A  ├── Capability B  ├── Capability C  └── Context `
Composition SHALL NOT automatically create Profile-to-Profile dependency.

ARM Profile Isolation remains intact.

No Profile gains constitutional authority over another merely because a CompositionManifest references both.

# 20. Profile Isolation

Z-PROF SHALL preserve ARM Profile Isolation.

A Profile SHALL NOT:

- redefine another Profile;

- inherit unauthorized semantic authority from another Profile;

- mutate another Profile;

- establish implicit constitutional precedence over another Profile.

Multiple Profiles may participate in a composition provided their respective constitutional boundaries remain independent.

# 21. Interrogation

**Interrogation** is the declarative expression of what constitutional information must be known or made available before a governed composition can be evaluated.

Interrogation asks:

**What must we know?**

It does not ask:

**How do we retrieve it from infrastructure?**

# 22. Epistemic Requirement Contract

Where required, an **Epistemic Requirement Contract** SHALL express semantic information requirements.

Conceptually:
`Requirement  ├── subject  ├── object  ├── event  ├── place  ├── valid time  ├── evidence  ├── context  └── applicable constraints `
The exact implementation representation is not constitutionally prescribed by this document.

The contract SHALL remain technology-independent.

# 23. Interrogation Is Not Data Access

Z-PROF SHALL NOT contain:

- SQL;

- database-specific queries;

- HTTP retrieval logic;

- PostgreSQL operations;

- object-storage operations;

- GraphQL execution;

- network access;

- infrastructure credentials.

Instead:
`Z-PROF   │   │ declares what is required   ▼ Application / governed orchestration   │   │ obtains permitted inputs   ▼ Registry / Evidence / other constitutional sources   │   ▼ validated constitutional inputs   │   ▼ Z-PROF composition `
Infrastructure access remains outside the constitutional Z-PROF composition boundary.

# 24. Interrogation and Authorization

Z-PROF SHALL distinguish:

**What information is required?**

from:

**Is this Subject permitted to access that information?**

A Domain Template Card MAY declare that Evidence is required.

It SHALL NOT determine whether a particular Subject may access that Evidence.

Authorization remains POL.

Security and trust remain SEC.

# 25. Interrogation and Evidence

Z-PROF may declare required Evidence characteristics.

It SHALL NOT become the Evidence authority.

The responsibility separation is:
`DTC   ↓ declares evidence requirement  Application   ↓ resolves permitted references  Evidence subsystem   ↓ retrieves / verifies Evidence  RI   ↓ consumes verified inputs where applicable  Z-PROF   ↓ uses the resulting governed input `
Z-PROF SHALL NOT fabricate missing Evidence.

# 26. Failure and Incompleteness

Z-PROF SHALL preserve the semantic distinction between incomplete information and negative information.

At minimum:
`UNKNOWN UNAVAILABLE UNVERIFIED CONFLICTING OUT_OF_SCOPE `
must remain distinguishable from:
`FALSE ABSENT INVALID DISPROVEN `
unless a domain-specific governed contract explicitly establishes the equivalence.

No silent completion is permitted.

# 27. Projection Boundary

Z-PROF SHALL NOT redefine Semantic Projection.

Projection remains under PRJ and the applicable ZRM projection architecture.

Z-PROF may:

- declare a projection requirement;

- reference an approved projection capability;

- bind a projection to a domain composition;

- route required inputs to the projection mechanism.

Z-PROF does not become the projection engine.

# 28. Translation Boundary

SIOS Translation Layers remain responsible for translating domain-specific language into constitutional concepts.

Z-PROF may provide the composition structure through which those translations participate in a governed domain.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT silently redefine itself as the universal Translation Layer.

The distinction is:
`SIOS Translation       ↓ domain language ↔ constitutional meaning  Z-PROF       ↓ governed composition of capabilities `

# 29. Reasoning and Intelligence Boundary

Z-PROF SHALL NOT become an AI reasoning engine.

A probabilistic AI output SHALL NOT automatically become:

- Reality;

- Evidence;

- an Event;

- a canonical Profile fact;

- an authorization decision.

Its constitutional status must derive from the mechanism that governs that output.

- a reasoning result;

`Domain Judgment ≠ Reality Domain Judgment ≠ Event Domain Judgment ≠ Evidence Domain Judgment ≠ ARM Profile `

# 32. Temporal Composition

Domain compositions SHALL respect Zyppi's constitutional treatment of Time.

A composition may be evaluated against:

- a temporal state;

- an Event interval;

- a historical Reality slice;

- another governed temporal context.

Z-PROF SHALL NOT reduce temporal semantics to a simple current timestamp.

A Profile version, Policy version, Evidence version, Projection version, or Execution version SHALL remain distinct from Reality's Valid Time.

# 33. Version Independence

The following versions are constitutionally distinct:
`Reality version ARM Profile version Domain Template Card version CompositionManifest version Projection version Policy version Evidence version Execution version `
Changing a Profile does not change Reality.

Changing a Domain Template Card does not change Reality.

Changing a Projection does not change Reality.

Changing Policy does not retroactively change whether an Event occurred.

# 34. Historical Reproducibility

Where a composition is required to be auditable or replayable, its relevant inputs SHALL be explicitly version-bound.

A replayable composition must preserve sufficient information to reconstruct:
`Domain Template Card + CompositionManifest + referenced capability versions + authorized constitutional inputs + Evidence references + Context + applicable governance context `
Runtime Execution Time SHALL remain distinct from the Valid Time of the underlying Reality.

Z-PROF does not create a second replay system.

Replay remains subject to the governing Runtime and constitutional replay mechanisms.

# 35. Provenance

Every governed composition SHALL preserve provenance sufficient to establish:

- which Domain Template Card was used;

- which version was used;

- which CompositionManifest was used;

- which constitutional capabilities were referenced;

- which relevant inputs were supplied;

- which Evidence supported those inputs;

- which Context applied;

- which downstream capability produced the resulting interpretation.

Composition SHALL NOT destroy provenance.

A composition that cannot be traced back to its constitutional inputs SHALL fail the applicable provenance requirement.

# 36. Conflict

Z-PROF SHALL NOT resolve conflicting domain outputs by arbitrary Profile precedence.

A conflict may arise because of:

- different Valid Times;

- different jurisdictions;

- different Contexts;

- different semantic vocabularies;

- different Evidence;

- different Projection contracts;

- genuinely incompatible Reality;

- incomplete Evidence.

Therefore:

**Conflict SHALL be classified before it is resolved.**

Z-PROF SHALL NOT manufacture a universal:
`Profile A > Profile B `
authority rule.

# 37. Domain Multiplication

Z-PROF is explicitly designed to support a large and evolving domain universe.

A future Zyppi installation may contain:
`100+ Asset Profiles 500+ Domains 1,000+ Projections thousands of contexts many jurisdictions many regulatory regimes `
This SHALL NOT require:
`one Profile per Asset × Domain × Projection × Context `
The architecture SHALL remain factorized.

# 41. Policy Boundary

# 42. Security Boundary

# 43. Registry Boundary

# 44. Evidence Boundary

Z-PROF SHALL consume Evidence.

It SHALL NOT redefine Evidence.

It SHALL NOT convert an interpretation into Evidence.

It SHALL NOT manufacture Evidence.

The Golden Question's:

Therefore:
`Reality:     "The transfer occurred."  Governance:     "The transfer was unauthorized."  `

The unauthorized nature of an Event SHALL NOT cause the Event itself to disappear from Reality.
