## 1. Purpose and Scope

`G-06-01` answered: *"What are the authoritative GS1 rules?"* It established the external standards baseline and deliberately left Zyppi's support policy undecided.

This record answers: *"Which of those rules does Zyppi adopt, to what scope, and with what behavior?"*

This record:
1. Ratifies the external GS1 standards baseline identified by `G-06-01`;
2. Selects Zyppi citation conventions and records known source discrepancies;
3. Records all research gaps without treating them as resolved;
4. Establishes the foundational distinction between GS1 validity, Zyppi support-profile eligibility, and M06 resolution eligibility;
5. Defines Zyppi's bounded GS1 support profile for the M06 wedge;
6. Fixes terminology and claim discipline;
7. Designates the follow-up research gates that must close before implementation.

This record does **not**:
- draft `M06-PLAN` or `AMS-0601`;
- authorize implementation, code, tests, or package changes;
- convert unverified research into binding policy;
- relabel GS1-valid content as invalid GS1;
- finalize the registry-key representation (formally gated under `G-06-03`).

## 3. Part A — Standards Baseline Ratification

### A-1. GS1 Digital Link URI Syntax — RATIFIED
The normative authority for GS1 Digital Link URI structure, AI path/query placement, GTIN representation, percent-encoding, and canonical URI definition is:

> **GS1 Digital Link Standard: URI Syntax, Release 1.6.0, Ratified**, GS1 AISBL, `https://ref.gs1.org/standards/digital-link/uri-syntax/`.

### A-2. GS1 General Specifications — RATIFIED
The normative authority for GS1 identification keys, Application Identifier definitions and data formats, check-digit and calendar-date algorithms is:

> **GS1 General Specifications Standard, Release 26.0, Ratified, Jan 2026**, GS1 AISBL, `https://ref.gs1.org/standards/genspecs/`.

Only Release 26.0 is pinned. The stale GS1 US-hosted PDF displaying "Release 25.0" and the older-edition cross-references inside current GS1 documents (V24.0, 23.0.0) are recorded as known staleness and **must not** be used (see gap G-05).

### A-3. GS1 Application Identifier Registry — RATIFIED WITH SNAPSHOT GATE
The authoritative source for AI definitions is the **GS1 Application Identifiers registry**, `https://ref.gs1.org/ai/`, backed by the GS1 General Specifications, with a machine-readable JSON-LD dataset.

**Snapshot policy:** Before implementation design treats any AI definition as pinned, Zyppi SHALL archive a dated snapshot of the JSON-LD dataset, recording the exact dataset URL, access date, and integrity hash. This is gated on research gap **G-03** and executed under **RG-06-B**. Until that snapshot is archived, AI definitions are "identified but not yet snapshotted."

### A-4. GS1-Conformant Resolver Standard — ADJACENT REFERENCE ONLY
The **GS1-Conformant Resolver Standard 1.2.0 (Jan 2026)** is recorded as an **adjacent reference only**. It is **not** adopted as a Zyppi requirement.

Rationale: A GS1-Conformant Resolver redirects GS1 identification keys to one or more *web resources*. Zyppi M06 does not redirect to web resources; it resolves a normalized identity reference against **Zyppi's own Registry** (`CAW-002` Flow 2; `M06-ADR` §2.1). Zyppi M06 is therefore **not** a GS1-Conformant Resolver, and no resolver-conformance obligation is accepted. This designation exists to prevent scope drift.

### A-5. Date Discrepancy — CITATION CONVENTION SELECTED; DISCREPANCY RECORDED
`G-06-01` recorded that the URI Syntax standard is shown as **"Mar 2025"** (official `ref.gs1.org` document summary and GS1 standards posting log) versus **"Apr 2025"** (gs1.org public overview page).

For Zyppi citation purposes, the constitutional record SHALL use **"Mar 2025,"** based on the higher-authority GS1 reference surface and posting log. The conflicting **"Apr 2025"** display remains recorded as a source-metadata discrepancy and does not affect the normative edition pin.

The normative edition (Release 1.6.0, Ratified) is pinned. The citation convention is selected. The metadata discrepancy remains documented and is not treated as having disappeared.

## 5. Part C — Foundational Distinction: GS1 Validity, Zyppi Eligibility, M06 Resolution

This distinction underpins every support-profile decision in Part D and is a constitutional protection against conflating external standards truth with Zyppi policy.

### C-0. The Three Layers

| Layer | Question | Determined By |
|---|---|---|
| **1. GS1 Validity** | Is this representation valid under the pinned GS1 standards? | GS1 standards (external authority) |
| **2. Zyppi Support-Profile Eligibility** | Is this GS1-valid content within Zyppi's approved M06 support profile? | Zyppi constitutional choice (bounded wedge) |
| **3. M06 Resolution Eligibility** | Is this content eligible for Registry resolution? | Zyppi resolution policy |

The layers are strictly ordered and must never be collapsed:

```
GS1 validity ≠ Zyppi support-profile eligibility ≠ M06 resolution eligibility
```

### C-0.1 GS1 Validity Is External
GS1 validity is determined solely by the pinned GS1 standards (`G-06-01`). Zyppi does not author, override, or relabel GS1 validity.

### C-0.2 Zyppi May Restrict Without Relabeling
**GS1 validity and Zyppi business acceptability are not necessarily the same thing.** If GS1 permits a representation that Zyppi later decides is unsuitable for deterministic Registry behavior, Zyppi may reject it as a **Zyppi support-profile restriction**. Zyppi **must not** relabel the GS1-valid representation as "invalid GS1."

A GS1-valid-but-unsupported input is therefore:
- **not** "invalid GS1";
- **not** eligible for M06 resolution under the current profile;
- **still** recognizable and preservable as GS1 content.

### C-0.3 Resolution Eligibility Is the Final Gate
Only content that is GS1-valid, within the Zyppi support profile, and successfully normalized to a supported identity reference is eligible for M06 Registry resolution. Failure at any layer produces a distinct typed outcome; the layers are never silently merged.

## 6. Part D — Zyppi GS1 Support Profile

### C-1. Primary Identification Key Scope — GTIN ONLY
The M06 initial profile supports **GTIN (AI 01) as the sole primary identification key**. No other GS1 primary key (GLN, SSCC, GRAI, GIAI, GSRN, etc.) is supported in the initial profile. Additional primary keys require an explicit profile amendment with standards evidence, domain review, and an attributable CAW task.

### C-2. Supported Qualifiers and Attributes — AI 21, AI 10, AI 17
The initial profile supports, in addition to AI 01:

| AI | GS1 Meaning | Zyppi Category (initial profile) |
|---|---|---|
| `01` | GTIN | Primary identification key (identity-bearing) |
| `21` | Serial Number | Supported key qualifier |
| `10` | Batch / Lot Number | Supported key qualifier |
| `17` | Expiration Date (YYMMDD) | Supported data attribute |

Exact per-AI validation rules are governed by the pinned GenSpecs 26.0 and the AI-registry snapshot, subject to RG-06-A / RG-06-B.

### C-3. Identity-Bearing Reference and Resolution Key — DIRECTION RECORDED; FORMALLY GATED
**Provisional preferred direction: K1 (normalized 14-digit GTIN).**

| Option | Representation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **K1** | Normalized 14-digit GTIN | `09506000134352` |
| **K2** | Canonical Digital Link URI | `https://id.gs1.org/01/09506000134352` |

The Council records **K1 as the provisional preferred direction** for the current M06 wedge, because:
- M06 is explicitly GTIN-centric;
- the Registry currently resolves product identity, not serial-level instance identity;
- the external domain is not identity-bearing;
- URI serialization introduces unnecessary dependence on host, URI canonicalization, encoding, and future qualifier behavior;
- the GTIN is the stable semantic identifier; the URI is a carrier representation.

The intended separation is:

```
Inbound Digital Link
   ↓ GS1 interpretation
   ↓ Normalized GTIN
   ↓ M06 Registry resolution key
   ↓ Registry identity record
```

Qualifiers and attributes remain in the parsed interpretation but do **not** alter the M06 resolution key.

**This is not finalized here.** The resolution-key form **must** pass the explicit **G-06-03** reconciliation against the M05 `identities.canonical_reference` contract and seed representation before `AMS-0601`. Until then, K1 is a provisional direction, not a ratified representation.

### C-4. Unsupported-but-Valid GS1 Content — RECOGNIZE, PRESERVE, TYPE, DO NOT RESOLVE
Recognized but unsupported GS1 content SHALL be parsed and preserved sufficiently to identify its **AI, placement, and standards-defined lexical structure**. It SHALL NOT be treated as supported Zyppi semantic content and SHALL make the input **ineligible for M06 Registry resolution**.

This policy operates across the three layers of C-0 and distinguishes:

1. **Standards recognition and structural parsing** — unsupported AIs that are GS1-recognized are still structurally parsed to the extent required to identify the AI, its placement, and its lexical form;
2. **Zyppi support-profile validation** — such content is classified as recognized-but-unsupported, not as supported semantic content;
3. **Registry resolution eligibility** — the presence of recognized-but-unsupported content makes the input ineligible for M06 Registry resolution.

The resulting outcome is:

```
UnsupportedGS1Content
   → parsed and preserved (AI, placement, lexical structure)
   → typed distinctly
   → not resolved
```

This avoids both silent data loss (losing the fact that the incoming identifier expressed additional meaning) and semantic risk (returning a product-level result while discarding expressed qualifiers or attributes).

The **exact typed representation** of unsupported content (schema, field names, diagnostics) is deferred to `M06-PLAN`.

### C-5. Normalization Boundary — ACCEPT ANY-DOMAIN, NORMALIZE DETERMINISTICALLY, INTERNAL CANONICAL REPRESENTATION
- M06 **accepts GS1 Digital Links on any domain** (the domain is not part of GS1 identity).
- M06 **does not require** inputs to be in the GS1 canonical (`id.gs1.org`) form and **does not rewrite** inputs to that form.
- M06 applies **standards-derived normalization** to identifier/AI content (14-digit GTIN padding, percent-decoding, AI ordering per the pinned standard) and produces a **deterministic internal canonical representation** used for resolution and replay.
- Producing the GS1 canonical URI (URI Syntax §4.12) as an output artifact is **out of M06 scope**.

The exact normalization contract is standards-derived and SHALL be specified in `M06-PLAN` after RG-06-A / RG-06-B close.

### C-6. AI 17 Validity Model — STRUCTURAL FLOOR NOW; CALENDAR SEMANTICS GATED ON G-02
- M06 enforces the **GS1-published structural rule** for AI 17: `YYMMDD`, per the AI-registry regular expression `(\d{2}(?:0\d|1[0-2])(?:[0-2]\d|3[01]))` (month 01–12, day 00–31).
- **Stricter calendar validation** (invalid day-for-month, leap-year handling) and the **day-`00` policy** are **not adopted here**. They remain **gated on G-02**, to be closed by RG-06-A against the pinned GenSpecs 26.0.
- **No universal day-`00` rejection is adopted at this time.** The Council will decide whether Zyppi imposes a stricter platform-level semantic rule only after the current authoritative GS1 semantics are extracted.
- Any stricter Zyppi rule, if later adopted, is a **Zyppi support-profile restriction** under C-0.2 and must not relabel GS1-valid day-`00` representations as "invalid GS1."
- `GSCN 21-040` (day `00` not valid for regulated healthcare products) is recorded as **context**, not as a wedge rule.

### C-7. Use of "GS1-conformant" — EVIDENCE GATE
Zyppi may describe M06 as **"GS1-standards conformant"** only when **all** of the following are evidenced:
1. Governing editions pinned (this record, Part A);
2. Support profile ratified (this record, Part D);
3. Standards-conformance tests with traceable provenance pass;
4. Licensing for any imported GS1 materials is verified (G-07 / RG-06-C);
5. No claim of GS1 certification is made.

Zyppi SHALL NOT claim **"GS1-certified," "GS1-qualified,"** or any official GS1 approval unless an applicable GS1 program exists, applies to Zyppi's actual software surface, and its verification process is completed and approved. Passing internal or standards-derived tests never constitutes certification.

## 7. Part E — Terminology and Claim Discipline

| Term | Permitted Use |
|---|---|
| **GS1-valid** | A representation that satisfies the pinned GS1 standards (external authority) |
| **GS1-invalid** | A representation that fails the pinned GS1 standards — never applied to GS1-valid-but-unsupported content |
| **Recognized-but-unsupported** | GS1-valid content outside the Zyppi support profile (C-4) |
| **GS1-standards conformant** | Only after all five conditions in C-7 are evidenced |
| **Conformance-testable** | M06 behavior can be independently tested against traceable GS1 requirements |
| **GS1-certified / GS1-qualified** | Prohibited absent an applicable GS1 program and completed verification |
| **GS1-Conformant Resolver** | Prohibited for Zyppi M06 — Zyppi is not a GS1 resolver (A-4) |
| **Resolved** (M06 outcome) | A Registry lookup result only; never "verified," "trusted," "authentic," or "authorized" |
