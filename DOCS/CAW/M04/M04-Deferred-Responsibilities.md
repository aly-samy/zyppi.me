# M04 — Runtime Skeleton Milestone Deferred Responsibilities

**Milestone:** M04 — Runtime Skeleton
**Status:** **CLOSED — DISPOSITION A RATIFIED**
**Binary Disposition:** **CLOSED**

---

## 1. Purpose

This register establishes the authoritative boundaries of Milestone M04. It precisely documents the responsibilities and architectural capabilities that have been intentionally excluded or deferred from the Runtime skeleton, identifying their named future milestones, tasks, and owners.

---

## 2. Source Availability and Citation Boundary

This closure review independently verifies M04-PLAN, CAW-011, the available AMS-0401–AMS-0407 evidence, applicable CEngS requirements, and the current repository state. The SEC, RI, and POL constitutional source series were not available in the execution workspace. Accordingly, those source series were not independently citation-verified, and no unverified clause title or wording is presented as source-confirmed. Where constitutional concepts are operationalized by available M04, CAW, AMS, or CEngS authority, those available sources are used as the evidentiary basis.

## 4. Evidentiary Boundary Analysis

This register confirms that the deferral of the `ExecutionReceipt` construction was explicitly designed-in, not improvised. Pursuant to the dependency graph in `CAW-011`, task `IT-0803 (Generate Execution Receipt)` depends on `IT-0405` (Receipt Generator) and `IT-0802`, proving that Milestone M04 was always intended to scaffold the pipeline's stages, leaving full receipt construction for Milestone M08.
