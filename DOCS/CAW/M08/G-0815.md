# 1. Question Presented

The governing question for G-0815 is:

**What semantic version identifies the policy state that governs a constitutional Runtime execution?**

G-0815 does not determine:

- policy selection;

- policy applicability;

- policy precedence;

- policy evaluation semantics;

- policy catalog architecture;

- receipt construction;

- cryptographic hash algorithms or hash domains.

Those matters remain governed by the appropriate neighboring gates.

G-0807 determines the policy semantics and the policy state selected for execution.

G-0809 determines canonical serialization and cryptographic treatment.

G-0816 determines the representation of policy decisions in the decision summary.

G-0815 determines the **semantic identity of the governing policy state**.

# 2. Authority and Evidence Basis

This decision is governed by:

1. Ratified Zyppi constitutional authorities;

3. `CAW-007 — Runtime Contracts`;

Repository behavior shall not override constitutional authority.

# 3. Constitutional Scope

The Council establishes the following boundary:

**G-0815 defines what `policyVersion` means. It does not define how policy is selected, how policy is evaluated, or how the value is cryptographically encoded.**

This distinction is mandatory.

The policy chain is therefore:
`Normative Authority         ↓ G-0807 Policy Semantics         ↓ Policy State Admitted for Execution         ↓ G-0815 policyVersion         ↓ Policy Evaluation         ↓ G-0816 decisionSummary         ↓ Execution Receipt `
`policyVersion` describes the governing policy state. It does not become an additional policy authority.

# 4. Directional Evaluation

## 4.1 Direction A — Single Policy Catalog Version

**DECISION: REJECTED**

Direction A would identify the policy state using one global catalog version.

The Council finds this insufficient because a catalog may evolve without changing the policy state relevant to a particular execution.

A global catalog version may therefore describe a broader administrative state rather than the exact policy state governing the execution.

This weakens precise replay and audit semantics.

Direction A is therefore rejected.

## 4.2 Direction B — Composite Policy Version

**DECISION: SELECTED**

Direction B identifies the policy state governing an execution as a composite policy state representing the exact policy state admitted for that execution.

This provides the required semantic precision without requiring every individual policy decision to carry an independent receipt-level version.

Direction B therefore provides the appropriate M08 balance between:

- deterministic replay;

- auditability;

- semantic precision;

- bounded receipt complexity;

- separation of policy semantics from cryptographic construction.

**Direction B is RATIFIED.**

## 4.3 Direction C — Version Per Policy Decision

**DECISION: REJECTED FOR M08 BASELINE**

Per-decision versioning provides greater granularity but introduces additional receipt, canonicalization, hashing, and verification complexity.

The Council finds no current constitutional requirement requiring every individual policy decision to carry an independent receipt-level policy version.

Individual policy records may retain their own version information where required by the policy model.

That does not require `policyVersion` itself to become a collection of per-decision versions.

Direction C is therefore rejected as the M08 baseline.

This rejection does not prevent a future gate from introducing greater granularity if a future constitutional requirement establishes such a need.

# 5. Ratified Semantic Definition

The Council establishes:

**`policyVersion` identifies the policy state admitted to govern the constitutional execution.**

The value therefore describes **the policy state that governed the execution**, rather than merely the latest available policy catalog version or the subset of policies that happened to produce decisions.

The semantic authority for determining which policy state governs an execution remains the policy architecture established by G-0807.

# 6. Policy State Boundary

For purposes of `policyVersion`, the relevant policy state is the **policy state admitted for the execution**.

It is not defined as:

- only the policies that happened to produce decisions;

- only the policies actually reached before budget exhaustion;

- only the policies that returned an allow or deny result;

- an arbitrary operational subset selected after evaluation.

This distinction is necessary because policy identity must describe the governing constitutional state, not merely the observed traversal of that state.

Conversely:

**Evaluation outcome does not redefine policy identity.**

A policy state remains the same policy state even where deterministic evaluation results in different subsets being reached because of execution-control conditions.

Such execution behavior belongs to the appropriate execution and diagnostic semantics, not to the semantic definition of `policyVersion`.

# 7. Policy Authority

G-0815 does not establish a second policy authority.

The Council reaffirms the separation established by the preceding architecture:

- the authoritative constitutional policy universe and applicability semantics are determined by the policy architecture established under G-0807;

- `PolicyContext` supplies the explicit evaluation context and parameters;

- `policyVersion` identifies the governing policy state;

- the Runtime shall not independently discover or substitute policy state.

Accordingly:
`Authoritative Policy State           │           ▼      policyVersion           │           ▼    Policy Evaluation `
and not:
`PolicyContext ───────► policyVersion `
where such a relationship would incorrectly make `PolicyContext` a second policy authority.

# 8. Determinism and Replay

The Council establishes:

**The same explicit policy state under the same constitutional execution boundary shall produce the same `policyVersion`.**

A replay of an execution therefore preserves the same semantic policy version when the relevant policy state is identical.

`policyVersion` shall not depend on:

- wall-clock time;

- ambient randomness;

- process identity;

- machine identity;

- execution history;

- network state;

- filesystem state;

- external policy discovery during Runtime execution.

Policy state must already be explicit and attributable at the execution boundary.

# 9. No Ambient Policy Resolution

The Runtime shall not obtain or resolve a policy version implicitly during constitutional execution.

In particular, the Runtime shall not:

- query an external policy registry for the current version;

- use the latest catalog version available at execution time;

- resolve a policy version from wall-clock time;

- substitute a missing version from environmental state;

- silently select another version because the requested version is unavailable.

The execution boundary must contain sufficient explicit policy state for the Runtime to determine the governing version.

# 10. Missing Policy Version

Where the governing policy semantics require a policy version to be identifiable and that version cannot be established from the explicit admitted policy state:

**The Runtime shall fail closed.**

It shall not silently substitute:

- an unspecified version;

- the latest version;

- a default version;

- an ambiently discovered version.

The precise error contract and failure classification may be established by the applicable implementation mandate.

# 11. Semantic Order Independence

Where the governing policy state consists of multiple policy records, the semantic identity of that state shall not depend upon incidental ordering.

The following shall therefore not create different semantic policy versions merely by changing incidental ordering:
`Policy A Policy B Policy C `
and:
`Policy C Policy A Policy B `
provided the admitted policy state is otherwise identical.

This is a **semantic requirement only**.

Canonical ordering, serialization, encoding, and cryptographic construction are explicitly delegated to **G-0809**.

# 12. Derivability and Reproducibility

The Council establishes a bounded derivability requirement:

**`policyVersion` shall be deterministically reproducible from the same admitted policy state.**

This does not prescribe a particular representation or cryptographic construction.

G-0815 therefore does **not** mandate:

- a particular hash algorithm;

- a digest format;

- a canonical serialization format;

- a concatenation scheme;

- a cryptographic hash domain;

- a specific implementation function.

Those decisions belong to G-0809.

The constitutional requirement is only that identical admitted policy state must not produce arbitrarily different `policyVersion` values.

# 13. Separation from Cryptographic Identity

`policyVersion` is a **semantic policy-state identifier**.

It shall not be interpreted by this gate as:

- the policy definition hash;

- the execution hash;

- the input hash;

- the receipt hash;

- a substitute for ACV identity;

- a substitute for `executionId`;

- a cryptographic proof of policy correctness.

Where `policyVersion` participates in a cryptographic construction, that participation is determined by G-0809.

# 14. Separation from `executionId`

The Council reaffirms that:
`executionId  = identity of the constitutional execution policyVersion = identity of the governing policy state `
These values identify different semantic objects.

A change in `policyVersion` therefore represents a different governing policy state; it does not create a new `executionId` by itself.

Conversely, a different `executionId` does not imply a different `policyVersion`.

# 15. Separation from `receiptId`

The Council further reaffirms:
`executionId  → execution identity policyVersion → governing policy-state identity receiptId     → receipt identity `
These identities shall not be conflated.

The precise relationship between `policyVersion` and receipt hashing remains subject to G-0809 and the receipt semantics established by the applicable M08 gates.

# 16. Relationship to Individual Policy Versions

Individual policies may possess their own semantic versions.

Such individual versions may remain part of the underlying policy model and may be relevant to policy decisions and later decision-summary construction.

However:

**G-0815 does not require every individual policy version to become an independent receipt-level `policyVersion`.**

The composite policy-state identity is the M08 baseline.

If future constitutional requirements establish that individual policy versions must independently appear in receipts or verification artifacts, that requirement shall be introduced through a future authorized decision rather than inferred from G-0815.

# 17. Ratified Invariants

## E-0815-01 — Semantic Identity

`policyVersion` identifies the policy state governing the constitutional execution.

## E-0815-02 — Execution Relevance

`policyVersion` identifies the policy state admitted for the execution, not merely the latest available catalog state.

## E-0815-03 — Policy Authority

The policy state identified by `policyVersion` shall correspond to the authoritative policy state selected under the policy semantics established by G-0807.

`PolicyContext` shall not become a competing source of policy authority.

## E-0815-04 — Determinism

The same admitted policy state under the same constitutional execution boundary shall produce the same `policyVersion`.

## E-0815-05 — No Ambient Resolution

The Runtime shall not obtain policy-version information from ambient or external state during execution.

## E-0815-06 — Fail Closed

Where policy-version identification is constitutionally required but cannot be established from the explicit admitted policy state, execution shall fail closed.

## E-0815-07 — Hash Separation

G-0815 defines the semantic meaning of `policyVersion`.

G-0809 determines canonical serialization and cryptographic participation.

## E-0815-08 — Bounded Representation

`policyVersion` shall identify the governing policy state without requiring the complete policy definitions or decision history to be embedded in the receipt.

## E-0815-09 — Reproducibility

`policyVersion` shall be deterministically reproducible from the same admitted policy state.

## E-0815-10 — Semantic Order Independence

The semantic identity of a composite policy state shall not depend upon incidental ordering of its constituent policy records.

# 18. Explicitly Rejected Interpretations

The Council rejects the following interpretations:

### R1 — Global Catalog Identity

`policyVersion` shall not merely identify the latest or current global policy catalog version.

### R2 — Decision-Subset Identity

`policyVersion` shall not be defined as the subset of policies that happened to produce decisions.

### R3 — Per-Decision Baseline

M08 shall not require a separate policy version field for every policy decision.

### R4 — PolicyContext as Policy Authority

`PolicyContext` shall not become a second authoritative policy source.

### R5 — Ambient Version Discovery

The Runtime shall not retrieve policy versions implicitly.

### R6 — Cryptographic Overreach

G-0815 shall not define hash algorithms, hash domains, or canonical cryptographic encoding.

### R7 — PolicyVersion as Execution Identity

`policyVersion` shall not be treated as an alternative to `executionId`.

# 20. Final Architectural Direction

The Council therefore establishes the following normative direction:
`Authoritative Policy State           │           ▼    Composite Policy State           │           ▼     policyVersion           │           ▼    Policy Evaluation           │           ▼     decisionSummary           │           ▼        Receipt `
The composite policy state is the semantic object.

`policyVersion` identifies that state.

The cryptographic representation of that identity is outside this gate.

# 21. Decision

The Council formally ratifies:

**Direction B — Composite Policy Version.**

`policyVersion` shall identify the **policy state admitted to govern the constitutional execution**.

The M08 baseline shall not require per-decision policy versioning.

The Runtime shall operate only on explicit policy state and shall not resolve policy versions from ambient or external state.

Identical admitted policy state shall produce an identical semantic `policyVersion`.

Cryptographic representation and hash participation remain delegated to G-0809.

# 23. Constitutional Principle Established

The Council records the following principle:

**A policy version identifies the policy state that governed an execution; it does not define that policy state, replace its authority, or encode its cryptographic proof.**

And:

**Policy identity shall describe the governing constitutional policy state, not the incidental path by which policy evaluation proceeded.**

These principles govern subsequent M08 work involving policy-version representation, replay, receipts, and verification.
