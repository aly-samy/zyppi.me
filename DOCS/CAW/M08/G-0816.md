# 1. Question Presented

The governing question for G-0816 is:

**What information shall the canonical execution receipt retain as the bounded semantic summary of the policy execution result?**

The Council is not deciding:

- policy selection;

- policy evaluation semantics;

- policy-version semantics;

- diagnostic or operational trace semantics;

- cryptographic digest construction;

- receipt persistence.

Those matters belong to the applicable neighboring gates.

G-0816 determines the **semantic meaning and bounded representation of `decisionSummary`**.

# 2. Authority and Evidence Basis

This decision is governed by, and shall not weaken:

1. Ratified Zyppi constitutional authorities;

2. applicable CEngS requirements;

3. `RI-006`;

4. `POL-001`;

5. `SEC-001`;

6. `CAW-007 — Runtime Contracts`;

7. `CAW-011 — Commerce Atlas Wedge Build Order`;

8. M08 preparation and gate records;

9. ratified predecessor gate decisions, particularly G-0807 and G-0815.

The M08-GDR identifies three candidate directions for `decisionSummary`:

- **Direction A — Final Outcome Only**

- **Direction B — Canonical Decision Summary**

- **Direction C — Decision Digest + Summary**.

The same governing register establishes that cryptographic construction is downstream under G-0809 and that diagnostics are separately governed by G-0817.

# 3. Constitutional Finding

The Council finds that **final outcome alone is insufficient** as the constitutional representation of policy execution.

A receipt containing only:

`ALLOW`

or:

`DENY`

does not provide sufficient bounded attribution of the policy result.

At the same time, retaining the complete policy-evaluation trace would improperly expand the receipt into operational evidence and create an unbounded evidence-growth problem.

The Council therefore establishes:

**`decisionSummary` is a bounded, deterministic semantic representation of the policy execution result.**

It is neither a complete policy trace nor a cryptographic commitment to such a trace.

# 4. Selected Architectural Direction

## D-0816-01 — Canonical Decision Summary

**DECISION: SELECTED AND RATIFIED**

M08 shall use **Direction B — Canonical Decision Summary**.

`decisionSummary` shall represent:

1. the resulting policy outcome; and

2. sufficient bounded policy-result information to make that outcome semantically attributable.

The representation shall be:

- deterministic;

- bounded;

- semantically meaningful;

- independently interpretable;

- compatible with subsequent canonical serialization and hashing.

The Council does **not** prescribe a concrete TypeScript interface in this gate.

# 5. Rejected Direction A — Final Outcome Only

## D-0816-02

**DECISION: REJECTED**

The Council rejects a receipt representation containing only the final policy outcome.

### Reason

A naked outcome does not provide sufficient bounded evidence of what policy execution produced that outcome.

For example, a `DENY` result without bounded attribution cannot distinguish the policy-result circumstances sufficiently for independent verification.

The Council therefore requires more than a bare outcome while explicitly rejecting unrestricted policy traces.

# 6. Rejected Direction C — Decision Digest + Summary

## D-0816-03

**DECISION: REJECTED FOR THIS GATE**

The Council does not establish a decision digest as part of `decisionSummary`.

Cryptographic digest construction belongs to **G-0809 — Hash Domains**.

G-0816 therefore defines the semantic material that may later participate in a cryptographic domain; it does not define:

- hash algorithms;

- digest algorithms;

- hash-domain labels;

- hash inclusion rules;

- canonical hash construction.

This preserves the separation between semantic receipt design and cryptographic construction.

# 7. Relationship to Policy Evaluation

## D-0816-04 — Derivation From Policy Evaluation

`decisionSummary` shall be derived from the **policy execution result established by the policy evaluation layer**.

The summary shall not independently re-evaluate policy.

Conceptually:
`Policy evaluation        │        ▼ Policy execution result        │        ▼ Bounded deterministic summarization        │        ▼ decisionSummary `
The exact policy evaluation algorithm remains outside G-0816.

G-0816 therefore does not redefine the semantics established by G-0807.

# 8. Outcome Representation

## D-0816-05 — Semantic Outcome

The canonical summary shall preserve the semantic distinction between an actual policy outcome and a state in which no policy outcome was produced.

A valid policy result shall not be silently converted into an unrelated status merely to simplify receipt representation.

Likewise, absence of a policy decision shall not be represented as an ordinary `ALLOW` or `DENY`.

The exact enumeration and type names are implementation-contract matters and shall not be inferred from this decision record.

# 9. Bounded Policy Attribution

## D-0816-06 — Bounded Attribution

Where policy attribution is constitutionally required, `decisionSummary` may retain bounded policy-result attributes such as policy identity or equivalent attribution.

The Council deliberately does **not** require the summary to contain the complete `PolicyDecision[]`.

The summary shall not become an unrestricted collection of individual policy decisions.

The representation must remain bounded regardless of the quantity of underlying evaluation information.

No arbitrary numerical threshold is established by G-0816.

Any concrete size limits required for implementation shall be established by the appropriate implementation governance without changing the semantic rule established here.

# 10. Policy Version Separation

## D-0816-07 — No Policy-Version Duplication

`decisionSummary` shall **not become a second representation of policy-version state**.

Policy-version semantics are governed by **G-0815**.

The canonical relationship is:
`Policy state      │      ├──► policyVersion      │      └──► policy execution               │               ▼        decisionSummary `
A policy version shall therefore not be redundantly embedded into `decisionSummary` merely to improve convenience.

This preserves a single semantic owner for policy-version information.

# 11. Diagnostics Separation

## D-0816-08 — Diagnostics Excluded

Detailed diagnostics do not belong in `decisionSummary`.

The following remain outside G-0816:

- operational traces;

- debugging information;

- stack traces;

- infrastructure observations;

- detailed evaluation telemetry;

- unrestricted reason material;

- diagnostic trace data.

These matters belong to **G-0817 — Diagnostics**.

A bounded semantic outcome may indicate that an evaluation did not produce a normal policy result where constitutionally necessary, but detailed explanation of why belongs to the appropriate diagnostic mechanism.

# 12. Structural Runtime Failure

## D-0816-09 — Policy Result Is Not Runtime Error

A structural or constitutional Runtime failure shall not automatically be represented as though it were an ordinary policy decision.

The Council distinguishes:
`Policy evaluation result         ≠ Runtime structural failure `
A failure preventing policy evaluation is not retroactively converted into `ALLOW`, `DENY`, or another policy result.

The precise Runtime error taxonomy remains outside G-0816 unless established by another governing contract.

This prevents `decisionSummary` from becoming an implicit error-code registry.

# 13. Determinism

## D-0816-10 — Deterministic Summary

For identical explicit execution inputs, identical authorized constitutional context, identical policy state, and identical policy execution result:

**the resulting `decisionSummary` shall be identical.**

The summary shall not depend upon:

- evaluation timing;

- machine identity;

- process identity;

- thread scheduling;

- network state;

- filesystem state;

- locale;

- ambient randomness;

- unspecified evaluation order.

This follows the M08 constitutional deterministic-execution principle, under which identical explicit inputs and authorized constitutional context must produce equivalent Runtime output.

# 14. Deterministic Ordering

## D-0816-11 — Ordering

Where the summary contains multiple attributable policy-result elements, their semantic ordering shall be deterministic.

The Council does not prescribe an implementation-specific sorting algorithm in this record.

The serialization and canonical ordering rules required for cryptographic participation remain delegated to **G-0809**.

No evaluation-order accident shall alter the semantic identity of the summary.

# 15. Boundedness

## D-0816-12 — Bounded Evidence

`decisionSummary` shall have bounded representation.

The size of the constitutional summary shall not grow without constitutional bound merely because the underlying policy evaluation produces additional operational detail.

Therefore:

**Detailed policy evaluation information belongs outside the canonical decision summary unless a bounded semantic projection of that information is constitutionally required.**

This preserves the M08 distinction between constitutional evidence and operational telemetry.

# 16. No Cryptographic Semantics

## D-0816-13

G-0816 does not determine whether `decisionSummary`:

- enters `outputHash`;

- enters `deterministicHash`;

- receives a dedicated digest;

- participates in a Merkle structure;

- receives a dedicated hash domain.

All such decisions remain delegated to **G-0809**.

G-0816 determines only the semantic content to which those later mechanisms may apply.

# 17. Relationship to G-0815

G-0815 establishes the semantic identity of the policy state governing the execution.

G-0816 establishes the semantic result produced by executing that policy state.

Accordingly:
`ACV / admitted policy universe             │             ▼       policyVersion             │             ▼      Policy evaluation             │             ▼      decisionSummary `
The two fields therefore have distinct constitutional meanings and shall not be collapsed.

# 18. Relationship to G-0817

The Council establishes the following boundary:

**G-0816 answers "What policy result did the Runtime produce?"**

**G-0817 answers "What bounded diagnostic information is necessary to explain the Runtime's execution condition?"**

G-0816 shall therefore not absorb G-0817 merely because certain diagnostic information might be useful to an operator.

Operational usefulness is not sufficient constitutional justification for expanding the decision summary.

# 19. Relationship to CAW-008 Persistence

G-0816 establishes the **domain semantic representation** of `decisionSummary`.

It does not authorize a change to the persistence schema.

Any persistence representation, including the existing CAW-008 `decision_summary` representation, must preserve the semantic meaning established here.

Whether persistence performs transformation, canonicalization, or storage-specific encoding is outside G-0816 and remains subject to the applicable persistence and serialization authorities.

# 20. What G-0816 Does Not Decide

G-0816 does not decide:

1. policy-selection semantics;

2. policy-evaluation algorithms;

3. policy-version semantics;

4. execution-budget semantics;

5. Runtime diagnostic taxonomy;

6. operational tracing;

7. cryptographic hashing;

No implementation agent may infer those decisions from this record.

# 21. Required Contract Direction

The semantic contract is therefore:
`decisionSummary     =     bounded deterministic semantic representation     of the policy execution result     with sufficient bounded attribution     to preserve constitutional auditability. `
It is **not**:
`decisionSummary = full PolicyDecision[] `
and it is not:
`decisionSummary = cryptographic digest `
and it is not merely:
`decisionSummary = ALLOW | DENY `

Rejected because it risks unbounded evidence growth.

### R2 — Arbitrary Policy Count Threshold

Rejected because no such threshold is established by G-0816 itself.

### R3 — Embedded Policy Versions

Rejected because policy-version semantics belong to G-0815.

### R4 — Embedded Operational Diagnostics

Rejected because diagnostics belong to G-0817.

### R5 — Embedded Cryptographic Digest

Rejected because cryptographic domain construction belongs to G-0809.

### R6 — Runtime Error Registry

Rejected because G-0816 must not become a general-purpose Runtime error taxonomy.

### R7 — Bare Final Outcome

Rejected because it provides insufficient bounded semantic attribution.

# 25. Closure Condition

The Council finds that the constitutional question presented by G-0816 has been resolved.

The selected direction is:

**Direction B — Canonical Decision Summary.**

The required semantic properties have been established:

- bounded;

- deterministic;

- attributable;

- policy-result focused;

- distinct from diagnostics;

- distinct from policy-version semantics;

# 26. Constitutional Principle Established

The Council records:

**The canonical decision summary shall preserve the semantic policy result and sufficient bounded attribution for independent interpretation, without becoming an unrestricted policy trace, diagnostic record, or cryptographic construction.**

And:

**Constitutional evidence shall retain what is necessary to establish what the Runtime decided, while operational detail remains outside the decision summary unless separately authorized.**
