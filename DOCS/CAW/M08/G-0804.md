# ZYPPI CONSTITUTIONAL COUNCIL

## G-0804 — Council Decision Record

**Gate:** G-0804 — Final Receipt Field Set **Program:** CAW-011 — Commerce Atlas Wedge **Milestone:** M08 — Runtime Verification Pipeline **Decision Status:** **RATIFIED** **Selected Direction:** **Direction C — Preserve Contract but Classify Fields** **Decision Authority:** Zyppi Constitutional Council **Implementation Authority:** Consequence of ratification only; no independent semantic invention authorized **Date:** August 9, 2026

## 1. Constitutional Question

G-0804 determines:

**Which fields are constitutionally required in `ExecutionReceipt`, and which are merely operational?**

The active CAW-007 contract defines `ExecutionReceipt` as an immutable per-execution artifact containing ten fields:

`receiptId · executionId · runtimeVersion · inputHash · outputHash · evidenceHash · policyVersion · decisionSummary · executionTime · deterministicHash`

CAW-007 simultaneously defines `ExecutionReceipt` as one sibling component of the broader `ExecutionOutput`, alongside `Outcome`, `evidenceReferences`, `TrustResult`, `PolicyDecision[]`, and `Diagnostics`.

M08-GDR presents three directions for this gate: preserve the ten-field contract, split constitutional and operational receipts, or preserve the contract while explicitly classifying its fields.

# 2. Council Decision

The Council **RATIFIES Direction C — Preserve Contract but Classify Fields.**

The existing ten-field `ExecutionReceipt` surface SHALL be retained.

G-0804 is hereby established as a **field membership and classification decision**. It does not independently redefine the semantic construction of fields whose dedicated downstream gates have jurisdiction over them.

The Council therefore rejects both:

- **Direction A — Keep the Existing Ten-Field Contract without classification**, because it leaves material semantic ambiguity unresolved.

- **Direction B — Separate Constitutional and Operational Receipts**, because it would alter the active CAW-007 contract and prematurely introduce a second receipt structure.

Direction C provides the required semantic clarification while preserving contract continuity and respecting the M08 gate dependency structure.

# 4. Structural Receipt Boundary

The Council further ratifies the following structural principle:

**`ExecutionReceipt` remains one contract surface, but its fields do not thereby acquire identical semantic authority or identical cryptographic treatment.**

The receipt SHALL be understood as a bounded execution artifact within `ExecutionOutput`, not as a replacement for `ExecutionOutput`.

The receipt SHALL NOT absorb:

- `TrustResult`;

- `PolicyDecision[]`;

- `Diagnostics`;

- `evidenceReferences`;

- `Outcome`.

These remain separate `ExecutionOutput` siblings under CAW-007.

This preserves the constitutional separation already established by the Runtime contract.

# 5. Cryptographic Boundary

G-0804 does **not** determine the exact hash preimage or hash-domain membership of the ten fields.

That authority belongs to **G-0809 — Hash Domains**, whose mandatory decisions include:

- algorithm;

- encoding;

- serializer;

- field inclusion;

- field exclusion;

- ordering;

- null/absent treatment;

- evidence digest relationship;

- circularity prevention.

However, G-0804 establishes one binding architectural constraint:

**`deterministicHash` SHALL NOT be interpreted as requiring the receipt to hash itself recursively.**

G-0809 SHALL therefore establish a non-circular canonicalization boundary between the material being hashed and the resulting cryptographic seal.

G-0804 does **not** prescribe whether that boundary is ultimately implemented as a payload/envelope construction, an excluded-field canonicalization rule, or another constitutionally valid mechanism. That remains G-0809's jurisdiction.

# 6. Downstream Gate Boundaries

This decision expressly preserves the following jurisdictional boundaries:

### G-0803 — `receiptId`

Determines the semantic identity and construction of `receiptId`.

### G-0814 — `executionId`

Determines execution identity.

### G-0815 — `policyVersion`

Determines how the evaluated policy baseline is represented.

### G-0816 — `decisionSummary`

Determines the semantic and deterministic construction of the decision summary.

### G-0817 — Diagnostics

Determines diagnostics semantics. Diagnostics remain a separate `ExecutionOutput` sibling and are not absorbed into `ExecutionReceipt`.

### G-0801 / G-0802 — `executionTime`

Determine the constitutional temporal model and the resulting meaning of `executionTime`.

### G-0809 — Hash Domains

Determines all cryptographic construction rules, including the exact canonical preimages and anti-circularity mechanism.

G-0804 therefore establishes **membership**, while these gates establish the detailed semantics of the corresponding members.

# 7. Rejected Alternatives

## Direction A — Keep Existing Contract Without Classification

**Rejected.**

Although it preserves compatibility with CAW-007, it does not adequately distinguish:

- identity from execution identity;

- derived hashes from source semantics;

- temporal information from cryptographic material;

- decision summaries from policy decisions;

- receipt membership from hash-domain membership.

The Council therefore considers unclassified retention insufficient.

## Direction B — Separate Constitutional and Operational Receipts

**Rejected.**

This would introduce a new receipt structure and modify the active CAW-007 surface.

The M08 corpus explicitly identifies this direction as requiring contract changes and potentially conflicting with CAW-007.

No such contract split is authorized by G-0804.

# 8. Constitutional Rationale

The Council adopts Direction C because it achieves the required balance between **contract stability and semantic precision**.

The active contract already establishes the ten-field receipt surface.

At the same time, the M08 decision framework explicitly identifies unresolved semantics around receipt identity, execution time, hashes, policy version, decision summary, and diagnostics.

G-0804 therefore SHALL NOT solve those questions by inventing replacement structures.

Instead:

**Contract membership is fixed here. Field semantics remain with their governing gates. Cryptographic binding remains with G-0809.**

This prevents G-0804 from becoming an accidental omnibus receipt-design gate.

# 9. Implementation Consequence

The ratification permits implementation planning to treat the **ten-field `ExecutionReceipt` surface as authoritative**.

It does **not** authorize an implementation agent to infer unresolved field semantics.

In particular, implementation SHALL NOT:

- remove `decisionSummary`;

- replace `policyVersion` with an `acvHash`;

- replace `runtimeVersion` with another identifier;

- invent a second receipt type;

- determine hash-domain membership independently;

- include `deterministicHash` recursively;

- embed `TrustResult`, `Diagnostics`, `PolicyDecision[]`, or `evidenceReferences` into the receipt;

- invent temporal semantics for `executionTime`.

Until the relevant governing gates are ratified, those questions remain unresolved.

This follows the M08 gate-closure rule: a gate becomes implementation-eligible only after selection, rationale, dependency verification, and ratification.

# 10. Required Downstream Actions

G-0804 creates the following explicit dependency consequences:

1. **G-0803** SHALL define `receiptId`.

2. **G-0814** SHALL define `executionId`.

3. **G-0815** SHALL define `policyVersion`.

4. **G-0816** SHALL define `decisionSummary`.

5. **G-0817** SHALL define `Diagnostics` independently of the receipt.

6. **G-0801 / G-0802** SHALL define the meaning of `executionTime`.

7. **G-0809** SHALL define the cryptographic domains and anti-circularity mechanism.

8. G-0804 itself SHALL NOT reopen the ten-field membership decision unless a higher-authority constitutional change modifies CAW-007.

# 11. Final Ratification

The Zyppi Constitutional Council hereby records:

**G-0804 is RATIFIED under Direction C — Preserve Contract but Classify Fields.**

The authoritative `ExecutionReceipt` surface remains:
`receiptId executionId runtimeVersion inputHash outputHash evidenceHash policyVersion decisionSummary executionTime deterministicHash `
The receipt remains a bounded component of `ExecutionOutput`.

No additional receipt object is created.

No existing field is evicted.

No downstream field semantics are preempted.

No hash-domain rule is invented at this gate.

The anti-circularity requirement is constitutionally preserved and delegated to G-0809 for exact construction.
