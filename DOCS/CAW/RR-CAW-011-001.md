# 1. Resolution

Following constitutional review of the implementation progress through M06, the Council hereby ratifies the **CAW-011 Reconciliation Review**.

The Council finds that the implementation has remained faithful to the constitutional architecture and that no evidence exists of architectural divergence requiring a redesign of the roadmap.

Accordingly, the implementation roadmap remains the governing implementation authority for Commerce Application Wedge development.

# 2. Constitutional Findings

The Council unanimously determines that:

-

The constitutional hierarchy remains preserved.

-

The Constitution continues to govern the architecture.

-

The architecture continues to govern CAW.

-

CAW continues to govern implementation.

-

Implementation has not introduced constitutional drift.

# 3. Ratified Classifications

The Council adopts the following classifications.

Classification

Meaning

No Change

Roadmap remains constitutionally correct.

Clarification

Improves wording without altering architectural intent, milestone scope, dependencies, or implementation order.

Refinement

Improves decomposition or architectural expression while preserving constitutional objectives.

Constitutional Change

Alters roadmap objectives or architecture and therefore requires independent constitutional ratification.

These classifications become the governing framework for all future CAW reconciliation reviews.

# 5. Ratified Architectural Clarifications

The following constitutional clarifications are hereby adopted.

## GS1

GS1 is affirmed as the first **Reference Commerce Application Wedge**.

It is not the objective of CAW.

It is the first constitutional witness demonstrating CAW.

## Constitutional Composition

The Council formally introduces the architectural concept of **Constitutional Composition**.

**Definition**

Constitutional Composition is the capability by which independently verified constitutional components cooperate to produce a higher-order constitutional capability while preserving their individual authorities, responsibilities, and constitutional boundaries.

This concept is recognized as an architectural principle and reaches its formal demonstration during M15.

## Boundary Preservation

Future milestone planning documents shall explicitly define:

- What the milestone SHALL perform.

- What the milestone SHALL NOT perform.

This becomes a standard planning requirement for all remaining milestones.

## Witness Principle

Commerce Application Wedges exist to demonstrate constitutional capability.

They do not redefine constitutional architecture.

# 6. Governance Decision

The Council determines that implementation experience has produced architectural clarity but has not justified altering the roadmap's constitutional direction.

Therefore:

- No milestone sequencing changes are ratified.

- No dependency changes are ratified.

- No milestone consolidation is ratified.

- No constitutional objectives are modified.

Only constitutional clarifications and limited refinements are adopted.

# 7. Authorization

The Chair is hereby authorized to prepare **CAW-011 Version 2.1** incorporating the ratified clarifications and refinements contained within this resolution.

Version 2.1 shall preserve all milestone objectives, dependency relationships, implementation sequencing, and constitutional governance established by Version 2.0.

# 8. Constitutional Effect

Upon ratification, this resolution becomes the authoritative interpretation of CAW-011 Version 2.0 until superseded by a later ratified reconciliation.

All future milestone preparation, planning, implementation, verification, and closure documents shall conform to this interpretation.
